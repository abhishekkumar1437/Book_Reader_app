The second month carries the heaviest B.Sc.-level content — exactly the chapters that gave the most PYQs: magnetism–EMI–AC (11 questions in TRE 1.0 + 2.0), electronics (8), modern physics (7), coordination compounds (8), organic functional groups (8), polymers–biomolecules (8), animal kingdom (6). By **Day 60 (22 December)** the whole syllabus will have been covered once. Even if BPSC fixes the exam for early January, you will have finished everything.

> **In simple words:** Same daily pattern as Phase 1. The chapters are harder, so spend the first 30 minutes of each science slot on the basics (NCERT Class 12 level) before the B.Sc. points. Review days are Days 35, 42, 49 and 56.

**Watch the calendar:** the exam date may be announced during this phase. If it is, open [How the Plan Works](/interview/bpsc-tre4-science/roadmap/how-the-plan-works) Section 6 the same day and pick your compression row.

**Every day:** 20 minutes of current affairs inside the GS slot.
