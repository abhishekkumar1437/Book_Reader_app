The first month builds the base. The science chapters here are the ones every later chapter depends on (units, mechanics, atomic structure, bonding, the cell, genetics), plus some of the highest-scoring PYQ chapters (heat–thermodynamics, redox–electrochemistry–kinetics, cell and biomolecules). You also read the whole exam guide in the first six days, in the Revision slot, so that you know the rules of the game before the first weekly test.

> **In simple words:** Each day = one science chapter (or half of a big one) + one GS/language topic + revision of older chapters. Day 7, 14, 21 and 28 are review days with a 40-question test. By Day 30 you will have finished 7 physics, 6 chemistry and 7 biology chapters.

**Before Day 1:** keep three subject notebooks, one GS notebook and one current-affairs notebook ready (see [Books, Notes and Revision](/interview/bpsc-tre4-science/exam-guide/books-notes-and-revision)). The online application closes on **26 October 2026** (as reported) — finish it on Day 1 or 2 if you have not already, and keep the PDF of the submitted form.

**Every day:** 20 minutes of current affairs inside the GS slot; write 5 lines in the current-affairs notebook.

## Checkpoint on Day 30

You are on track if:

1. Your four weekly tests (Days 7, 14, 21, 28) average **28/40 or more** net (with −1/3).
2. You can write, from memory, the units sheet (dioptre, henry = Ω·s, tesla, weber, A·m², pascal, joule, watt, decibel).
3. You can state the second-ionisation-energy order O > F > N > C and explain why.
4. You can explain why internal energy does not change in a cyclic process.
5. You can list cell organelles with one function each (ribosome → protein synthesis; mitochondria → ATP).
6. Your language sets are at **9/30 net or above** every time (target 20+).

> **Exam angle:** If you are behind by more than three science days, use the next review day's evening to catch up and read [How the Plan Works](/interview/bpsc-tre4-science/roadmap/how-the-plan-works) Section 7. Do not skip the revision slot to catch up.
