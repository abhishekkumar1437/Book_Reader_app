The last 30 days turn knowledge into marks. You will read every chapter a second time — this time only its *Quick revision*, *Traps and exceptions* and *Practice with answers* plus your own error log — and you will practise the full exam under negative marking twice a week. Mocks are on Days 63, 67, 70, 74, 77, 81, 84 and 88; Prelims-pattern GS mocks on Days 66, 73, 80 and 87.

> **In simple words:** Revise two or three chapters a day, take a full mock every three or four days, analyse every mock the same day, and keep the error log growing. No new books, no new topics.

### How to run a mock day

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| Exam shift time (check admit card; assume 10:00 am until known) | Full mock, 150 minutes, OMR sheet, E for skips, no erasing |
| After lunch (2 h) | Score with +1/−1/3 for each part; tag every wrong or guessed answer K/F/T/R/G |
| Evening (1.5 h) | Read the chapter sections behind the K and F errors; add the T errors to the *Traps* page |

> **Exam angle:** Watch the **accuracy** column more than the score. If accuracy in Part III is below 80%, you are attempting too many doubtful questions — follow the E rule strictly in the next mock. The target from [Exam Pattern and Marking](/interview/bpsc-tre4-science/exam-guide/exam-pattern-and-marking) is 65–72 attempts at 85–90% accuracy (about 55–62 net).

## After Day 90

- If the exam is **after** 21 January 2027, repeat the Phase 3 cycle from Day 61 with new mock papers, but spend the revision days on the chapters with the most error-log entries.
- In the **final 7 days before the exam** — whenever it is — follow the last-week routine in [Exam-Day Strategy](/interview/bpsc-tre4-science/exam-guide/exam-day-strategy).
- If the **Prelims** comes before the Mains, take the Prelims GS mocks of this phase earlier and follow the Prelims section of [How the Plan Works](/interview/bpsc-tre4-science/roadmap/how-the-plan-works).

## Final checklist

1. All 37 teaching chapters read once, and their *Quick revision* read at least twice.
2. All three question banks done: [Physics](/interview/bpsc-tre4-science/physics/physics-question-bank-and-revision), [Chemistry](/interview/bpsc-tre4-science/chemistry/chemistry-question-bank-and-revision), [Biology](/interview/bpsc-tre4-science/biology/biology-question-bank-and-revision).
3. At least 6 full Mains mocks taken **and analysed**.
4. Language net 9/30 or more in every mock.
5. Units sheet, formula sheet, orders/trends pages and error log ready for the last week.
6. Admit card, ID, photos, two pens ready; centre route checked.

> **Remember:** Confirm the exam-day rules (option E, negative marking, reporting time) from the admit card and the official notice on [bpsc.bihar.gov.in](https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in). If anything differs from this collection, the official document wins.
