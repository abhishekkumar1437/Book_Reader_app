Also a separate chapter on topics that appeared in the 2026 paper but are not named in the syllabus — biostatistics, toxicology, animal behaviour and wildlife conservation (including the protected areas and Ramsar sites of Uttar Pradesh). Every genetic ratio is worked out, actual 2026 questions are quoted throughout, and the last chapter is a question bank.
