## 3. Formulas and ratios at a glance

<div class="formula">

**Genetics:** monohybrid 3 : 1 / 1 : 2 : 1 · dihybrid 9 : 3 : 3 : 1 · test cross 1 : 1 and 1 : 1 : 1 : 1 · gametes 2ⁿ, genotypes 3ⁿ, phenotypes 2ⁿ · lethal 2 : 1 · complementary 9 : 7 (test cross 1 : 3) · recessive epistasis 9 : 3 : 4 (1 : 1 : 2) · dominant epistasis 12 : 3 : 1 (2 : 1 : 1) · duplicate 15 : 1 (3 : 1) · inhibitory 13 : 3 (3 : 1) · cumulative 9 : 6 : 1 (1 : 2 : 1) · polygenic 2n + 1 classes, extremes (1/4)ⁿ · multiple-allele genotypes n(n + 1)/2

**Mapping:** RF % = recombinants/total × 100 = cM · CoC = observed DCO / expected DCO · interference = 1 − CoC

**DNA:** length = bp × 0.34 nm · H-bonds = 2(A–T) + 3(G–C) · Meselson-Stahl hybrids after n generations = 2/2ⁿ · PCR copies = 2ⁿ · a 6-bp enzyme cuts every 4⁶ = 4096 bp

**Population:** p + q = 1 · p² + 2pq + q² = 1 · X-linked: male frequency = q, affected females = q²

**Statistics:** s² = Σ(x − x̄)²/(n − 1) · SE = s/√n · CV = s/x̄ × 100 · χ² = Σ(O − E)²/E, df = n − 1 (3.84 / 5.99 / 7.82) · binomial mean np, variance npq · Poisson mean = variance · ±1σ 68%, ±2σ 95%, ±3σ 99.7%

## 5. Answering strategy (+3 / −1 marking)

- In the new pattern: **120 questions (30 General Knowledge + 90 Biology), 2 hours, +3 for correct, −1 for wrong or multiple marking, 0 for skipped**.
- **Decide by expected value (EV):**
  - **Two options eliminated for certain** (choosing one of two) — EV = (3 − 1)/2 = **+1** → **definitely answer**.
  - **One option eliminated** (one of three) — EV = (3 − 2)/3 = **+0.33** → small gain; answer if you have time and a leaning.
  - **No option eliminated** (blind guess) — EV = (3 − 3)/4 = **0** → no gain, only risk; **skip**.
- **Options with absolute words** ("only", "always", "all", "under any circumstances", "completely", "without any") are usually wrong — this clue worked in almost every interpretation question of 2026.
- **Assertion-Reason:** in 2026 the order of options changed from question to question ("both true, R is the correct explanation" was sometimes (A), sometimes (B), sometimes (D)) — mark by reading the **statement**, not the letter.
- **Statement-combination:** check the most doubtful statement first; eliminating it usually cuts two options (the EV +1 situation).
- **Calculation questions** (HW, χ², ratios, DNA content) — do not spend more than 60–90 seconds; if the formula table above is memorised, these are the most reliable marks.
- Time split (suggested): General Knowledge ≈ 25 minutes, Biology ≈ 85 minutes, last 10 minutes for checking the OMR and marked questions.

## Traps and exceptions (selected from the whole book)

- Yellow **seed** is dominant, yellow **pod** is recessive.
- Crossing over in pachytene, chiasmata in diplotene.
- ³²P = DNA (inside), ³⁵S = protein (outside).
- Pol III is the main replicating enzyme; Pol I was discovered first.
- In trp, tryptophan is the co-repressor; in lac, allolactose is the inducer.
- In birds the female is ZW; in grasshoppers the male is XO.
- Klinefelter has 1 Barr body, Turner has 0.
- Homologous = divergent; analogous = convergent.
- Drift in small populations; gene flow makes populations similar.
- Lower LD₅₀ = more toxic; organophosphates are more toxic but less persistent.
- Kinesis is non-directional, taxis is directional.
- 4 schedules after WPA 2022; Dudhwa is the only national park in UP.
