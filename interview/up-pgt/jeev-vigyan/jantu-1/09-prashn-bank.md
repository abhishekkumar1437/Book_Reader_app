## 11. Answering strategy for +3/−1 marking

- Each correct answer **+3**, wrong or more than one mark **−1**, left blank **0**.
- **Expected value (EV) of a guess:**
  - **Can eliminate two options** (choosing 1 of 2): EV = (3 − 1)/2 = **+1** → **always answer**.
  - **Can eliminate one option** (1 of 3): EV = (3 − 2)/3 = **+0.33** → slight gain; answer if confident about the topic.
  - **Cannot eliminate any** (blind guess, 1 of 4): EV = (3 − 3)/4 = **0** → no gain, only risk — **leave it**.
- **Tips for eliminating options in this book's questions:**
  - **Matching questions** — start with the most certain pair (e.g. nauplius = 3 pairs of appendages, or complete double circulation = mammals); most options are cut immediately.
  - **Assertion–reason** — first check A and R separately for true/false; then read them with "because"; **read the option letters every time** (the order changes).
  - **"Best explanation"** — wrong options usually have a **wrong animal name** (pluteus/auricularia for starfish), a **reversed relationship** (right/left arch), or **absolute words** ("only", "without any reorganisation").
  - **Statement combination** — usually one statement is exaggerated ("rodents cause no damage", "superior in phylogeny because of simplicity").
- **Time:** 120 questions / 120 minutes — ~75–80 minutes for the 90 Biology questions; type-study and factual questions in 30–40 seconds, long case questions up to 1.5 minutes; certain questions in the first round, "two options eliminated" ones in the second round.
- **Target:** **at least 80% correct** in the ~15–20 questions from this book's area — because they are fact-based and give the highest return on preparation.
