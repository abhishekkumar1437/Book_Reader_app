title: Zoology I
subtitle: Animal diversity, non-chordate types, chordates and comparative anatomy
description: A book covering the first five points of Section B (Zoology) of the UP PGT Biology syllabus, from NCERT Class 11 up to B.Sc./M.Sc. level — taxonomy and the bases of classification, all phyla with characters and examples, the type studies named in the syllabus — Amoeba, Sycon, Hydra, Ascaris, cockroach, prawn (Palaemon), Pila and starfish; parasitic protozoa (Plasmodium, Entamoeba, Trypanosoma, Leishmania, Giardia), adaptations of helminth parasites, economic importance of insects; chordate classification, aquatic adaptations of fishes and flight adaptations of birds, origin of chordates, phylogeny of Prototheria–Metatheria–Eutheria; comparative anatomy of frog, Uromastyx, pigeon and rabbit, and animal histology. Questions from the actual 2026 paper are given as "PYQ" and the last chapter is a question bank.
