Months of preparation turn into marks in two hours. This chapter has a concrete plan for the **last seven days** (9–15 December 2026) and the **120 minutes in the exam hall**, so that on exam day you do not have to take any decision for the first time.

> **In simple words:** **Nothing new** in the final week — only tables, the error log and starred questions. In the exam: GK first (~20 minutes), then Biology in two rounds, OMR page by page, and guess only after eliminating two options.

> **Common mistake:** Taking a new mock in the last two days, seeing low marks and losing confidence. No full mock after Day 42 — only revision.

### What not to do in the final week

- A new book, new coaching notes, a new YouTube "marathon".
- Groups discussing "what will the cut-off be" — they cost both time and morale.
- Studying till 1–2 am — the speed of your brain at exam time depends on sleep.

## 4. The 120 minutes in the exam hall

<div class="formula">

| Minutes | Stage | Target |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Seal of the question booklet, page count, **series**; roll number, series, signature on the OMR | Zero errors |
| 3–23 | **General Knowledge (30)** | 20–24 attempted, ≥75% accurate |
| 23–83 | **Biology round 1** — sure questions (P) at once; in long questions, if two options are cut in 15 seconds, answer; otherwise "?" | ~60 attempted |
| 83–108 | **Round 2** — "?" questions; two go → answer; one goes and you are truly sure → answer; leave the rest | +10–15 attempted |
| 108–117 | OMR check — verify the serial number at every 10th question | |
| 117–120 | Buffer | No new guesses |

</div>

> **Exam angle:** Whether GK or Biology is printed first in the paper — **do GK first**, because it is fast and gives early confidence. But if the first 5 GK questions feel hard, do not get stuck there — move to Biology at the 20-minute limit.

### OMR precautions

1. **After every page**, fill that page's answers on the OMR — do not leave it for the end.
2. Match the question number with the OMR row every 10 questions — one row slipping can make 20 answers wrong.
3. Fill the circle fully and darkly; no ✓ or ×.
4. **More than one circle = −1** — no option to change (whitener is usually banned).
5. Leave the row of skipped questions blank — put the "fill later" mark on the question booklet, not on the OMR.

### Rules for difficult moments

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| 5 questions in a row not known | Don't panic — even in 2026 ~9% of questions were M.Sc.-level; move on |
| Letter confusion in A–R | Write the situation ("both true, explanation"), then read the options and find the letter |
| Hindi translation unclear | Read the English version |
| You think the question has an error | Choose the best option from sure knowledge; note the question number (for an objection) |
| 20 questions left at 100 minutes | One-liners first; leave the long ones |
| Fear of "attempted too few" | 75 questions at 85% accuracy = 64 right → 4×64 − 75 = **181**; even 75 questions can give a good score |

## 5. When to answer — the final rule (repeat)

| Options left | Expected marks | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | +3 | Answer |
| 2 | +1 | **Answer** |
| 3 | +0.33 | Only if the eliminated option is surely wrong |
| 4 | 0 | Leave |

## 6. After the exam

| Step | When |
|---|---|
| Copy the answers marked on your question booklet onto a sheet of paper (if you are allowed to take the booklet) | Same day |
| Provisional answer key — the commission's website | Date announced by the commission |
| Objection — for a faulty question, with proof (textbook page) | Within the prescribed period |
| Your estimated score: 4S − H | After the key |
| Interview preparation (40 marks) — teaching methods, class 11–12 Biology, laboratory, Uttar Pradesh's education system | While waiting for the result |

> **In simple words:** On the day after the exam, rest for 2–3 hours, then check your answers. If your Biology exam is on the 15th and you have no other subject on the 16th, you can start light interview preparation from the 16th.

## Traps and exceptions

- Confirm the exam date (15 or 16) **from the admit card** — not by guesswork.
- Forgetting to fill the question-booklet **series** on the OMR — the evaluation of the whole answer sheet can be affected.
- Blind guesses in the last 5 minutes — average 0, but risk of OMR errors.
- New sources in the final week — they increase confusion.
- Studying late on the night before the exam — both reading speed and accuracy drop the next day.

## Quick revision

1. Final week — nothing new.
2. No full mock after Day 42.
3. Admit card about 3 days before; 2 prints + original photo ID.
4. Visit the centre a day before; arrive 45 minutes before reporting.
5. 0–3 minutes — seal, series, OMR details.
6. GK 20 minutes.
7. Biology — round 1 (60 minutes) + round 2 (25 minutes).
8. OMR page by page; check every 10 questions.
9. Multiple circles = −1.
10. Two options left → answer.
11. Unclear Hindi → English version.
12. Faulty question — note the number, file an objection.
13. Sleep at 10 pm.
14. After the exam — answer key, objections, interview (40).
15. 75 questions, 85% accuracy = 181 marks.

## Practice questions (with answers)

1. Most important task in the first 3 minutes in the exam hall? — Filling the **series and roll number on the OMR** correctly.
2. How much time for GK? — **About 20 minutes**.
3. When to fill the OMR? — **After finishing each page**.
4. 80 attempted, 68 right — marks? — **192** (272 − 80).
5. Until when are mocks taken in the final week? — **Before Day 42 (13 December)**; after that only revision.
6. What to check on the admit card? — Name, photo, **subject (Biology, code 06)**, date, shift, centre.
7. How to avoid letter confusion in A–R? — First write the **situation**, then the letter.
8. 3 options left and the eliminated option is doubtful — what to do? — **Leave it**.
9. After the exam, for a faulty question? — An **objection** within the prescribed period, with proof.
10. What to read on the night before the exam? — Only the **one-page list of 20 facts**.
11. 20 questions left at 100 minutes — priority? — **One-line questions** first.
12. About when does the exam-district notice come? — **10 days before** the exam.
13. Interview marks? — **40**.
14. Marks for filling multiple circles? — **−1**.
15. If Biology turns out to be on 16 December? — Shift the plan one day forward; **15 December light revision**.
