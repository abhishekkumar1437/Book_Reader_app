This chapter tells you **which exam** you are preparing for, **which post** it is, **how many posts** there are and **what happens when**. Read this 20-minute chapter once before you start studying — every later plan is built on it.

> **In simple words:** UP PGT = the post of **Lecturer** who teaches classes 11–12 in non-government aided inter colleges of Uttar Pradesh. Recruitment is done by the **UP Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC), Prayagraj**. There are **130 posts** in Biology, the written exam is on **15–16 December 2026**, and selection will be on **400 marks (360 written + 40 interview)**.

## 1. UPESSC — the recruiting body

| Point | Details |
|---|---|
| Full name | Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) |
| Headquarters | Prayagraj |
| Who did it earlier | At the secondary level, the UP Secondary Education Service Selection Board (UPSESSB) — TGT/PGT recruitment |
| Now | Recruitment for secondary (TGT/PGT), higher education (aided degree colleges) etc. under a single commission |
| Advertisement for this exam | **Advertisement No. 06/2026**, dated **15 September 2026** — Lecturer (PGT) |

> **Remember:** The PGT exam of the old board's (UPSESSB) advertisement **02/2022** was finally held on **9–10 May 2026** (Biology on **9 May 2026**). That question paper is analysed in [Chapter 4](/interview/up-pgt/jeev-vigyan/pariksha-margdarshika/pichhla-prashnapatra-2026-vishleshan) of this book. Your exam (Advt. 06/2026) will be on the **new pattern**.

### Government (GIC) vs aided — a common confusion

| Type of school | Who recruits the Lecturer |
|---|---|
| Non-government aided inter college — run by a management committee, salary paid by the state government | **UPESSC** (this exam) |
| Government Inter College (GIC) — directly under the government | Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) — a separate exam |

> **Common mistake:** Treating "UP PGT" and "UPPSC GIC Lecturer" as the same. Their syllabi are similar, but the exam, pattern and commission are different. This collection is for **UPESSC**.

## 3. Eligibility (Biology Lecturer)

| Condition | Details |
|---|---|
| Educational qualification | **Postgraduate (PG) in Botany or Zoology** from a recognised university |
| Training qualification | **B.Ed.** (from an NCTE-recognised institution) |
| Age | **21–62 years** as on 1 July 2026 (reported — verify with the official notification) |
| Pay scale | **₹47,600 – 1,51,100** (pay level; reported — verify with the official notification) |

> **In simple words:** M.Sc. Botany or M.Sc. Zoology — either one, plus B.Ed. This means questions come from both sides: **a Botany graduate must study Zoology, and a Zoology graduate must study Botany**, up to B.Sc. level. In the 2026 paper the two shares were roughly **1 : 1**.

## 4. Selection process — step by step

1. **Written exam** (360 marks): 120 multiple-choice questions — 30 General Knowledge + 90 Biology, 2 hours, +3/−1.
2. **Interview** (40 marks): for candidates called on the basis of written-exam marks. (How many times the number of posts will be called — verify with the official notification.)
3. **Final merit** = written + interview (out of 400).
4. **In case of a tie**, the order is: first, higher written-exam marks → then higher interview marks → then postgraduate (PG) percentage → then training (B.Ed.) percentage → finally, the older candidate ranks higher.
5. School allotment/counselling — as per the commission's procedure.

> **Exam angle:** 360 of the 400 marks are from the written exam — i.e. **90% of the decision is made in the written exam**. Prepare for the interview after the written exam; for now, put every hour into the written exam.

## 5. UPTET and the Lecturer post — is TET needed?

| Exam | For whom |
|---|---|
| UPTET (Uttar Pradesh Teacher Eligibility Test) | Eligibility for teachers of classes **1–5** (primary) and **6–8** (upper primary) |
| CTET | Classes 1–8 in Kendriya Vidyalayas etc. |
| UP PGT (this exam) | Lecturer for classes **11–12** — eligibility = PG + B.Ed. |

> **Remember:** UPTET gives eligibility only for classes 1–8. **Advt. 06/2026 has no TET condition for the Lecturer (PGT) post** — your eligibility comes from PG (Botany/Zoology) + B.Ed. So do not spend time on UPTET in the middle of PGT preparation. (Teacher-eligibility rules change from time to time; for any new government order, verify with the official notification.)

## Traps and exceptions

- "UP PGT" ≠ "UPPSC GIC Lecturer" — different commission and pattern.
- The 2026 (May) paper belonged to the **old** Advt. 02/2022 — it had 125 questions and no General Knowledge. Your paper will have **120 questions (30 + 90)**.
- Biology posts are split between **boys' (117) + girls' (13)** schools — the option you filled in the application affects allotment later; check your application PDF.
- UPTET is not a condition for the Lecturer post.
- Verify "reported" facts such as the age limit and pay scale with the official notification.
