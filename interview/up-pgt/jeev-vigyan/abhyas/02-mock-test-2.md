This second full mock test follows the UPESSC **new pattern**: 30 General Knowledge questions and 90 Biology questions, 120 questions in all, 2 hours. Sub-topics different from Mock Test 1 have been chosen — Bryophyta, gymnosperms and palaeobotany, families, modifications, embryo sac, plant growth regulators, fungi and lichens, biofertilisers, Sycon–Palaemon–Pila–starfish, histology, excretion, operon, gene interaction, placenta, t-test and the Wildlife Act.

**How to attempt:**

1. Set a 2-hour timer and do not open answers in between.
2. Keep writing your answers on a sheet of paper (1–120). If you cannot eliminate even two options in a question, skip it.
3. Only after time is up, open "Show answer" and check.
4. Marks: **correct = +3, wrong = −1, skipped = 0**. Maximum 360.
5. Target: 200+ on the first attempt, 240+ by the second or third mock.

> **Exam angle:** After eliminating two options, the expected gain from guessing is (3 − 1)/2 = +1; after eliminating only one, +0.33; a completely blind guess, 0. So do not guess blindly.
