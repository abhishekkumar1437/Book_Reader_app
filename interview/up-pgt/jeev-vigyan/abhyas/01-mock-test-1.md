This mock test follows the **new UPESSC pattern**: a total of **120 questions** — **30 General Knowledge** in Part A and **90 Biology** in Part B, four options per question, **+3 for a correct answer and −1 for a wrong one (or for marking more than one option)**, and 0 for an unattempted question. The level and style of the questions match the actual paper of 9 May 2026 — long, balanced options, statement combinations, matching, Assertion–Reason and sequence questions.

**How to attempt it:**

1. Set a **2-hour timer** and solve the whole paper in one sitting — do not open answers in between.
2. Write your answers on paper (or in an OMR-like table) against the question numbers.
3. When time is up, click **"Show answer"** under each question to check and read the explanation.
4. Use the **Scoring** section at the end to work out your score (3 × correct − 1 × wrong) and identify weak topics.

> **Exam angle:** Because of the −1 marking, do not guess blindly. If you can eliminate two options, the average gain from guessing is +1; if you can eliminate only one, about +0.33; if you have no idea at all, 0. Make **240+/360** your first target.

**Q16.** <span class="qa-tag">GK · Physics</span>

Two statements are given below:
