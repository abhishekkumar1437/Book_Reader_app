Today is **1 November 2026 (Sunday)** and the written exam is on **15–16 December 2026**. This plan runs from **Day 1 = Monday 2 November** to **Day 44 = Tuesday 15 December** (exam) — i.e. **43 study days**. Each day's table links directly to the chapter to be read that day.

> **In simple words:** Days 1–15 = the heaviest part (cryptogams, gymnosperms, microbes, economic botany, animal types, chordates). Days 16–30 = physiology, ecology, embryology, cell, genetics, evolution, applied zoology — **full syllabus by Day 30**. Days 31–43 = two mocks, revision, question banks. Day 44 = exam. About **6 hours** daily: Biology 4 + General Knowledge 1.5 + revision 0.5–1.

## 2. Days by weightage — why this order

Scaling the 9 May 2026 paper ([analysis](/interview/up-pgt/jeev-vigyan/pariksha-margdarshika/pichhla-prashnapatra-2026-vishleshan)) to 90 questions:

| Book | Main units | Expected questions out of 90 (approx.) | Study days (Days 1–30) | Revision days (31–43) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Botany I](/interview/up-pgt/jeev-vigyan/vanaspati-1/padap-vargikee-aur-kul) | Taxonomy-families, algae, bryophytes, pteridophytes, gymnosperms, microbes, diseases, economic | ~33 | **7** (a full day for each cryptogam chapter) | 32, 33, 38, 42 |
| [Botany II](/interview/up-pgt/jeev-vigyan/vanaspati-2/aavritbeeji-aakariki) | Morphology, tissues, embryology, physiology, ecology | ~16 | 5 | 36, 40 |
| [Zoology I](/interview/up-pgt/jeev-vigyan/jantu-1/prani-vividhata-aur-vargikee) | Diversity, type studies, parasites, chordates, anatomy, tissues | ~17 | 4.5 | 34, 39 |
| [Zoology II](/interview/up-pgt/jeev-vigyan/jantu-2/poshan-aur-pachan) | Physiology, immunity-enzymes, embryology | ~8 | 5 | 35, 41 |
| [Zoology III](/interview/up-pgt/jeev-vigyan/jantu-3/koshika-sanrachna-aur-koshikaang) | Cell, genetics, biotechnology, evolution, biostatistics-toxicology-behaviour-wildlife | ~16 | 4.5 | 41 |
| Review/consolidation days | Question banks, self-tests | — | 4 (Days 7, 14, 21, 30) | 31, 37 (mocks) |

> **Exam angle:** Botany I got the most days because in 2026 plant diversity alone was 32/125, and cryptogam type studies are the newest material for candidates with a Zoology background. The chapters of Zoology II and III are close to NCERT, so there it is **two chapters a day**.

## 3. Daily 6 hours — a sample routine

| Time | Slot | What |
|---|---|---|
| 06:00–08:00 | Biology — part 1 | First half of the chapter: introduction, In simple words, NCERT-level sections |
| 08:00–09:00 | Break | Breakfast, walk |
| 09:00–11:00 | Biology — part 2 | B.Sc.-level sections, "PYQ" (answer covered), Traps and exceptions, practice questions |
| 11:00–15:00 | Break/other work | |
| 15:00–16:30 | General Knowledge | That day's GK chapter + 20 minutes of current affairs |
| 19:30–20:30 | Revision | The "revision" column of the table (+1, +3, +7) and the error log |
| Before sleep | 10 minutes | Today's chapter's "Quick revision" aloud |

> **Remember:** Take the mocks on Days 31 and 37 at **the same time** as the shift in which your exam is likely to be (it will be confirmed when the admit card comes). Until then, assume 10–12 in the morning.

## 4. How to read the table

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Day / date | Day of the plan and weekday-date |
| Biology (~4 hours) | Book name (bold) + chapter link; focus points on some days |
| General Knowledge (~1.5 hours) | Chapter of the [General Knowledge book](/interview/up-pgt/samanya-gyan/pattern-aur-ranniti) + 20 minutes of current affairs |
| Revision / mock | **+1 recall** — write 10 facts from yesterday's chapter without looking, then check; **+3 practice questions** — redo the practice questions of the chapter from three days ago; **+7 traps** — "Traps and exceptions" of the chapter from seven days ago and your own table |

### Review days (Days 7, 14, 21, 30)

1. Practice questions of that week's chapters again.
2. A 40-question self-test (from the question banks), 40 minutes, marked +3/−1 (4S − H).
3. Count the mistake codes in the error log — K, J, R, G, T ([method](/interview/up-pgt/jeev-vigyan/pariksha-margdarshika/pustaken-notes-aur-punravritti)).
4. If any chapter was missed, give it the first two hours.

## 5. If a day is missed

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| One day missed (illness, travel) | Do that chapter in the first 2–3 hours of the next **review day**; do not shift the rest of the table |
| Two–three days missed | Replace priority-C chapters (tissues, plant pathology, cell cycle) with that part of the same book's question bank; the rest on the review day |
| Syllabus incomplete by Day 30 | Take time from the revision slots of Days 32–36, but **do not drop Mock 1 (Day 31) and Mock 2 (Day 37)** |
| A chapter needs another half day | Give that day's GK slot to Biology; do not double GK the next day — just do the skipped chapter on the next GK-revision day |

> **Common mistake:** Shifting the whole plan one day forward when a day is missed. This cuts the mock and revision days at the end — which raise marks the most.

## 6. For those studying alongside a job (~4 hours/day)

| Slot | Time | Change |
|---|---|---|

| General Knowledge | 45 minutes | 15 minutes of current affairs + the chapter's tables |
| Revision | 20–30 minutes | Only +1 and +7; +3 together at the weekend |
| Weekend | 8 hours | Missed parts + review-day work |

## 7. The General Knowledge cycle

| Pass | Days | Method |
|---|---|---|
| First pass | 1–11 | The first 10 chapters (except the question bank) fully, one at a time |
| Second pass | 12–22 | Revision of the same chapters + questions |
| Third pass | 23–29 | Only tables, Uttar Pradesh specials, current-affairs list |
| Timed | 14, 21, 30, 42 | 30 questions from the [question bank](/interview/up-pgt/samanya-gyan/prashn-bank), 20 minutes |
| Final | 38–43 | 30 facts about Uttar Pradesh, current affairs |

## Traps and exceptions

- The plan assumes Day 44 = 15 December; if Biology turns out to be on 16 December, run Day 43 twice.
- The admit-card (about 3 days before) and exam-district notice (about 10 days before) dates are "approximate" — check the commission's website daily.
- Review days are not "holidays" — these are the days that keep the plan on track.
- No new chapter on mock days; analysis is as important as the mock.
- GK slot every day — 30 questions = 90 marks, 22.5% of the total.
