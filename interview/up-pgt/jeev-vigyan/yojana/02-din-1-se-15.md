The first 15 days are devoted to the **parts that carry the most marks**. In the 2026 paper, plant diversity (32), microbes (7), economic botany (7), non-chordates (10) and chordates (11) — a total of **67 questions (54%)** — came from these very chapters. So **Botany I and Zoology I** are almost completed in this phase.

> **In simple words:** Each day = 1 heavy Botany chapter **or** 2 Zoology chapters + 1 GK chapter + revision. Days 7 and 14 are review days. By Day 15 you will have completed 9 chapters of Botany I, 8 chapters of Zoology I and 3 chapters of Botany II.

**Before Day 1:** Five notebooks ready (see [Books, notes and revision](/interview/up-pgt/jeev-vigyan/pariksha-margdarshika/pustaken-notes-aur-punravritti)), NCERT 11–12 on the desk. In the first six days, the exam guide is read in the revision slot.

**Every day:** 20 minutes of current affairs in the GK slot; 5 lines in the current-affairs notebook. In the revision column, "+1 recall" = 10 facts from yesterday's chapter without looking; "+3 practice questions" = the practice questions of the chapter from three days ago; "+7 traps" = the "Traps and exceptions" section of the chapter from seven days ago.
