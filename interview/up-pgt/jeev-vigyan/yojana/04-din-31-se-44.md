**Nothing new** is read in the final phase. The two full mocks (+3/−1, 2 hours) show your weaknesses, and the days in between revise accordingly. The revision order follows the 2026 weightage — first Botany I (cryptogams), then Zoology I (types and chordates), then physiology and ecology.

> **In simple words:** Day 31 = Mock 1; Days 32–36 = book-wise revision + question banks; Day 37 = Mock 2; Days 38–43 = final week (matches [Exam-day strategy](/interview/up-pgt/jeev-vigyan/pariksha-margdarshika/pariksha-diwas-ranniti)); Day 44 = exam.

> **Remember:** This table assumes the Biology exam is on **15 December**. If the admit card shows **16 December**, repeat Day 43's plan on both 14 and 15 December (the second day even lighter), and the exam on the 16th.

> **Exam angle:** If Mock 2's score is lower than Mock 1's, do not panic — mocks differ in difficulty. Compare by **accuracy and mistake codes**, not by total marks.

> **Common mistake:** Taking "one more mock" after Day 42. The last two days are only for tables, sleep and confidence.
