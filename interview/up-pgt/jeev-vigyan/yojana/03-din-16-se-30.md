In the second phase **Zoology II** (physiology and embryology) and **Botany II** (plant physiology and ecology) run alternately, then **Zoology III** (from the cell to evolution). In 2026 relatively fewer questions came from this part (physiology 9 + 3, ecology 10, cell-genetics-evolution 9, applied 12), but these are closest to NCERT — so the pace is kept at **two chapters a day**.

> **In simple words:** 2 chapters every day. Day 21 is a review day, Day 30 a consolidation day. By the night of Day 29 the full syllabus is done once; on Day 30 the three remaining question banks.

**Every day:** 20 minutes of current affairs in the GK slot. In this phase the GK chapters get their **second and third pass** — only tables and your own mistakes.
