2–3 general science questions are expected, at the Class 9–10 level. For a Biology student the biology questions are easy, so this chapter has more **physics and chemistry**, and biology only briefly. The technology part (ISRO, defence) links to current affairs.

> **In simple words:** GK science asks "what" more than "why" — units, discoverers, common names, disease agents, vitamins, mission names and years.

## Physics

### SI units

| Quantity | SI unit |
|---|---|
| Force | newton (N) |
| Work / energy | joule (J) |
| Power | watt (W); 1 horsepower = 746 W |
| Pressure | pascal (Pa) |
| Frequency | hertz (Hz) |
| Electric current | ampere (A) — base unit |
| Potential difference | volt (V) |
| Resistance | ohm (Ω) |
| Temperature | kelvin (K) — base unit |
| Luminous intensity | candela (cd) — base unit |
| Amount of substance | mole — base unit |
| Power of a lens | dioptre (D) |
| Sound intensity level | decibel (dB) |
| Astronomical distance | light-year, parsec (units of distance, not time) |

- 7 base SI units — metre, kilogram, second, ampere, kelvin, mole, candela.
- Celsius and Fahrenheit are equal at −40°; K = °C + 273.15.

### Motion, force, gravity

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Newton's first law | Law of inertia |
| Second law | F = ma |
| Third law | Action-reaction — rocket, recoil of a gun |
| Value of g | ~9.8 m/s²; maximum at the poles, less at the equator; zero at the Earth's centre |
| Escape velocity (Earth) | ~11.2 km/s |
| Weight | On the Moon 1/6 of that on Earth (mass the same) |
| Archimedes' principle | Buoyant force = weight of liquid displaced |
| Pascal's law | Hydraulic lift, brakes |
| Bernoulli's theorem | Flight of an aeroplane |

### Light

| Phenomenon | Example |
|---|---|
| Refraction | A stick looks bent in water, stars twinkle |
| Total internal reflection | **Sparkle of diamond**, mirage, optical fibre |
| Scattering | **Blue sky** (Rayleigh), red sunrise-sunset; **Raman effect** (C.V. Raman, Nobel 1930; 28 February — National Science Day) |
| Dispersion | Rainbow, prism — violet bends the most |
| Myopia (short-sightedness) | **Concave** lens |
| Hypermetropia (long-sightedness) | **Convex** lens |
| Rear-view mirror of vehicles | **Convex** mirror |
| Shaving/dentist's mirror | **Concave** mirror |
| Speed of light (vacuum) | ~3 × 10⁸ m/s; Sun to Earth ~8 minutes 20 seconds |

### Sound

- Sound is a mechanical wave — **it does not travel in a vacuum**; speed — solid > liquid > gas (~343 m/s in air).
- Audible range 20 Hz – 20,000 Hz; below this **infrasonic** (earthquakes, elephants), above it **ultrasonic** (bats, SONAR, ultrasound).
- Minimum distance for an echo ~17 metres.

### Electricity and magnetism

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Ohm's law | V = IR |
| Household electricity | AC, **220 V, 50 Hz**; circuits in **parallel** |
| Fuse | Wire of low melting point (tin-lead alloy) |
| Bulb filament | **Tungsten** (high melting point) |
| Heater wire | **Nichrome** |
| Electric motor | Electrical → mechanical energy |
| Generator (dynamo) | Mechanical → electrical; **Faraday's** electromagnetic induction |
| Transformer | Works only on AC; changes voltage |
| Semiconductors | Silicon, germanium |

### Heat and modern physics

- Heat transfer — conduction (solids), convection (liquids-gases; sea and land breeze), radiation (even through vacuum; heat of the Sun).
- Maximum density of water — at **4°C** (that is why lakes freeze from the top).
- Nuclear energy — **fission** (nuclear reactor, atom bomb), **fusion** (Sun, hydrogen bomb).
- India's nuclear power stations — Tarapur (first, 1969), Rawatbhata, Kalpakkam, **Narora (Bulandshahr, UP)**, Kaiga, Kudankulam, Kakrapar.
- India's nuclear programme — **Homi Jehangir Bhabha**; nuclear tests — Pokhran 1974 ("Smiling Buddha") and 1998 (Operation Shakti).

## Space (ISRO)

| Mission | Date | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| Aryabhata | 1975 | India's first satellite (on a Soviet rocket) |
| Rakesh Sharma | 1984 | First Indian in space (Soyuz T-11) |
| Chandrayaan-1 | 2008 | Water molecules on the Moon |
| Mangalyaan (MOM) | 2013 | First country to reach Mars orbit in the first attempt |
| **Chandrayaan-3** | Landed **23 August 2023** | First country to land near the lunar **south pole**; site "**Shiv Shakti Point**"; 23 August — National Space Day |
| Aditya-L1 | 2 September 2023 | Study of the Sun; at the L1 point (6 January 2024) |
| SpaDeX | Docking 16 January 2025 | **Fourth** country to dock in space |
| **Axiom-4 / Shubhanshu Shukla** | June–July 2025 | Group Captain Shubhanshu Shukla of **Lucknow (UP)** — **first Indian** to go to the **International Space Station (ISS)**; second Indian in space after Rakesh Sharma |
| NISAR | 30 July 2025 | Joint NASA–ISRO Earth-observation satellite (GSLV-F16) |
| CMS-03 | 2 November 2025 | On LVM3-M5; India's heaviest communication satellite (~4,400 kg), for the Navy |
| LVM3-M6 (BlueBird Block-2) | 24 December 2025 | Commercial satellite of the US company AST SpaceMobile |
| Gaganyaan G1 (uncrewed) | Target end of 2026 | Robot **Vyommitra**; crewed flight after 2027 |

- ISRO — founded 1969; founder **Vikram Sarabhai**; headquarters Bengaluru; chairman **V. Narayanan** (since January 2025).
- Launch centre — **Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota** (Andhra Pradesh); second centre — Kulasekarapattinam (Tamil Nadu, under construction).
- Indian space station — "**Bharatiya Antariksh Station (BAS)**" targeted by 2035.

> **Exam angle:** **Shubhanshu Shukla is from Lucknow** — this can be a question in both UP-GK and science. He is also one of the four astronauts selected for Gaganyaan.

## Environment — key facts

- Earth Summit — Rio 1992 (UNFCCC); Kyoto Protocol 1997; **Paris Agreement 2015** (2°C, effort for 1.5°C).
- COP30 — Belém, Brazil (November 2025); COP31 — Antalya, Türkiye (2026).
- India's target — **net-zero by 2070** (COP26, Glasgow 2021).
- Environment Protection Act 1986 (after the Bhopal tragedy); Wildlife Protection Act 1972; **NGT 2010**.

## Traps and exceptions

- The light-year is a unit of **distance**, not time.
- Myopia — concave; hypermetropia — convex.
- Sound does not travel in a vacuum; light does.
- LPG — butane; CNG — methane; smell — mercaptan.
- Water has its maximum density at 4°C, not at 0°C.
- First Indian to go to the ISS — Shubhanshu Shukla; first Indian in space — Rakesh Sharma.
- BrahMos — named after the **Brahmaputra + Moskva** rivers.
