> **In simple words:** The backbone of UP's economy is **agriculture** (first in the country in wheat, sugarcane, potato, milk), its identity comes from **handicrafts** (ODOP), and the emphasis of the last ten years has been on **expressways, airports and investment**.

- UP has **9 agro-climatic zones**.
- Largest source of irrigation — **tube wells**, then canals.
- Land reform — **UP Zamindari Abolition and Land Reforms Act, 1950** (in force 1 July 1952) — among the first states to abolish zamindari.
- Consolidation of holdings (chakbandi) — UP Consolidation of Holdings Act, 1953.

> **Exam angle:** "In which is UP **not** first?" — a favourite form of question. The answer is usually one of **paddy/rice, lentil, cotton, soybean, oilseeds**.

## Transport and infrastructure

### Expressways

| Expressway | Connects | Length | Inaugurated |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yamuna Expressway | Greater Noida – Agra | 165 km | 2012 |
| Agra–Lucknow Expressway | Agra – Lucknow | 302 km | November 2016 |
| Delhi–Meerut Expressway (NHAI) | Delhi – Meerut | ~82 km | 2021 |
| **Purvanchal Expressway** | Lucknow (Chand Sarai) – Ghazipur (Haidaria) | ~341 km | **16 November 2021** (Sultanpur; Air Force display on the airstrip) |
| **Bundelkhand Expressway** | Chitrakoot (Bharatkoop) – Etawah (Kudrail) | ~296 km | **16 July 2022** (Jalaun) |
| Gorakhpur Link Expressway | Gorakhpur – Azamgarh (joins Purvanchal) | ~91 km | 2024–25 |
| **Ganga Expressway** | **Meerut – Prayagraj** | **594 km** | **29 April 2026, Hardoi** (Prime Minister) |

**Ganga Expressway — key facts:** UP's **longest** expressway; six lanes (expandable to eight); cost about **₹36,230 crore**; **12 districts** — Meerut, Hapur, Bulandshahr, Amroha, Sambhal, Budaun, Shahjahanpur, Hardoi, Unnao, Raebareli, Pratapgarh, Prayagraj; Meerut–Prayagraj travel cut from 10–12 hours to about 6 hours; extension from **Meerut to Haridwar** announced at the inauguration; airstrip for the Air Force at Shahjahanpur.

> **Remember:** UP has the largest expressway network in the country; the building agency is **UPEIDA** (UP Expressways Industrial Development Authority).

## Traps and exceptions

- UP is **not** first in **lentil and paddy**; it is first in **mentha, amla, potato, sugarcane, wheat, milk**.
- Modern Coach Factory is in **Raebareli**; rail wheel factories are at **Bela (Bihar)** and **Yelahanka (Bengaluru)**.
- IISR (sugarcane) is in **Lucknow**; IIPR (pulses) in **Kanpur**; IIVR (vegetables) in **Varanasi**.
- The Ganga Expressway was inaugurated at **Hardoi**, not at Meerut or Prayagraj.
- Noida airport was **inaugurated on 28 March 2026**, but **flights began on 15 June 2026** — the two dates are different.
- Purvanchal Expressway **Lucknow–Ghazipur**; Bundelkhand Expressway **Chitrakoot–Etawah**.
- ODOP **2018** (UP Day); Kanya Sumangala **2019**; Mission Shakti **2020**; Abhyuday **2021**.
