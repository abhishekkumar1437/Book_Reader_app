In a UP exam 2–3 questions on the state's latest events are almost certain — "**where was it inaugurated**", "**which site was added to the Ramsar list**", "**size of the budget**". 1–2 questions come from sports and awards. Every fact has been verified by web search and the month is given; whatever could not be confirmed has been left out.

> **In simple words:** The three biggest UP stories of this year — **Ganga Expressway (April 2026)**, **flights from Noida airport (June 2026)** and **four new Ramsar sites (Surha Tal is India's 100th)**.
