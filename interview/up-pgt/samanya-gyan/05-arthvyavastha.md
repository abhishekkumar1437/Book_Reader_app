2–3 economy questions are expected — usually on **definitions (GDP, inflation, repo rate), institutions (RBI, NITI Aayog, SEBI), the year/benefit of a scheme and the budget**. The economy, agriculture and state schemes of Uttar Pradesh are in [Chapter 08](/interview/up-pgt/samanya-gyan/uttar-pradesh-arthvyavastha-krishi-aur-yojnayen).

> **In simple words:** Economy = what the country produces (production), how money moves (banks, RBI), how the government earns and spends (taxes, budget), and what schemes exist for the poor, farmers and youth.

## Basic concepts

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **GDP** (Gross Domestic Product) | Market value of final goods and services produced within the country's borders in a year |
| **GNP** | GDP + net factor income from abroad |
| **NNP** | GNP − depreciation |
| National income | NNP at factor cost |
| Per capita income | National income ÷ population |
| Base year (GDP) | **2022–23** — new series released on **27 February 2026** (earlier 2011–12); new base year of **CPI** is **2024** (12 February 2026) |
| First estimate of national income | **Dadabhai Naoroji** (*Poverty and Un-British Rule in India*) — "**Drain of Wealth theory**" |
| Scientific estimate | V.K.R.V. Rao (1931) |
| Agency releasing data | **NSO** (Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation) |

| Sector | Examples | Share in GVA (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Agriculture, mining, fisheries | ~18% (but employment ~45%) |
| Secondary | Manufacturing, construction, electricity | ~28% |
| Tertiary | Services, banking, IT, trade | ~54% (the largest) |

> **Common mistake:** Older books give the GDP base year as 2011–12; from February 2026 it is **2022–23** (the first "normal" year after Covid). The new series adds GST data and "double deflation".

## From Planning to NITI Aayog

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planning Commission | **15 March 1950**; chairman — Prime Minister; first deputy chairman Gulzarilal Nanda |
| National Development Council | 1952 |
| **NITI Aayog** | **1 January 2015** (replaced the Planning Commission); chairman PM; CEO — **Anurag Jain** (since July 2026) |
| First Plan (1951–56) | Agriculture; **Harrod-Domar** model; successful |
| Second (1956–61) | Heavy industry; **P.C. Mahalanobis** model |
| Third (1961–66) | Self-reliance; failed because of wars → plan holiday (1966–69) |
| Fifth (1974–79) | "**Garibi Hatao**" |
| Eighth (1992–97) | After liberalisation; human development |
| 12th (2012–17) | Last Five-Year Plan; "Faster, more inclusive and sustainable growth" |

## Economic reforms of 1991

- Balance of payments crisis; Prime Minister **P.V. Narasimha Rao**, Finance Minister **Manmohan Singh**.
- **LPG** — Liberalisation, Privatisation, Globalisation; New Industrial Policy (24 July 1991) — licence raj ended.

## Banking and the RBI

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| RBI established | **1 April 1935** (recommendation of the Hilton Young Commission); nationalised **1949**; headquarters Mumbai (earlier Calcutta) |
| First Governor | Sir Osborne Smith; first Indian — **C.D. Deshmukh** |
| Current Governor | **Sanjay Malhotra** (since December 2024) |
| Bank nationalisation | **1969 — 14 banks**, **1980 — 6 banks** |
| SBI | 1955 (from the Imperial Bank) |
| NABARD | **1982** — agricultural-rural credit |
| SIDBI | 1990 — small industries (headquarters **Lucknow**) |
| Regional Rural Banks | 1975 — first **Prathama Grameen Bank, Moradabad (UP)**, 2 October 1975 |
| Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) | 6 members, chaired by the RBI Governor; inflation target **CPI 4% (±2%)** |
| **Repo rate** | **5.50%** — raised by 25 basis points on 7 October 2026 (earlier 5.25%) |

> **Exam angle:** **SIDBI headquarters at Lucknow** and the **first Regional Rural Bank at Moradabad** — these two UP facts of the economy are asked again and again.

### Instruments of monetary policy

| Instrument | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Repo rate | Rate at which the RBI gives short-term loans to banks — raising it reduces inflation |
| Reverse repo / SDF | Interest paid to banks for parking funds with the RBI |
| CRR | Share of deposits that banks keep as cash with the RBI |
| SLR | Share of deposits that banks themselves keep in liquid assets (gold, government securities) |
| Open market operations (OMO) | Buying and selling of government securities |

### Inflation

- **CPI** (Consumer Price Index) — released by the NSO; the RBI's target index.
- **WPI** (Wholesale Price Index) — Ministry of Commerce (Office of the Economic Adviser).
- **Stagflation** — inflation + recession/unemployment together.
- **Deflation** — general fall in prices.

## Taxes, GST and budget

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Direct tax | Income tax, corporate tax — burden on the person on whom it is levied |
| Indirect tax | GST, customs duty — burden can be shifted to others |
| **GST** | **1 July 2017** (101st Amendment); GST Council — **Art. 279A**, chaired by the Union Finance Minister |
| GST reform ("GST 2.0") | From **22 September 2025** two main rates — **5% and 18%**; 40% on luxury/harmful goods |
| New Income Tax Act | In force from **1 April 2026** (replacing the 1961 Act) |
| Finance Commission | **Art. 280**, every 5 years; **16th — chairman Arvind Panagariya** (period 2026–31) |
| Fiscal deficit | Total expenditure − (revenue receipts + non-debt capital receipts) = total borrowing of the government |
| FRBM Act | 2003 |

## Poverty, unemployment, indices

## Traps and exceptions

- **Raising the repo rate** = curbing inflation (reducing money flow); cutting it = boosting growth.
- CPI is released by the **NSO**; WPI by the Ministry of Commerce.
- First Regional Rural Bank — **Prathama, Moradabad** (1975).
- NITI Aayog is **not** constitutional (formed by executive resolution); the Finance Commission is constitutional (Art. 280).
- GST Council — Art. **279A**; Finance Commission — **280**.
- Mahalanobis — Second Plan; Harrod-Domar — First.
