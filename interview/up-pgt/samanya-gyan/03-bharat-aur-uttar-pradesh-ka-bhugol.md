3–4 geography questions are expected on India and at least 2–3 on Uttar Pradesh. In UP geography questions are built again and again on the same things — **in which district, on which river, which is the largest/smallest, in which soil.** The India part is brief; the UP part is detailed.

> **In simple words:** Think of UP as three belts — **the Terai in the north** (Himalayan foothills, dense forest, humid), **the flat Ganga-Yamuna plain in the middle** (about three-quarters of the state, fertile alluvium), **the plateau in the south** (Bundelkhand and Vindhya, rocky, dry, minerals).

### Physical divisions

| Division | Key facts |

### Climate, soils, forests

- India's climate — **tropical monsoon**; south-west monsoon reaches Kerala ~1 June; highest rainfall — **Mawsynram** (Meghalaya).
- Soils (ICAR — 8 types): **alluvial** (most widespread, ~40%), **black/regur** (cotton, Deccan), red, laterite (tea, cashew), desert, mountain, saline, peat.
- **ISFR 2023 (released December 2024):** forest cover **21.76%**, forest + tree cover **25.17%**; largest forest area — **Madhya Pradesh**.
- **Ramsar sites — 103** (after Sukhna Lake, Chandigarh and Sauj Lake, UP were added on 15 October 2026); most — **Tamil Nadu (20)**, second **Uttar Pradesh (14)**.

### Location, extent and boundaries

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|

| Latitude / longitude | about 23°52′ – 30°24′ N; 77°05′ – 84°38′ E |

### Physical regions

| Region | Area | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Bhabar** | Foot of the Shiwaliks (northern strip of Saharanpur, Bijnor, Pilibhit) | Pebbles and boulders; rivers go underground; unsuitable for farming |
| **Terai** | South of the Bhabar (Saharanpur to Kushinagar) | Rivers re-emerge; marshy, dense forest, tall grass; Dudhwa, Pilibhit; sugarcane-paddy |
| **Ganga-Yamuna plain** | About **three-quarters** of the state | Alluvium — **Bangar** (old, higher) and **Khadar** (new, flood zone); Bhur (sand dunes) |
| **Southern plateau** | Bundelkhand (Jhansi, Lalitpur, Jalaun, Hamirpur, Mahoba, Banda, Chitrakoot) and Vindhya/Baghelkhand (Mirzapur, Sonbhadra, south of Prayagraj) | Ancient rocks, low rainfall, minerals; ravines (Chambal, Yamuna) |

### Rivers and confluences

| River | UP-related facts |
|---|---|
| **Ganga** | Enters UP at **Bijnor**; leaves for Bihar at **Ballia**; longest stretch is in UP; Kanpur, Prayagraj, Varanasi, Ghazipur on its banks |
| **Yamuna** | Enters at **Saharanpur**; Mathura, Agra, Etawah; meets the Ganga at **Prayagraj** (Saraswati invisible — Triveni) |
| **Ramganga** | Joins the Ganga near **Kannauj** |
| **Gomti** | Origin **Gomat Tal (Fulhar Jheel), Pilibhit**; Lucknow, Jaunpur; joins the Ganga near **Kaithi (Ghazipur/Varanasi border)** |
| **Ghaghara (Saryu)** | Karnali in Nepal; Ayodhya on its bank; joins the Ganga near Chhapra in Bihar |
| **Sharda (Kali)** | Forms the India–Nepal border; Sharda canal (Banbasa) |
| **Rapti** | From Nepal; **Gorakhpur** on its bank; joins the Ghaghara |
| **Chambal** | Joins the Yamuna near **Etawah**; National Chambal Sanctuary (gharial, dolphin) |
| **Pachnada** | **Jalaun** (Etawah border) — confluence of the Yamuna, Chambal, Sindh, Pahuj, Kwari |
| **Betwa** | Joins the Yamuna at **Hamirpur**; Matatila, Rajghat dams |
| **Ken** | Joins the Yamuna near **Chilla (Banda)**; Ken-Betwa link project |
| **Son** | Sonbhadra; Rihand is its tributary (Rihand dam — Govind Ballabh Pant Sagar) |
| **Hindon** | From Saharanpur; through Ghaziabad-Noida into the Yamuna |
| **Varuna and Assi** | Varanasi — settled between the two, hence "Varanasi" |

> **Common mistake:** The Gomti does **not** rise in the Himalaya but in a lake in Pilibhit — it is a plains (groundwater-fed) river.

| Name | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Govind Ballabh Pant Sagar** | **Rihand dam, Pipri (Sonbhadra)**; among the largest artificial lakes in the country (UP–MP) |
| Matatila / Rajghat | On the Betwa (Lalitpur; UP–MP joint) |

### Soils

| Soil | Where | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Alluvial — Bangar** | Higher, older plains | Contains kankar, less fertile |
| **Alluvial — Khadar** | Flood zones of rivers | New, more fertile |
| Bhabar / Terai | Northern strip | Terai — fine, moist, rich in humus |
| **Soils of Bundelkhand** | Jhansi-Banda region | **Rakar** (stony, red), **Parwa** (light loam), **Mar** (black, cotton soil — "Mar"), **Kabar** (heavy black clay) |
| **Usar (Reh/Kallar)** | Raebareli, Unnao, Pratapgarh, Sultanpur etc. | Saline-alkaline; treatment — **gypsum**, pyrite, dhaincha |
| Bhur | Along the Ramganga-Ganga | Sandy |

> **Memory trick:** The four soils of Bundelkhand — "**R-P-M-K**" (Rakar, Parwa, Mar, Kabar). Mar and Kabar are black; Rakar is red and stony.

| Mineral | District |
|---|---|

> **Remember:** UP's minerals are almost entirely in the **southern plateau** (Sonbhadra, Mirzapur, Lalitpur, Jhansi, Chitrakoot). Sonbhadra is called the "**energy capital**" — Anpara, Obra, Rihand, Shaktinagar.

### Forests

- UP's forest cover is **around 6%** (ISFR 2023) — far below the national average (21.76%); about 10% including tree cover.
- Most forest — **Sonbhadra** (Vindhya), then the Terai districts (Lakhimpur Kheri, Bahraich, Pilibhit).
- Forest types — **tropical moist deciduous (sal)** in the Terai, **dry deciduous** in the south (tendu, teak, khair, mahua), thorn forests in the west.

### Climate

- Type — **tropical monsoon (humid subtropical)**; three seasons — summer (March–June, "loo"), rainy (June–September), winter (October–February).
- Rainfall **decreases from east to west and from north to south** — highest in the eastern Terai districts (Gorakhpur, Maharajganj), lowest in the west (Mathura-Agra) and Bundelkhand.
- Hottest region — **Bundelkhand** (Jhansi, Banda); light winter rain from western disturbances (beneficial for rabi).
- Agro-climatic zones — **9**.

## Traps and exceptions

- UP is **fourth** by area, not third (after Rajasthan, MP, Maharashtra).
- UP borders **8 states + Delhi (UT)** — an option saying "9 states" counts Delhi.
- Dudhwa is in **Lakhimpur Kheri**, not Lalitpur; UP has **only one** national park.
- First sanctuary **Chandraprabha (Chandauli)**, newest tiger reserve **Ranipur (Chitrakoot)**.
- The Ganga enters UP at **Bijnor** (Haridwar is in Uttarakhand).
- Pachnada is in **Jalaun**; the Betwa-Yamuna confluence is at **Hamirpur**; Ken-Yamuna at **Banda**.
- The state animal is the **barasingha**, not the tiger (the tiger is the national animal).
