5–7 current affairs questions are expected (including UP current affairs). They come from the **6–12 months** before the exam and usually ask "**who, where, which number**" — who is the 53rd CJI, where was the 18th BRICS, where is the 2026 G20. Here the month is given with every fact; office-holders' names are as of **1 November 2026**. UP, sports and awards are in the next chapter.

> **In simple words:** From every event take four things — **when, where, who, which number.** Many questions are built on the number (e.g. 15th Vice-President, 29th Air Force chief).
