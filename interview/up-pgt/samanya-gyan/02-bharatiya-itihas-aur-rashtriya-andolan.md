4–5 questions are expected from history, and the syllabus says "especially the freedom movement". So ancient and medieval India are covered briefly here and **1857–1947 in detail**. Facts related to Uttar Pradesh are marked **[UP]** — these are twice as likely to be asked. A detailed account of UP's own history (Awadh, the centres of 1857, UP's freedom fighters) is in [Chapter 07](/interview/up-pgt/samanya-gyan/uttar-pradesh-itihas-sanskriti-aur-parv).

> **In simple words:** For every event remember four things — **when, where, who, and why it is famous.** The tables are built in this order.

### Vedic period

- Rigveda — the oldest; 10 mandalas, 1028 hymns; **Gayatri Mantra** (3rd mandala, dedicated to Savitr).
- "Satyameva Jayate" — from the **Mundaka Upanishad**.
- Most important Rigvedic river — Indus; most sacred — Saraswati.
- In the later Vedic period the Aryans spread into the Ganga-Yamuna Doab **[UP]**; Kuru-Panchala were prominent.

### Harshavardhana (606–647 CE)

- Capital — Thanesar, then **Kannauj [UP]**.
- Chinese traveller **Xuanzang (Hiuen Tsang)** — studied at Nalanda; *Si-Yu-Ki*.
- **Kannauj assembly (643 CE)** and the Mahamoksha Parishad at **Prayag [UP]** every five years.
- Court poet **Banabhatta** — *Harshacharita*, *Kadambari*; Harsha himself — *Nagananda*, *Ratnavali*, *Priyadarshika*.
- After Harsha, the **tripartite struggle for Kannauj** — Gurjara-Pratiharas, Palas, Rashtrakutas.

## Traps and exceptions

- **Meerut (10 May)** is the start of the revolt; **Mangal Pandey (29 March, Barrackpore)** is a solitary incident before it.
- At **Kanpur 1925** Sarojini Naidu was the "first **Indian** woman president"; the first woman president was **Annie Besant (1917)**.
- **Chauri-Chaura** is in Gorakhpur; the Bardoli resolution was passed in Gujarat.
- The **Swaraj Party** was formed at Allahabad (1923) — after differences at the Gaya session (1922).
- **Lucknow Pact (1916)** was between the Congress and the Muslim League; **Poona Pact (1932)** between Gandhi and Ambedkar.
- **First sermon** at Sarnath, **nirvana** at Kushinagar — do not swap them.
- **Agra** was founded by Sikandar Lodi; **Fatehpur Sikri** by Akbar.
