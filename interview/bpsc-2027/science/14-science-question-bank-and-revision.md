## Recall questions

### Physics — units, motion, gravitation

1. ⭐ What is a light year a unit of? — **Distance** (≈ 9.46 × 10¹⁵ m).
2. ⭐ What is the commercial unit of electrical energy and its value in joules? — **kWh**; 3.6 × 10⁶ J.
3. How many SI base units are there? — **Seven** (m, kg, s, A, K, mol, cd).
4. What is the SI unit of pressure? — **Pascal** (N/m²).
5. Which unit measures the power of a lens? — **Dioptre**.
6. 1 horsepower is roughly how many watts? — **746 W**.
7. ⭐ Why does a passenger fall forward when a bus stops suddenly? — **Inertia of motion** (Newton's first law).
8. ⭐ Which law explains rocket propulsion? — **Newton's third law** (action–reaction).
9. Why does a cricketer pull his hands back while catching? — Increases time of impact, reducing force (**impulse**).
10. ⭐ Where is the value of g maximum on Earth, and what is it at the centre? — **Poles**; **zero** at the centre.
11. What is the escape velocity from Earth? — **11.2 km/s**.
12. ⭐ Why do astronauts feel weightless in orbit? — They are in continuous **free fall** with the spacecraft.
13. Height of a geostationary satellite? — About **35,786 km** (≈ 36,000 km), period 24 h.
14. Why are dams thicker at the base? — Liquid **pressure increases with depth**.
15. ⭐ Hydraulic brakes and lifts work on which principle? — **Pascal's law**.
16. Lift on an aeroplane wing is explained by which principle? — **Bernoulli's principle**.
17. Why does an iron ship float? — It displaces water equal to its weight (**Archimedes' principle**).
18. Why do raindrops become spherical? — **Surface tension**.
19. How much work does a coolie standing still with a load do? — **Zero** (no displacement).
20. If speed doubles, kinetic energy becomes? — **Four times**.
21. The human forearm is which class of lever? — **Third class**.

### Physics — heat, light, sound

22. ⭐ At what temperature are Celsius and Fahrenheit equal? — **−40°**.
23. Normal body temperature in °F? — **98.6 °F** (37 °C).
24. Why do lakes freeze from the top? — **Anomalous expansion** of water (densest at 4 °C).
25. ⭐ Why are steam burns worse than boiling-water burns? — **Latent heat of vaporisation**.
26. Why does water stay cool in an earthen pot? — **Evaporation** causes cooling.
27. Who invented the thermos (vacuum) flask? — **James Dewar**.
28. ⭐ Diamond's sparkle, mirage and optical fibres are due to? — **Total internal reflection**.
29. ⭐ Why is the sky blue and the sunset red? — **Scattering** of light (blue scattered most).
30. Why do stars twinkle? — **Atmospheric refraction**.
31. ⭐ Which lens corrects myopia, and which hypermetropia? — **Concave**; **convex**.
32. Which mirror is used as a rear-view mirror? — **Convex** mirror.
33. ⭐ National Science Day and its reason? — **28 February**; announcement of the **Raman effect** (1928).
34. Speed of light in vacuum? — **3 × 10⁸ m/s**.
35. Audible frequency range? — **20 Hz to 20 kHz**.
36. Bats navigate using? — **Ultrasound** (echolocation).
37. Why is lightning seen before thunder is heard? — Light travels much faster than sound.
38. Loudness depends on? Pitch depends on? — **Amplitude**; **frequency**.
39. Change in pitch of a passing train's whistle is called? — **Doppler effect**.

### Physics — electricity and modern physics

40. ⭐ State Ohm's law. — **V = IR** at constant temperature.
41. ⭐ How are household appliances connected? — In **parallel**.
42. Filament of a bulb is made of? Heater coil? — **Tungsten**; **nichrome**.
43. Who discovered electromagnetic induction? — **Michael Faraday** (1831).
44. A transformer works on AC or DC? — **AC only**.
45. Who discovered the electron, proton and neutron? — **Thomson**, **Rutherford**, **Chadwick**.
46. Which radiation penetrates the most? — **Gamma** rays.
47. ⭐ Which isotope is used for dating archaeological remains? — **Carbon-14**.
48. In a reactor, what does the moderator do? Name one. — Slows neutrons; **heavy water** or graphite.
49. Control rods are made of? — **Cadmium or boron**.
50. Energy of the Sun comes from? — **Nuclear fusion**.
51. Einstein's Nobel Prize was for? — **Photoelectric effect**.
52. Bosons are named after? — **Satyendra Nath Bose**.

### Chemistry

53. ⭐ Name four substances that sublime. — **Camphor, naphthalene, iodine, ammonium chloride** (and dry ice).
54. Crude oil is separated by? — **Fractional distillation**.
55. Cream is separated from milk by? — **Centrifugation**.
56. Who gave the gold-foil experiment? — **Rutherford**.
57. ⭐ Most abundant element and metal in the Earth's crust? — **Oxygen**; **aluminium**.
58. The only liquid metal and liquid non-metal at room temperature? — **Mercury**; **bromine**.
59. Which form of carbon conducts electricity? — **Graphite**.
60. Sodium and potassium are stored in? — **Kerosene**.
61. ⭐ Rusting needs? How is it prevented by galvanisation? — **Air and moisture**; coating with **zinc**.
62. Catalyst in the Haber process? In hydrogenation of oils? — **Iron**; **nickel**.
63. ⭐ Acid in tamarind, ant sting, vinegar, curd? — **Tartaric, formic, acetic, lactic**.
64. ⭐ pH of human blood? — About **7.4**.
65. Acidic soil is treated with? Alkaline soil? — **Lime**; **gypsum**.
66. ⭐ Chemical formula of baking soda and plaster of Paris? — **NaHCO₃**; **CaSO₄·½H₂O**.
67. ⭐ Chemical in indelible election ink? — **Silver nitrate**.
68. Aqua regia composition? — **3 HCl : 1 HNO₃** (dissolves gold).
69. ⭐ Composition of brass and bronze? — **Cu + Zn**; **Cu + Sn**.
70. Ore of aluminium? Of lead? — **Bauxite**; **galena**.
71. Why is ethyl mercaptan added to LPG? — To give a **smell** to detect leaks.
72. Fuel with highest calorific value? — **Hydrogen**.
73. ⭐ Minamata disease and itai-itai disease are caused by? — **Mercury**; **cadmium**.
74. Who discovered penicillin? — **Alexander Fleming** (1928).
75. Gas leaked in the Bhopal tragedy? — **Methyl isocyanate**.

88. Which blood cells fall in dengue? — **Platelets**.
89. Which part of the brain controls balance? — **Cerebellum**.
90. ⭐ Which hormone lowers blood sugar? — **Insulin** (pancreas).
91. Iodine deficiency causes? — **Goitre**.
92. ⭐ Deficiency of vitamins A, B₁, C, D? — **Night blindness, beriberi, scurvy, rickets**.

94. ⭐ Malaria is caused by? Spread by? — ***Plasmodium***; **female *Anopheles***.
95. ⭐ Kala-azar is caused by? Spread by? — ***Leishmania donovani***; **sandfly**.

97. ⭐ When was India certified polio-free? — **27 March 2014**.
98. Ringworm is caused by? — A **fungus**.
99. Which plant hormone ripens fruit? — **Ethylene**.
100. Oxygen in photosynthesis comes from? — **Water**.
101. ⭐ India's national aquatic animal and its sanctuary in Bihar? — **Gangetic dolphin**; **Vikramshila Gangetic Dolphin Sanctuary, Bhagalpur**.

## Mains questions with outlines

### Science & technology for development (GS II section 3)

**1. ⭐ Discuss the role and impact of science and technology in the development of Bihar.** (38 marks)

- **Intro:** Bihar's challenges (floods, small farms, health, migration) and S&T as an enabler; heritage of Aryabhata and Nalanda.
- **Agriculture:** Road Maps, flood-tolerant rice, zero tillage, climate-resilient agriculture, makhana/litchi value chains.
- **Disasters:** flood forecasting (FMISC), satellite maps, Indra Vajra, DRR Roadmap.
- **Water and health:** Har Ghar Nal Ka Jal with arsenic/fluoride plants, kala-azar elimination, AES SOP, eSanjeevani.
- **Governance:** RTPS, grievance redressal, DBT, Bihar Bhumi, Dial 112.
- **Energy and industry:** Har Ghar Bijli, smart meters, ethanol policy, start-up and IT policies.
- **Challenges:** low R&D, brain drain, digital divide, maintenance.
- **Conclusion:** people-centred S&T can speed up Bihar's convergence with richer states.

**2. ⭐ "India's space programme serves the common man." Discuss.** (38 marks)

- **Intro:** Sarabhai's vision.
- **Body:** communication, weather and cyclone warnings, remote sensing for crops and water, flood maps for Bihar, NavIC, Bhuvan geotagging, SVAMITVA, tele-education and telemedicine; prestige missions (Chandrayaan-3, Aditya-L1) building capability and inspiring youth; private-sector reforms (IN-SPACe).
- **Conclusion:** space is development infrastructure.

**3. ⭐ Evaluate India's digital public infrastructure. What are its risks?**

- **Intro:** India Stack.
- **Body:** Aadhaar, UPI, DBT, DigiLocker, CoWIN, ONDC; financial inclusion; Bihar's DBT schemes; risks — exclusion, privacy (DPDP Act 2023), cyber fraud, digital divide; remedies.
- **Conclusion:** inclusion and trust are the next frontier.

**4. ⭐ Discuss the potential and concerns of artificial intelligence in governance and agriculture in India.**

- **Intro:** IndiaAI Mission (2024), #AIforAll.
- **Body:** applications (advisories, disease detection, forecasting, translation via Bhashini, health screening); concerns (jobs, bias, deepfakes, privacy, accountability); regulation approach.
- **Conclusion:** responsible, language-inclusive AI.

**5. "Biotechnology holds the key to India's food and health security." Examine.**

- **Intro:** rDNA, PCR, CRISPR; BioE3 policy.
- **Body:** Bt cotton, genome-edited rice (2025), biofortification, Sub-1 rice for Bihar, vaccines (Covaxin, ZyCoV-D), diagnostics (rK39), CAR-T; GM debates, biosafety, GEAC, biopiracy and TKDL.
- **Conclusion:** science-based regulation and public trust.

**6. ⭐ Discuss India's strategy for energy transition and its implications for Bihar.**

- **Intro:** Panchamrit, 2070 net zero, 50% non-fossil capacity reached in 2025.
- **Body:** solar (PM Surya Ghar, KUSUM), wind, storage, green hydrogen mission, nuclear (PFBR, SMRs), ethanol; Bihar — rooftop/floating solar, maize ethanol (Purnia), biogas, Husk Power mini-grids, dependence on coal plants; just transition.
- **Conclusion:** local green growth.

**7. Explain the three-stage nuclear power programme of India and its significance for energy security.**

- **Intro:** Bhabha; low uranium, high thorium.
- **Body:** PHWR → FBR → thorium; status; benefits; risks; reforms and SMRs.
- **Conclusion:** long-term thorium vision.

**8. ⭐ "Self-reliance in defence technology is essential for national security." Discuss with recent examples.**

- **Intro:** import dependence.
- **Body:** IGMDP to Agni-V MIRV, BrahMos exports, Tejas, INS Vikrant, Arihant, positive indigenisation lists, corridors, iDEX, defence exports; Ordnance Factory Nalanda; challenges (engines, R&D).
- **Conclusion:** steady policy and private participation.

### Health, nutrition and environment

**9. ⭐ How has science helped Bihar fight kala-azar and AES? What lessons do these offer for public health?**

- **Intro:** endemic diseases of Bihar.
- **Body:** kala-azar — rK39, single-dose AmBisome, spraying, ASHAs; AES — research linking hypoglycaemia, litchi toxins and malnutrition; night-meal campaign, glucose correction, JE vaccine, SOP.
- **Lessons:** research + local data + community action + nutrition.
- **Conclusion:** model for other diseases.

**10. ⭐ Malnutrition and anaemia remain high in Bihar. Suggest science-based solutions.**

- **Intro:** NFHS-5 figures (check NFHS-6).
- **Body:** fortification, biofortified crops, IFA and deworming, Poshan Tracker, WASH, diverse diets (millets, pulses, makhana), Jeevika nutrition; convergence.
- **Conclusion:** multi-sector action.

**11. Discuss the problem of groundwater contamination in Bihar and the technological remedies.**

- **Intro:** arsenic (Ganga belt), fluoride (south), iron (north-east).
- **Body:** health effects; testing, treatment plants, surface-water schemes (Har Ghar Nal Ka Jal, Ganga water lift), rainwater harvesting, research.
- **Conclusion:** safe water as a right.

**12. Explain how vaccines work and assess India's immunisation achievements.**

- **Intro:** active immunity.
- **Body:** vaccine types; UIP, Mission Indradhanush, U-WIN; smallpox, polio, MNT; COVID-19 vaccination; gaps.
- **Conclusion:** last-mile coverage.

**13. ⭐ "Bihar must learn to live with floods." Discuss the role of technology.**

- **Intro:** Kosi, 2008 Kusaha breach.
- **Body:** forecasting, mapping, warnings, resilient crops, raised housing, embankment science, wetlands, Nepal cooperation, institutions.
- **Conclusion:** from flood control to flood management.

**14. Discuss the role of science and technology in reducing air pollution in Indian cities with reference to Patna.**

- **Intro:** NCAP cities including Patna, Gaya, Muzaffarpur.
- **Body:** sources; BS-VI, e-mobility, CNG, zig-zag kilns, monitoring and AQI, dust control, waste processing, Ujjwala; enforcement.
- **Conclusion:** airshed approach.
