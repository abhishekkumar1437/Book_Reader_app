Government schemes are the largest single source of current-affairs questions in state PSC papers, and they feed Mains answers in polity, economy and social justice. The problem is volume: hundreds of schemes, frequent renaming, changing targets. The answer is a **tracker** — one table you keep updating all year. This chapter gives you the template and fills it with the major central schemes as they stood in 2026. Bihar's own schemes (Saat Nischay, JEEViKA, Jal-Jeevan-Hariyali and others) are covered in [Government Schemes and Policies of Bihar](/interview/bpsc-2027/bihar-special/government-schemes-and-policies-of-bihar), and the economics of welfare in [Social Sector and Flagship Schemes](/interview/bpsc-2027/economy/social-sector-and-flagship-schemes).

> **In simple words:** For every scheme, learn **name — launch year — ministry — who benefits — one key number — Bihar link.** Do not memorise every detail. Learn the core six fields; add news (a new phase, a new target, a renamed scheme) as it comes.

> **Remember:** Everything in this chapter is **as of 2026 — check the latest**. Schemes get extended, merged and renamed. Before Prelims, check each major scheme's current status on **pib.gov.in** or the ministry website.

Two terms you must know:

- **Central Sector Scheme (CS):** fully funded and run by the Centre (for example, PM-KISAN).
- **Centrally Sponsored Scheme (CSS):** run by states with shared funding — usually **60:40** (Centre:State), **90:10** for North-Eastern and Himalayan states, and **100%** for UTs without a legislature (for example, PMAY-G, Jal Jeevan Mission). Bihar pays the state share for CSS, which matters for Bihar's budget.

> **Prelims angle:** Questions often test **launch year**, **ministry**, **the amount of benefit**, and "which of these is not a feature". Watch for renamed schemes: Mid-Day Meal → **PM POSHAN**; Health and Wellness Centres → **Ayushman Arogya Mandir**; Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna → **Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna**; MGNREGA → **VB-G RAM G** (from 1 July 2026).

- [ ] Name in English and Hindi; any earlier name
- [ ] Launch date and place (PM's launch city is often asked)
- [ ] Ministry or department

- [ ] Bihar link (number of beneficiaries, rank, district, model scheme)
- [ ] One Mains point: what problem does it solve, what is its weakness?

## Key terms

| Term | Simple meaning |
|---|---|
| Central Sector Scheme | A scheme fully paid for and run by the Union government |
| Centrally Sponsored Scheme | A scheme run by states with costs shared between Centre and state |
| DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) | Money sent straight into the beneficiary's bank account |
| JAM trinity | Jan Dhan accounts + Aadhaar + Mobile phones, used together for DBT |
| SHG (Self-Help Group) | A small group of (usually) women who save together and take loans |
| PVTG | Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Group — 75 most marginalised tribal communities |
| ODF | Open Defecation Free |
| Convergence | Combining several schemes in one place for a bigger effect |
| Social audit | Checking of a scheme's work and accounts by the local community |
| Delimitation | Redrawing of election constituency boundaries based on population |
