Your goal this fortnight is to **revise four big subjects once, quickly and actively**, while taking **two full mocks a week**. By Day 266 you should have revised polity, geography, economy and most of science, and your mock scores should show a clear trend you can read.

> **In simple words:** Each study day you revise one block of a subject from your notes and quick-revision boxes, then test it with old BPSC questions. Twice a week you take a full 2-hour mock and spend an hour learning from it. No new books now — only revision and testing.

Keep the **1-hour newspaper block** daily. Day 266 is the **month-9 revision day** with the monthly compilation.

**Score tracking.** Keep a small table: mock number, net score, right, wrong, unattempted, and section scores (history, polity, geography, economy, science, CA, GMA, Bihar). After four mocks, look at the trend: which section gives the most wrong answers? That section gets extra time in Round 2.

## What you will learn

**Polity at revision speed.** You should now recall article numbers without effort: 14–18 (equality), 19 (freedoms), 21 (life and liberty), 21A (education), 32 (remedies), 44 (uniform civil code — DPSP), 51A (duties), 72 (pardon by President), 110 (Money Bill), 123 (ordinance), 148 (CAG), 280 (Finance Commission), 324 (Election Commission), 352/356/360 (emergencies), 368 (amendment).

**Geography at revision speed.** Focus on maps in your head: rivers of Bihar, national parks and sanctuaries, mineral belts, soils, crops by season, Census 2011 numbers.

**Economy at revision speed.** Definitions first (GDP, repo, CRR, fiscal deficit), then schemes and Bihar's data card.

**Science at revision speed.** Units, everyday applications, vitamins, diseases, body systems — the most repeated areas.

**Past-paper sets.** Do not just check right or wrong. For each question, ask: what fact was tested? Is it in my notes? If not, add it.

**Statement-based questions.** Many BPSC questions give 2–3 statements and ask which are correct. Read each statement on its own, mark it true or false, and only then look at the options. Watch for absolute words like "only", "always", "all" — they are often false.

## End-of-fortnight self-check

1. Which article gives the President the power to grant pardons?
2. Which article deals with ordinances by the President?
3. Which article lays down the procedure to amend the Constitution?
4. What is the Uniform Civil Code article, and in which part is it?
5. What is CRR?
6. What is the difference between kharif and rabi crops?
7. Which river joins the Ganga near Patna from the south?
8. What does a statement with the word "only" usually signal in an MCQ?
9. How long should mock analysis take after a 2-hour mock?
10. What is the net score for 120 attempted with 90 right?

**Mains question to write in full (300 words, keep the habit):** "Examine the role of the Finance Commission in strengthening fiscal federalism, with reference to Bihar." Points: Article 280, composition and tenure; vertical and horizontal devolution; criteria (population, area, income distance, forest, demographic performance); grants; Bihar's large share and why; issues — cesses and surcharges outside the divisible pool, conditional grants; conclusion.

### Answers

1. **Article 72**.
2. **Article 123**.
3. **Article 368**.
4. **Article 44**, in **Part IV** (Directive Principles).
5. **Cash Reserve Ratio** — the share of a bank's deposits that it must keep as cash with the RBI.
6. **Kharif** crops are sown with the monsoon (June–July) and harvested in autumn (rice, maize); **rabi** crops are sown in winter (October–November) and harvested in spring (wheat, gram, mustard).
7. The **Son**.
8. It is often a **trap** — absolute words make many statements false; check carefully.
9. About **1 hour**.
10. Wrong = 30 → 90 − 10 = **80**.

## If you fall behind

- Never move a mock. If a day slips, drop that day's past-paper set, not the mock.
- If you are behind by more than 3 days, revise two smaller subjects in one day (e.g. geography chapters 1–4 and 5–8 together) using only quick-revision boxes.
- Keep Bihar parts of every subject at full depth.
