Your goal in these last six days is to **arrive at the exam hall fresh, confident and fully prepared for the rules of the paper**. The learning is done. What remains is to keep facts warm, practise the exact exam routine once more, and avoid silly losses — a wrongly filled OMR, a missing document, a blank row.

> **In simple words:** Take your last mocks, revise only your one-page sheets and mistake log, rest on the last day, and follow the checklist below on exam day. Read the OMR instructions slowly — especially what option E means and what happens to blank rows.

Keep the **newspaper hour** till Day 299, but only skim headlines and Bihar news.

## Option E and negative marking — the final strategy

The **72nd CCE** Prelims has **150 questions, 150 marks, 2 hours, and 1/3 mark deducted for each wrong answer**. The OMR sheet has a fifth bubble, **E**. Secondary reports on the 72nd notification (May 2026) say:

- **Option E = "not attempted"** — you fill E when you do not want to answer; it gives **0 marks**.
- A row left **completely blank** (no bubble filled) is **penalised like a wrong answer**.

This is the rule in the official 72nd CCE notice. **Read the instructions on your own 73rd notification, admit card and OMR sheet** and follow them exactly, in case BPSC changes it.

**Safe strategy.**
1. **Never leave a row blank.** Every row gets exactly one bubble — A, B, C, D, or E as per the rules.
2. **Sure answers:** mark them.
3. **Two options removed:** guess between the remaining two — on average this gains about ⅓ mark per question.
4. **One option removed:** guess only if your mocks show your accuracy in such cases is good; otherwise use E.
5. **No idea at all:** use E (if E means "not attempted" in your paper).
6. **Bubble as you go**, in small groups (say every 10 questions), not all at the end. Keep the last 10 minutes to check that every row has one bubble.

**Time plan (120 minutes, about 48 seconds per question).**

| Time | What you do |
|---|---|
| First 2–3 min | Check booklet series, fill roll number and booklet code exactly as instructed |
| Pass 1 (about 70 min) | Go through all 150; answer the sure ones, mark "?" on the booklet for the doubtful ones |
| Pass 2 (about 30 min) | Return to "?" questions; apply the two-option rule |
| Last 10–15 min | Fill any remaining rows (E as per the rules), check every row has exactly one bubble, check roll number and series |

GMA questions can be done in Pass 1 if they are quick; leave long calculations for Pass 2.

## Prelims-day checklist

**The day before (Day 300)**
- [ ] Download and print the **admit card**; check name, photo, roll number, centre, reporting time and exam time.
- [ ] Print **two copies** of the admit card and keep one in the bag.
- [ ] Keep the **original photo ID** named on the admit card (e.g. Aadhaar, voter ID, PAN, driving licence, passport — follow the admit card's list) and a photocopy if asked.
- [ ] Keep **2–3 passport photos** if the admit card asks for them.
- [ ] Keep **two black (or blue, as instructed) ballpoint pens** that write smoothly.
- [ ] Read the "do's and don'ts" printed on the admit card — banned items (mobile phones, smart watches, calculators, papers, bags) must be left at home or as instructed.
- [ ] Check the route to the centre and travel time; plan to reach well before the reporting time.
- [ ] Keep a transparent water bottle and a light snack if allowed.
- [ ] Light reading only; no new topics; sleep early.

**On the day (Day 301)**
- [ ] Eat a light, familiar breakfast.
- [ ] Reach the centre early; find your room and seat.
- [ ] Before writing, read every instruction on the OMR sheet and booklet cover — **especially option E and the rule for blank rows**.
- [ ] Fill roll number, booklet series and all other details exactly; darken the bubbles fully.
- [ ] Follow the two-pass time plan above.
- [ ] Watch for "not correct" / "incorrect" questions; underline those words.
- [ ] In the last 10 minutes, check that **every row has exactly one bubble**.
- [ ] Keep the question booklet if candidates are allowed to take it; otherwise note your answers as permitted.

**After the paper**
- [ ] Do not discuss answers at the gate; go home and rest.
- [ ] Next day, match answers with keys **once**, note an estimate, and stop.
- [ ] Start the Mains sprint on Day 303 whatever your estimate — Mains preparation time is short.
- [ ] Watch bpsc.bihar.gov.in for the provisional answer key and the window to file objections.

## What you will learn

**Exam-day calm.** Most Prelims losses in the last week come from panic, not from lack of knowledge: changing correct answers, misreading "not", filling the wrong row. A fixed routine reduces panic.

**The OMR.** An OMR (optical mark recognition) sheet is read by a machine. Half-filled or double bubbles may be read wrongly or rejected. Darken fully, only one bubble per row.

**Change of answers.** Change an answer only if you find a clear reason (you misread the question or remembered a fact). Do not change on a vague feeling.

## If you fall behind

- In the final week there is no "behind". If you missed mocks, do not try to fit them all in; take one on Day 299 at exam time and rest on Day 300.
- If you feel unwell, rest. One extra hour of sleep is worth more than one more mock now.
