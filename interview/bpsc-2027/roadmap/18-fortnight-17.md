Your goal this fortnight is to **learn to write a 1,000-word essay in about an hour** and to **start General Hindi**. The essay paper carries **300 marks** — as much as a full GS paper — and General Hindi must be cleared with at least **30%** — its marks are not added to merit, but failing it can cost you selection (check the exact rule in your notification). Both reward steady practice more than reading.

> **In simple words:** An essay is a long, well-organised answer on a broad theme. You plan it in 10 minutes, write it in about 50, and fill it with examples, data and quotes — especially from Bihar. General Hindi is class-10 grammar and writing; you only need to pass it, but you must not take it lightly.

Keep the **1-hour newspaper block** daily — it is your best source of fresh essay examples. Day 238 is the **month-8 revision day** with the monthly compilation.

## What you will learn

**The essay paper.** Three sections; you write **one essay from each section**, in about three hours in total (check the exact word limits and topics per section in your notification and past papers). Topics are broad: society, economy, Bihar, science, environment, ethics.

**Planning.** Spend 10 minutes before writing: list 6–8 **dimensions** (social, economic, political, historical, environmental, Bihar, global, ethical), choose an opening, place quotes and examples, and decide the conclusion.

**Structure.** **Introduction** (a story, quote, question or fact) → **body** (one paragraph per dimension, linked by transition sentences) → **counter-view** (challenges, criticism) → **way forward** → **conclusion** (return to the opening idea, hopeful and balanced).

**Content banks.** A **quotes bank** (Gandhi, Ambedkar, Tagore, Vivekananda, Dinkar, Kalam…), a **Bihar examples bank** (JEEViKA women, Kosi floods, Nalanda's revival, Madhubani, migrant workers, panchayat reservation), and a small **data bank** with sources and years.

**General Hindi pattern.** A 100-mark qualifying paper at about class-10 level: essay, grammar, sentence structure, précis (संक्षेपण), comprehension (check the section-wise marks in the official syllabus).

**Sandhi (संधि).** Joining of sounds when two words meet. Three types: **स्वर संधि** (vowel), **व्यंजन संधि** (consonant), **विसर्ग संधि** (visarga). Examples: विद्या + आलय = **विद्यालय** (दीर्घ संधि); सूर्य + उदय = **सूर्योदय** (गुण संधि); सत् + आचार = **सदाचार** (व्यंजन संधि); मनः + योग = **मनोयोग** (विसर्ग संधि).

**Samas (समास).** Joining of words into a compound. Six types: **अव्ययीभाव** (यथाशक्ति), **तत्पुरुष** (राजपुत्र = राजा का पुत्र), **कर्मधारय** (नीलकमल = नीला है जो कमल), **द्विगु** (चौराहा = चार राहों का समूह), **द्वंद्व** (माता-पिता), **बहुव्रीहि** (नीलकंठ = नीला है कंठ जिसका, अर्थात् शिव).

**Upsarg and pratyay (उपसर्ग, प्रत्यय).** An **upsarg** is added before a root word and changes its meaning: अभि + मान = अभिमान; प्र + गति = प्रगति. A **pratyay** is added after: सुंदर + ता = सुंदरता; लिख + आवट = लिखावट.
