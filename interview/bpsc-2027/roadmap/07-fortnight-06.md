## What you will learn

**Anti-defection law.** The **Tenth Schedule**, added by the **52nd Amendment (1985)**, disqualifies a legislator who voluntarily leaves his party or defies the party whip. The **91st Amendment (2003)** removed the "split" exception (one-third); only a **merger** with at least two-thirds of members is protected. The **Speaker or Chairman** decides disqualification, subject to judicial review (Kihoto Hollohan, 1992).

**Key amendments.** 1st (1951, Ninth Schedule), 42nd (1976, "mini-Constitution"), 44th (1978, right to property removed from Fundamental Rights), 52nd (1985, anti-defection), 61st (1989, voting age 21 → 18), 73rd and 74th (1992, local bodies), 86th (2002, Article 21A), 101st (2016, GST), 103rd (2019, 10% EWS reservation), 106th (2023, one-third seats for women in Lok Sabha and Assemblies — to apply after the next delimitation).

**RTI Act 2005.** Any citizen can ask a public authority for information; reply within **30 days** (**48 hours** when life or liberty is involved). Appeals go to the Information Commissions.

**RTE.** **Article 21A** (86th Amendment) and the **RTE Act 2009** give free and compulsory education to children aged **6–14**; private schools reserve **25%** of entry-level seats for weaker sections.

**Bihar Right to Public Services Act 2011.** Bihar's law that fixes a **time limit** for services such as caste, income and residence certificates; if an officer delays without reason, the citizen can appeal and the officer can be fined. It is a model of citizen-centred governance.

**The Earth's motions.** **Rotation** (about 24 hours) causes day and night. **Revolution** around the Sun (about 365¼ days) plus the **23.5° tilt** of the axis causes the **seasons**. **Equinoxes** (about 21 March and 23 September) have equal day and night; **solstices** (about 21 June and 22 December) have the longest and shortest days. Indian Standard Time is based on **82.5° E** (near Mirzapur).

**Interior of the Earth.** **Crust → mantle → outer core (liquid) → inner core (solid)**. The **Moho** discontinuity separates crust and mantle; the **Gutenberg** discontinuity separates mantle and core. Scientists learn this from **seismic waves** (P-waves pass through liquids; S-waves do not).

**Rocks.** **Igneous** (from cooled magma — granite, basalt), **sedimentary** (layers — sandstone, limestone; contain fossils), **metamorphic** (changed by heat and pressure — marble from limestone, slate from shale).

**Plate tectonics.** The crust is broken into **plates** that move. **Convergent** boundaries make fold mountains (the Himalaya, as the Indian plate pushes into the Eurasian plate) and earthquakes; **divergent** boundaries form mid-ocean ridges; **transform** boundaries slide past each other. Much of north Bihar lies in high earthquake-risk zones because of this Himalayan collision (check the latest seismic zone map of the Bureau of Indian Standards); the 1934 Bihar–Nepal earthquake is the famous example.

**Atmosphere.** Layers: **troposphere** (weather happens here) → **stratosphere** (ozone layer) → mesosphere → thermosphere (ionosphere) → exosphere. Nitrogen (about 78%) and oxygen (about 21%) are the main gases.

**Winds and cyclones.** Air moves from high to low pressure; the **Coriolis force** turns winds to the right in the northern hemisphere. **Tropical cyclones** form over warm seas (above about 27 °C) and are very destructive near coasts; **temperate cyclones** form where warm and cold air masses meet.

**Oceans.** Salinity averages about **35 parts per thousand**. Currents are driven by winds, temperature and salinity differences and the Earth's rotation. **Tides** are caused mainly by the Moon's pull.

1. Which amendment added the Tenth Schedule?
2. Which amendment lowered the voting age from 21 to 18?
3. Within how many days must an RTI reply normally be given?
4. Which article gives the right to education, and for which age group?
5. In which year did Bihar pass its Right to Public Services Act?
6. What is the tilt of the Earth's axis, and what does it cause?
7. Which discontinuity separates the crust from the mantle?
8. What kind of rock is marble, and what does it form from?
9. In which layer of the atmosphere is the ozone layer?
10. In which direction does the Coriolis force deflect winds in the northern hemisphere?

**Mains question to write in full (300 words):** "Critically examine the anti-defection law in India." Points: purpose (stability, stop "Aaya Ram Gaya Ram"); provisions of the Tenth Schedule; 91st Amendment; Kihoto Hollohan; problems — Speaker's delays and partisanship, curbs on free speech of legislators, mergers used as loopholes; reforms — time limit for decisions, decision by the Election Commission or a tribunal; conclusion.

### Answers

1. The **52nd Amendment (1985)**.
2. The **61st Amendment (1989)**.
3. **30 days** (48 hours where life or liberty is involved).
4. **Article 21A**, for children aged **6–14**.
5. **2011**.
6. About **23.5°**; together with revolution it causes the **seasons** (and the change in day length).
7. The **Mohorovičić (Moho) discontinuity**.
8. **Metamorphic**; formed from **limestone**.
9. The **stratosphere**.
10. To the **right**.
