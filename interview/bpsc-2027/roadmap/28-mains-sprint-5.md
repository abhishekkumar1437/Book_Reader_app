## What you will learn

**Outline method.** For each likely question, write in 5 minutes: a one-line introduction, 6–8 body points as keywords, one Bihar example, one data point, one diagram idea, one-line conclusion. Outlines are faster to revise than full answers and train your planning.

**The final Bihar data card.** Suggested rows (fill with the latest official figures and year): population and density (Census 2011), literacy and sex ratio (Census 2011), GSDP growth and per capita income (Economic Survey), sector shares (Economic Survey), budget size and education share (Budget), fiscal deficit (Budget), panchayat women's reservation (50%, 2006 Act), districts and divisions, forest cover (Forest Survey of India — check latest), key health and nutrition indicators (NFHS — check latest).

**One year of current affairs.** Revise by theme, not by month: polity and governance; economy and schemes; society and social justice; environment and disasters; science and technology; international relations; Bihar. For each theme, list 5–10 items you can quote in answers.

**S&T for Bihar.** Have one page with ten examples: flood forecasting with satellite data, solar pumps and rooftop solar, e-governance services (RTPS), digital land records, telemedicine, crop advisories by mobile, makhana and litchi processing technology, drone use in farming, ethanol plants, DBT for welfare.

**Mains question to write in full (timed, 300 words):** "How can technology transform agriculture in Bihar?" Points: farm profile and challenges; technologies — seeds, soil testing, micro-irrigation, solar pumps, mechanisation through custom hiring, weather and price advisories, drones, cold chain and processing, e-NAM and digital payments; institutions and schemes (check latest); barriers — small holdings, cost, skills, power, credit; way forward — FPOs, extension through KVKs and JEEViKA, women farmers; conclusion with a data point.

### Answers

1. **Introduction line, body keywords (6–8), Bihar example, data point, diagram idea, conclusion line**.
2. They are **quick to revise**, cover **more questions**, and train **planning**, which is the hardest part in the exam.
3. For example: population density — **Census 2011**; GSDP growth — **Bihar Economic Survey**; budget size — **Bihar Budget**; women's reservation in panchayats — **Bihar Panchayati Raj Act 2006**.
4. The **Forest Survey of India** (India State of Forest Report).
5. The **National Family Health Survey (NFHS)**.
6. **Polity and governance; economy and schemes; society and social justice; environment and disasters; science and technology; international relations; Bihar**.
7. Any three of: **RTPS online services, digital land records, DBT for scholarships and pensions, e-office, online grievance systems**.
8. **2006**.
9. **2016**.
10. **Rajendra Prasad** (President of the Assembly) and **Sachchidananda Sinha** (interim President). Check the member list in the polity book before quoting more names.
