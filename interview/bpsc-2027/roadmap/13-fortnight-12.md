## What you will learn

**S&T for Bihar's farms.** Flood-tolerant rice varieties, soil health cards, drip and sprinkler irrigation, mobile weather advisories, drones for spraying, cold storage and food processing for litchi, makhana and vegetables. The **Bihar Agricultural University (Sabour)** and **Dr Rajendra Prasad Central Agricultural University (Pusa)** are the main research centres.

**S&T for floods.** Satellite flood mapping (ISRO's Bhuvan, NRSC), river-level sensors and early warning, embankment monitoring, flood forecasting with Nepal's data, and mobile alerts.

**S&T for health and governance.** Telemedicine, e-Sanjeevani, digital health records, e-governance portals for certificates (RTPS), DBT for scholarships and pensions, online land records.

**Ancient Bihar — the key facts.** Magadha's capitals **Rajgir** and **Pataliputra**; the **Vajji (Licchavi) republic** at Vaishali; the **Buddha** (Bodh Gaya) and **Mahavira** (Kundagrama, Pawapuri); the Mauryas and Guptas; **Nalanda** (UNESCO World Heritage Site since **2016**) and **Vikramshila**; the **Mahabodhi Temple** at Bodh Gaya (UNESCO site since **2002**).

**Medieval Bihar.** Bakhtiyar Khalji's raids; **Sher Shah Suri** of Sasaram; Mughal subah of Bihar with Patna as an important centre; the **Sikh link** — Guru Gobind Singh's birthplace at **Patna Sahib** (Takht Sri Harimandir Ji); Sufi saints at Maner and Bihar Sharif.

**Modern Bihar.** Company rule after **Buxar (1764)**; Permanent Settlement's burden on Bihar's peasants; **Kunwar Singh** and 1857; the **Wahabi movement** with its centre at Patna; separation from Bengal in **1912**.

**Bihar in the freedom struggle.** **Champaran (1917)**; Non-Cooperation with **Bihar Vidyapith** and **Sadaqat Ashram**; Civil Disobedience; **Kisan Sabha** under Swami Sahajanand; **Quit India (1942)** with the Patna Secretariat martyrs and JP's underground work.

**Bihar after independence.** **Sri Krishna Sinha** was Bihar's first Chief Minister and **Anugrah Narayan Sinha** the first Deputy CM and Finance Minister; Bihar pioneered **zamindari abolition** legislation (Bihar Land Reforms Act 1950); the **JP Movement (1974)**; creation of **Jharkhand (15 November 2000)**.

**Bihar's art and festivals.** **Madhubani** (Mithila painting, done mainly by women, natural colours, themes from myth and nature); **Manjusha** (Bhagalpur; the Bihula–Vishahari story); **Tikuli**; **Sujni** embroidery; **Sikki** grass craft. Festivals: **Chhath** (four-day Sun worship with arghya to the setting and rising Sun), **Sama-Chakeva**, **Jitiya**, **Sonepur Mela** (Hariharkshetra; one of Asia's largest cattle fairs).
