Your goal this fortnight is to **finish Round 2 and enter the last 15 days in control**. From Day 287 you take **one full mock every day** and use the rest of the day for rapid revision of one subject from your one-page sheets and mistake log. Nothing new is learned now; the aim is speed, accuracy and calm.

> **In simple words:** Finish the second revision, solve one real past BPSC paper, and then for two weeks: mock in the morning (at exam time if you can), short analysis, then quick revision of one subject. Sleep well and keep reading the newspaper.

Keep the **1-hour newspaper block** daily. Day 294 is the **month-10 revision day** — your **last monthly compilation before Prelims**.

**Rules for the last 15 days.**
- **No new sources.** Only your notes, one-page sheets, quick-revision boxes and the mistake log.
- **Short analysis.** 20 minutes per mock — just log the wrong facts. Deep analysis was for Rounds 1 and 2.
- **Sleep 7–8 hours.** Memory is fixed during sleep. Late nights now cost marks.
- **Keep the newspaper hour,** but only note items likely to be asked (big events, Bihar, schemes, appointments, awards).
- **Practise the OMR.** In every mock, fill an OMR-style sheet with a black or blue ballpoint pen (as your admit card says), including the option-E rule.

## What you will learn

**One-page sheets.** For each subject, one page with the 30–40 facts you keep forgetting. In the last week these sheets replace the books.

**The Bihar facts list.** A final list of 100 Bihar facts: firsts, symbols, districts and places, rivers, protected areas, Ramsar sites, GI tags, schemes with years, leaders and events of the freedom struggle.

**Exam-time training.** Take mocks at the time your Prelims will be held, so your mind is fresh at that hour. Eat and drink the same way you will on exam day.

**The safe-attempt idea.** Do not chase a fixed number of attempts. Attempt every question you are sure of, every question where you can remove two options, and leave (with option E, as per the rules) those where you are blindly guessing. Your mocks tell you your own accuracy rate.

## If you fall behind

- If Round 2 is not complete by Day 285, finish the leftover subjects using only their question-bank "last-week revision" boxes — and still start the last-15-days plan on Day 286.
- If you feel tired, take a half-length mock (75 questions in 60 minutes) instead of a full one, but do not skip the day's mock entirely.
- Never cut sleep to catch up.
