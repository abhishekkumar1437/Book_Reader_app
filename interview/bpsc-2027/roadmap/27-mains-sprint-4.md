## What you will learn

**India–Nepal relations and Bihar.** Open border of about 1,750 km under the **1950 Treaty of Peace and Friendship**; Bihar shares a long stretch of it. Issues: rivers and floods (Kosi and Gandak agreements — the **Kosi Agreement 1954** and **Gandak Agreement 1959**), trade through Raxaul–Birgunj (integrated check post), the Raxaul–Kathmandu railway proposal, cross-border crime, cultural ties (Janakpur–Sitamarhi).

**International themes.** India's neighbourhood policy, Act East, the Global South, G20 legacy, climate negotiations, UN reform, energy security. Use the frame: background → developments → India's interest → Bihar link (if any) → way forward.

**Geography answers.** Always add a **schematic diagram**: Bihar's river system, flood-prone belts, physiographic regions, monsoon winds. Link geography to economy (floods → crop loss → migration).

**Social-sector chapters of the Economic Survey.** Health (hospitals, doctors per population, immunisation), education (enrolment, dropout, learning), women and child development, social welfare. Copy key numbers with year to the data card.

**S&T for development.** Space (remote sensing for crops and floods, communication, navigation), ICT (Digital India, e-governance, UPI), biotechnology (vaccines, GM crops debate), energy (solar, ethanol), health tech (telemedicine). In each answer: technology → how it works in one line → benefit → Bihar example → limits → way forward.

**Data interpretation.** Read the table or graph carefully, compute what is asked (growth, share, average), and write one line of interpretation — examiners give marks for the comment too.

**Mains question to write in full (timed, 300 words):** "Discuss India's relations with Nepal and their importance for Bihar." Points: historical and cultural ties; 1950 treaty and open border; trade and transit (Raxaul–Birgunj ICP); water resources — Kosi and Gandak agreements, flood control and the proposed high dam; energy cooperation; irritants (border map dispute, political shifts); Bihar's stake — floods, trade, migration, tourism (Buddhist and Ramayana circuits); way forward; conclusion.

### Answers

1. The **India–Nepal Treaty of Peace and Friendship, 1950**.
2. **Kosi — 1954**; **Gandak — 1959**.
3. **Raxaul** (East Champaran).
4. **Janakpur** (Nepal), the capital of King Janaka, and **Sitamarhi** (Bihar), linked with the tradition of Sita's birth — both are on the Ramayana circuit.
5. India's **regional satellite navigation system** (Navigation with Indian Constellation), built by ISRO.
6. Collecting information about the Earth from **satellites or aircraft** without touching it; it maps **flooded areas, embankment breaches and crop damage** quickly.
7. Giving **medical advice and treatment from a distance** using phone, video or internet.
8. Any two of: **solar (rooftop, solar pumps, floating solar), ethanol from maize and sugarcane, biomass, small hydro**.
9. **Technology → how it works (one line) → benefit → Bihar example → limits → way forward**.
10. **One line interpreting** what the number shows.
