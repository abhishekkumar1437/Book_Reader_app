## What you will learn

**National movement answers with Bihar.** Prepare one Bihar paragraph for each phase: **Non-Cooperation** (Bihar Vidyapith, Sadaqat Ashram, boycott of courts and colleges), **Civil Disobedience** (salt satyagraha and no-tax campaigns in Bihar), **Kisan Sabha** (Sahajanand, the bakasht struggle), **Quit India** (Patna Secretariat, JP, Azad Dasta), **1946** (interim government — Rajendra Prasad as a minister).

**Polity answers with articles.** Quote the article in every polity answer: President (52–62), PM and ministers (74–75), Parliament (79–122), Supreme Court (124–147), Governor (153–162), CM and ministers (163–164), state legislature (168–212), High Courts (214–231).

**Bihar Budget.** The state's annual financial statement, presented in the Bihar Vidhan Sabha by the Finance Minister around February–March. Note: total size, revenue and capital expenditure, the largest sectors (education is usually the biggest), fiscal deficit as a share of GSDP, and new schemes — each with the budget year.

**Culture answers.** Use a table or diagram: temple styles, Buddhist art, Mughal architecture, Bihar's folk paintings and festivals. For Bihar culture, give region, materials, themes and present status (GI tag, livelihood, government support).

**Panchayati Raj answers.** Structure: constitutional basis (73rd Amendment, Part IX) → Bihar Panchayati Raj Act 2006 → 50% reservation for women and reservation for EBCs → functions and finances → achievements (women leaders, local works) → problems ("mukhiya pati" proxy rule, low own revenue, capacity) → reforms.

**Full essay paper.** Three essays in three hours, one from each section. Spend about 10 minutes planning each and about 50 writing; keep a few minutes to check.

**Mains question to write in full (timed, 300 words):** "Evaluate the role of women representatives in Bihar's panchayats since 2006." Points: 50% reservation; numbers of women heads (cite the latest State Election Commission data with year); positive changes — confidence, focus on water, schools, sanitation, health; JEEViKA links; problems — proxy rule by husbands, low literacy, limited funds and powers; measures — training, SHG networks, digital tools, more fiscal devolution; conclusion.

### Answers

1. **Bihar Vidyapith** and **Sadaqat Ashram** (Mazharul Haque).
2. A peasant struggle (1930s–40s) led by the **Kisan Sabha** to recover **bakasht lands** — lands from which tenants had been evicted by zamindars and which zamindars claimed as their own cultivation.
3. **Article 155** (appointment) and **Article 156** (term).
4. **Article 226**.
5. The **Finance Minister of Bihar**, in the **Bihar Vidhan Sabha**.
6. **Education** (check the latest budget).
7. When an elected woman head's **husband or male relative runs the panchayat** in her name.
8. **Three** essays; about **one hour each** (10 minutes planning, about 50 minutes writing).
9. **Sri Krishna Sinha**; **Anugrah Narayan Sinha**.
10. **Seven students were shot** while trying to hoist the national flag on the Patna Secretariat during the Quit India Movement.
