## What you will learn

**Industrial location.** Industries come up near raw materials (iron and steel near coal and iron ore), markets (consumer goods near cities), power, labour and transport. **National Waterway 1** runs on the Ganga–Bhagirathi–Hooghly system from **Prayagraj to Haldia**, passing through Bihar.

**Census 2011 — India.** Population about **121 crore**; sex ratio **943** females per 1,000 males; literacy **74.04%**; urban share about **31%**. The next census (with caste enumeration) has been announced; note its schedule and results from the news (as of 2026 — check the latest).

**Census 2011 — Bihar.** Population about **10.41 crore** (third largest state then); **highest density in India — 1,106 persons per km²**; sex ratio **918**; literacy **61.8%** (lowest among states); urban share only about **11.3%**.

**Urbanisation.** The share of people living in towns and cities. Bihar's level is low because the economy is mostly farming, there are few large industries, and many people migrate out to work in other states instead of moving to Bihar's own towns.

**National income terms.** **GDP** — value of all final goods and services produced **within** a country in a year. **GNP** — GDP + net income from abroad. **NNP** — GNP − depreciation. **Per capita income** — national income ÷ population. **Factor cost vs market price** — market price includes indirect taxes minus subsidies. **GVA** — gross value added, used for sector-wise output.

**Three methods.** **Production (value added) method**, **income method** (wages + rent + interest + profit), **expenditure method** (consumption + investment + government spending + net exports).

**Planning.** The **Planning Commission** (1950) made Five-Year Plans: the **First Plan (1951–56)** focused on agriculture (Harrod–Domar model); the **Second (1956–61)** on heavy industry (**Mahalanobis model**). **NITI Aayog** replaced it on **1 January 2015** as a think tank promoting **cooperative federalism**, without the power to give funds.

**Money and banking.** Money has functions of medium of exchange, measure of value, store of value. Commercial banks accept deposits and give loans. **14 banks were nationalised in 1969** and **6 more in 1980**.

**The RBI.** India's central bank, set up on **1 April 1935** and **nationalised in 1949**. Its tools: **repo rate** (rate at which the RBI lends to banks), **reverse repo**, **CRR** (cash banks must keep with the RBI), **SLR** (share of deposits banks must keep in liquid assets like government securities), **MSF**, and **open market operations**.

**Inflation.** A general rise in prices. **Demand-pull** (too much money chasing few goods) vs **cost-push** (rising input costs). India uses **CPI** (consumer price index) for its inflation target. The **Monetary Policy Committee** (6 members, headed by the RBI Governor) targets **4% CPI inflation with a band of 2–6%**.

**Mains question to write in full (300 words):** "Explain the instruments of monetary policy used by the RBI. How do they affect a common borrower in Bihar?" Points: quantitative tools (repo, reverse repo, CRR, SLR, MSF, OMO), qualitative tools; transmission — repo up → bank loan rates up → costlier crop, housing and vehicle loans; limits of transmission in Bihar (low credit–deposit ratio, informal moneylenders); conclusion on financial inclusion.

### Answers

1. **1,106 persons per km²** — the **highest** among Indian states.
2. India **943**; Bihar **918** (females per 1,000 males).
3. About **61.8%**.
4. **National Waterway 1**, from **Prayagraj (Allahabad) to Haldia**.
5. GNP = GDP **+ net factor income from abroad** (income earned by citizens abroad minus income earned by foreigners in India).
6. The **Mahalanobis model** (focus on heavy industry).
7. **1 January 2015**.
8. Established **1 April 1935**; nationalised **1 January 1949**.
9. The rate at which the **RBI lends short-term money to banks** against government securities.
10. **4% CPI inflation, with a band of 2–6%**; set by the government, met through decisions of the RBI's **Monetary Policy Committee**.
