## What you will learn

**Statistics in the test.** Expect problems on mean, median, mode, standard deviation, percentages, ratios and averages, and on reading or drawing graphs (check past GS I papers for the pattern). Show every step: formula → substitution → answer with units. Draw graphs with a scale, labelled axes and a title.

**Economy answers for Mains.** Use a fixed frame: concept (one line) → current status (data with year) → issues → government steps (central and Bihar) → way forward. Topics: growth and inflation, agriculture and food security, industry and MSMEs, infrastructure, fiscal federalism and GST, poverty and unemployment, migration.

**Bihar Economic Survey — sector chapters.** For **agriculture**: production of rice, wheat, maize, pulses; makhana, litchi; the **Agriculture Road Maps** (Bihar has run a series of farm road maps since 2008 — check the latest). For **industry**: industrial policy, food processing, ethanol, textiles and leather policies (check the latest). For **infrastructure**: roads, power, urban development.

**National current events.** Choose 15 big themes of the year (for example: a major law, a new scheme, an economic reform, a court judgment, a social issue). For each, write a 6-line outline: background, what happened, issues, impact on Bihar, way forward.

**Bihar schemes for Mains.** For each major scheme, know the aim, start year, key numbers and one criticism. Mark everything "as of 2027 — check the latest".

## End-of-fortnight self-check

1. Write the formula for the mean of grouped data.
2. Find the median of 11, 4, 7, 9, 2.
3. A value rises from 200 to 250. What is the percentage increase?
4. What does a low standard deviation mean?
5. What frame should an economy answer follow?
6. What is "disguised unemployment"? Give a Bihar example.
7. What are the Agriculture Road Maps of Bihar?
8. In which year did Bihar repeal its APMC Act?
9. What share of urban and rural people does the NFSA cover?
10. How should each number on your data card be written?

**Mains question to write in full (timed, 300 words):** "Discuss the challenges and prospects of agro-based and food-processing industries in Bihar." Points: raw-material strength (makhana, litchi, maize, vegetables, milk — COMFED/Sudha); policies (industrial and food-processing incentives — check latest); prospects — jobs, farm income, exports with GI tags; challenges — power, cold chain, credit, land, skills, floods; measures — clusters, FPOs, cold storage, roads, branding; conclusion with a data point from the latest Economic Survey.

### Answers

1. Mean = **Σ(f × x) ÷ Σf** (f = frequency, x = class mid-value).
2. Ordered 2, 4, 7, 9, 11 → **7**.
3. (250 − 200) ÷ 200 × 100 = **25%**.
4. The values are **close to the mean** (little spread).
5. **Concept → current status with data → issues → government steps → way forward**.
6. More people working on a farm than needed, so removing some would not reduce output — e.g. **several family members on a small Bihar landholding**.
7. Multi-year **state plans for agriculture and allied sectors** that Bihar has run since **2008** (first road map 2008–12; later ones followed — check the latest).
8. **2006**.
9. Up to **75% rural** and **50% urban**.
10. With its **source and year**.
