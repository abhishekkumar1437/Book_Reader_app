Your goal this fortnight is to **give your daily newspaper reading a strong static base**. Most current-affairs MCQs are really about a fixed fact behind the news: which body publishes a report, where a grouping's headquarters is, when a convention was signed. By Day 196 you should have one **master current-affairs file** organised by syllabus head, covering about six months.

> **In simple words:** News changes daily, but the background stays the same. This fortnight you learn that background — international bodies, reports and rankings, defence and space, environment treaties, awards and government schemes — so every news item you read later "clicks" into place.

The **1-hour newspaper block** continues daily. On revision days (189, 196) revise the week's CA notes; Day 195 merges all six monthly compilations into one master file.

## What you will learn

**The United Nations.** Founded on **24 October 1945**; headquarters **New York**. The **Security Council** has **15 members — 5 permanent** (USA, UK, France, Russia, China) with veto power, and 10 elected for two years. India seeks a permanent seat. Other bodies: General Assembly, ECOSOC, International Court of Justice (**The Hague**), Secretariat; agencies like WHO (Geneva), UNESCO (Paris), FAO (Rome), ILO (Geneva), UNICEF (New York), UNDP (New York).

**Groupings.** **G20** — 19 countries plus the EU and (since 2023) the **African Union**; India held the presidency in 2023 (New Delhi summit, September 2023). **BRICS** — expanded from five members since 2024 (check the latest list). **SCO** — HQ **Beijing**; India a full member since 2017. **QUAD** — India, USA, Japan, Australia. **ASEAN** — HQ **Jakarta**. **SAARC** — 8 members, HQ **Kathmandu**. **BIMSTEC** — **7 members** around the Bay of Bengal, HQ **Dhaka**; links India's Act East policy with Nepal and Bhutan, Bihar's neighbours.

**Indices and reports.** Learn **who publishes what**: Human Development Index — **UNDP**; World Happiness Report — UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network; Global Hunger Index — Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe; Global Gender Gap — **World Economic Forum**; Ease of Living / SDG India Index — NITI Aayog (SDG India Index); Human Capital Index — World Bank. India's ranks change every year — mark them "check latest".

**Defence and space.** Missiles: **Agni** (long-range ballistic), **Prithvi**, **BrahMos** (supersonic cruise, India–Russia), **Akash** (surface-to-air). Exercises with partner countries have names worth noting (e.g. Malabar — naval, with QUAD partners). Space: Chandrayaan, Aditya-L1, Gaganyaan, NavIC (India's navigation system).

**Environment conventions.** **Ramsar Convention (1971, Ramsar, Iran)** — wetlands. **CITES (1973)** — trade in endangered species. **UNFCCC (1992, Rio Earth Summit)** → **Kyoto Protocol (1997)** → **Paris Agreement (2015, COP21)** — keep warming well below 2 °C. **Montreal Protocol (1987)** — ozone. **Convention on Biological Diversity (1992)**.

**Bihar's Ramsar sites.** **Kanwar Jheel (Kabartal), Begusarai** was Bihar's first Ramsar site (2020). Later additions include **Nagi** and **Nakti** bird sanctuaries (Jamui) and more — check the latest Ramsar list, which is updated often.

**IUCN Red List.** Categories such as Critically Endangered, Endangered, Vulnerable. The **Gangetic dolphin** (India's national aquatic animal) is Endangered; the **Vikramshila Gangetic Dolphin Sanctuary** is near Bhagalpur.

**Awards.** **Bharat Ratna** — highest civilian award; **Karpoori Thakur** (former Bihar CM) received it posthumously (announced in **2024**). Other awards: Padma awards, Jnanpith (literature), Dadasaheb Phalke (cinema), Khel Ratna (sports), Nobel and Booker prizes.

**Scheme tracker.** For each central scheme note: name, ministry, year, aim, beneficiaries, any Bihar data. Examples: **PM-KISAN** (2019; ₹6,000 a year to farmer families in three instalments), **Jal Jeevan Mission** (2019; tap water to rural homes), **PM Awas Yojana**, **Ayushman Bharat** (2018), **PM Ujjwala** (2016). Mark everything "as of 2026 — check the latest".

1. When was the UN founded, and how many permanent members does the Security Council have?
2. Where is the headquarters of BIMSTEC, and how many members does it have?
3. Which organisation publishes the Human Development Index?
4. Where and when was the Ramsar Convention signed?
5. At which COP was the Paris Agreement adopted?
6. Which was Bihar's first Ramsar site, and in which district?
7. Which protocol deals with the ozone layer?
8. Which Bihar leader received the Bharat Ratna posthumously in 2024?
9. Which body joined the G20 as a permanent member in 2023?
10. How much does PM-KISAN give a farmer family per year?

**Mains question to write in full (300 words):** "Discuss the significance of BIMSTEC for India's Act East Policy and for Bihar." Points: members and origin (1997 Bangkok Declaration); India's goals — Bay of Bengal connectivity, trade, security, alternative to stalled SAARC; projects (Kaladan, India–Myanmar–Thailand highway, energy grid); Bihar angle — Nepal and Bhutan links, trade through Raxaul and Jogbani, tourism (Buddhist circuit), electricity trade; challenges; conclusion.

### Answers

1. **24 October 1945**; **5** permanent members.
2. **Dhaka**; **7** members (Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand).
3. **UNDP** (United Nations Development Programme).
4. At **Ramsar, Iran**, in **1971**.
5. **COP21** (Paris, 2015).
6. **Kanwar Jheel (Kabartal)**, **Begusarai**.
7. The **Montreal Protocol (1987)**.
8. **Karpoori Thakur**.
9. The **African Union**.
10. **₹6,000** a year, in three instalments of ₹2,000 (as of 2026 — check the latest).
