Your goal this fortnight is to **finish General Hindi, then switch fully into Prelims mode**. From Day 246 the main block is revision, not new reading; the GMA block becomes speed drills; and you start **full mock tests — two a week**. Each mock is followed by an hour of analysis, which matters as much as the mock itself.

> **In simple words:** First you finish Hindi grammar and writing. Then for the next 55 days you only revise and test: read your notes and quick-revision boxes subject by subject, solve past BPSC questions, take full 2-hour mocks and learn from every mistake.

Keep the **1-hour newspaper block** daily — current affairs keeps changing right up to the exam.

**Mock analysis routine (1 hour after every mock).** (1) Mark the paper and compute the net score (right − wrong ÷ 3). (2) Sort every wrong and every unattempted question into: *did not know*, *knew but confused*, *silly mistake*, *bad guess*. (3) Write each one in the mistake log with the correct fact. (4) Note time taken per section. (5) Write one rule for the next mock ("no guess in science unless two options are eliminated").

## What you will learn

**Paryayvachi, vilom (पर्यायवाची, विलोम).** Synonyms and antonyms. Examples: जल — नीर, पानी, वारि, सलिल; उन्नति × अवनति; आदि × अंत.

**One-word substitution (अनेक शब्दों के लिए एक शब्द).** जो ईश्वर में विश्वास करे — **आस्तिक**; जिसका कोई शत्रु न हो — **अजातशत्रु**.

**Sentence correction (शुद्ध-अशुद्ध).** Common errors in **vachan** (number), **ling** (gender), **karak** (case markers like ने, को, से). Example: "मैंने पुस्तक पढ़ा" → **"मैंने पुस्तक पढ़ी"** (पुस्तक is feminine).

**Muhavare and lokoktiyan.** **आँखों का तारा** — बहुत प्यारा; **नौ दो ग्यारह होना** — भाग जाना; **अधजल गगरी छलकत जाय** — कम ज्ञान वाला अधिक दिखावा करता है.

**Précis (संक्षेपण).** Shorten a passage to about **one-third** of its length in your own words, keeping all main ideas, with a short title.

**Revision Round 1.** A full pass of every subject in about four weeks, from your own notes and the quick-revision boxes — not from the full chapters. Re-read the full chapter only where the mistake log shows repeated errors.

**Past-paper sets.** Solve old BPSC Prelims questions subject by subject (25 at a time). The aim is to learn the **pattern**: statement-based questions, match-the-following, Bihar-specific facts. Download official papers from bpsc.bihar.gov.in.

## End-of-fortnight self-check

1. Give two synonyms of जल.
2. What is the antonym of उन्नति?
3. What is the one word for "जिसका कोई शत्रु न हो"?
4. Correct the sentence: "मैंने पुस्तक पढ़ा".
5. What does "नौ दो ग्यारह होना" mean?
6. To what length should a précis normally be shortened?
7. How many mocks a week in Round 1?
8. What are the four kinds of mistakes you sort in mock analysis?
9. With 1/3 negative marking, when is a guess worth taking?
10. You attempted 110, got 80 right. What is your net score?

**Mains question to write in full (300 words, keep the hand moving):** "Assess the contribution of the Mauryas to Indian administration." Points: central control and the king's council; provinces and princes as governors; officials (adhyakshas); revenue system; spy network; city administration of Pataliputra (Megasthenes' committees); Ashoka's dhamma-mahamattas; legacy for later empires and Bihar's place as the first imperial centre.

### Answers

1. Any two of **नीर, पानी, वारि, सलिल, अम्बु**.
2. **अवनति**.
3. **अजातशत्रु**.
4. **"मैंने पुस्तक पढ़ी।"**
5. **भाग जाना** (to run away).
6. About **one-third** of the original.
7. **Two**.
8. **Did not know, knew but confused, silly mistake, bad guess**.
9. When you can **eliminate at least one or two options** confidently; a blind guess among four (or five) options does not pay on average.
10. Wrong = 30 → 80 − 30/3 = 80 − 10 = **70**.
