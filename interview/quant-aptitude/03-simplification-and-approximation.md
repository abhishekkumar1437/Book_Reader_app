## Squaring tricks

- Numbers ending in 5: n5² = [n × (n + 1)] followed by 25. 65² = (6 × 7) 25 = 4225. 115² = (11 × 12) 25 = 13225.
- Near 50: (50 + k)² = 2500 + 100k + k². 53² = 2500 + 300 + 9 = 2809. 47² = 2500 − 300 + 9 = 2209.
- Near 100: (100 + k)² = 10000 + 200k + k². 104² = 10816. 97² = 10000 − 600 + 9 = 9409.
- Any two-digit: (a + b)² with a the tens. 38² = (40 − 2)² = 1600 − 160 + 4 = 1444.

## Multiplication tricks

- By 11: write the digits with the sums of neighbours between them. 63 × 11: 6, (6 + 3), 3 = 693. 78 × 11: 7, 15, 8 → carry → 858.
- By 25: divide by 4, append 00. 84 × 25 = 21 × 100 = 2100.
- By 125: divide by 8, append 000.
- By 9, 99: multiply by 10 or 100 and subtract once. 47 × 99 = 4700 − 47 = 4653.
- Two numbers near 100: 96 × 97 → deficits 4 and 3 → (96 − 3) then (4 × 3) → 93 12 = 9312.
- Same tens, unit digits summing to 10: 43 × 47 = (4 × 5) then (3 × 7) = 20 21 = 2021.

## Fractions and decimals

- Compare fractions by cross-multiplying, or convert to percentages using the table.
- Recurring decimals: 0.ababab... = ab/99; 0.3777... = (37 − 3)/90 = 34/90 = 17/45.
- Mixed operations: convert everything to improper fractions, cancel before multiplying.

**Example.** Simplify 3½ ÷ 1¾ × 2⅔ − 1.
7/2 ÷ 7/4 = 7/2 × 4/7 = 2. 2 × 8/3 = 16/3. 16/3 − 1 = 13/3 = 4⅓.

**Example.** Which is largest: 5/7, 7/9, 11/15, 13/17?
Percentages: 71.4, 77.8, 73.3, 76.5. Largest 7/9.

## Surds

**Example.** Simplify √50 + √18 − √8.
5√2 + 3√2 − 2√2 = 6√2.

**Example.** Value of 1/(√5 + √3) + 1/(√5 − √3).
Rationalise: (√5 − √3)/2 + (√5 + √3)/2 = √5.

**Example.** If x = 2 + √3, find x + 1/x.
1/x = 1/(2 + √3) = 2 − √3. Sum = 4.

**Example.** √(12 + √(12 + √(12 + ...))) = ?
Let y = √(12 + y) → y² − y − 12 = 0 → (y − 4)(y + 3) = 0 → y = 4.

## Indices

**Example.** If 2^(x+3) = 64, find x. 64 = 2⁶ → x + 3 = 6 → x = 3.

**Example.** Simplify (27)^(2/3) × (16)^(3/4) ÷ (125)^(1/3).
9 × 8 ÷ 5 = 72/5 = 14.4.

**Example.** Which is larger, 2^300 or 3^200?
2^300 = (2³)^100 = 8^100; 3^200 = (3²)^100 = 9^100. So 3^200.

## Approximation for DI and CSAT

Round to two significant figures, compute, then adjust. The options in approximation questions are far apart.

**Example.** 4523 × 0.298 ÷ 61 ≈ ?
4500 × 0.3 = 1350; 1350 / 60 ≈ 22.5. (Exact ≈ 22.1.)

**Example.** 38.7% of 1,249 ≈ ?
40% of 1250 = 500, minus about 1.3% of 1250 (≈ 16) → about 484. (Exact 483.4.)

**Percent of a number by pieces:** 23% of 640 = 20% (128) + 3% (19.2) = 147.2.

## Traps

- Doing subtraction before multiplication because it came first in the line.
- Forgetting that (a − b)² ≠ a² − b².
- Rationalising with the wrong sign.
- Treating "of" as lower priority than ÷ when the setter intends otherwise: when in doubt, the intended reading is that "of" binds tightly.
- Approximation errors compounding: round once, in the direction that keeps the answer inside the options.

## Practice set

1. 36² and 64² without writing multiplication.
2. 87 × 93.
3. 0.4545... as a fraction.
4. Simplify: 2⅕ × 1⅔ ÷ 3⅔.
5. √(0.0169) × 40.
6. (√7 + √5)/(√7 − √5) simplified to the form a + b√35.
7. If x = 3 + 2√2, find x − 1/x.
8. Arrange in ascending order: 3/5, 5/8, 7/11, 9/14.
9. (0.1)² + (0.01)² + (0.001)² = ?
10. 5^(x−2) = 125; find x.
11. Approximate: 1,247 × 0.61 + 3,890 ÷ 9.7.
12. The value of 1/(1×2) + 1/(2×3) + ... + 1/(9×10).

**Answers**

1. 36² = (40 − 4)² = 1600 − 320 + 16 = 1296; 64² = (60 + 4)² = 3600 + 480 + 16 = 4096.
2. Deficits 13 and 7 → (87 − 7) = 80, 13 × 7 = 91 → 8091.
3. 45/99 = 5/11.
4. 11/5 × 5/3 × 3/11 = 1.
5. 0.13 × 40 = 5.2.
6. Multiply by (√7 + √5)/(√7 + √5): (7 + 5 + 2√35)/2 = 6 + √35.
7. 1/x = 3 − 2√2; difference = 4√2.
8. 60%, 62.5%, 63.6%, 64.3% → 3/5 < 5/8 < 7/11 < 9/14.
9. 0.01 + 0.0001 + 0.000001 = 0.010101.
10. 125 = 5³ → x − 2 = 3 → x = 5.
11. ≈ 1250 × 0.6 = 750 (a little more: 760); 3890/9.7 ≈ 400. Total ≈ 1,160 (exact 1,161.7).
12. Telescopes: 1 − 1/10 = 9/10.
