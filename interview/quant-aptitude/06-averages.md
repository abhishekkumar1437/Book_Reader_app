## Where it appears

Every exam, one or two direct questions, plus inside DI. The questions are formulaic: average of a set, a new member changes the average, one wrong entry replaced, average of consecutive numbers, and average speed.

## Formulae

<div class="formula">

| What | Formula |
|---|---|
| Average | sum ÷ count, so sum = average × count |
| Combined average of two groups | (n₁a₁ + n₂a₂) ÷ (n₁ + n₂) · lies between them, nearer the bigger group |
| Alligation on averages | n₁ : n₂ = (a₂ − m) : (m − a₁) |
| Newcomer raises the average by d | newcomer = old average + d × new count |
| Leaver raises the average by d | leaver = old average − d × new count |
| Wrong entry corrected | correct average = wrong average + (correct − wrong) ÷ n |
| Consecutive numbers or any AP | average = (first + last) ÷ 2 = middle term |
| Average speed, equal distances | 2uv ÷ (u + v) |
| Average speed, equal times | (u + v) ÷ 2 |

</div>

## The sum method

**Example.** The average of 8 numbers is 20. If one number is removed, the average becomes 18. Which number was removed?
Sum before 160, after 7 × 18 = 126. Removed = 34.

**Example.** Average age of 30 students is 15. The teacher joins and the average becomes 15.5. Teacher's age?
New sum = 31 × 15.5 = 480.5; old sum = 450 → 30.5. Faster: 15 + 0.5 × 31 = 30.5.

**Example.** The average of 11 results is 50. The first six average 49, the last six 52. The sixth result?
6 × 49 + 6 × 52 − 11 × 50 = 294 + 312 − 550 = 56.

**Example.** A batsman scores 87 in his 17th innings and his average rises by 3. New average?
Let old average a. 16a + 87 = 17(a + 3) → 87 − 51 = a → a = 36; new = 39.

**Example.** The average of 10 numbers was computed as 32, but one entry 46 was read as 64. Correct average?
32 + (46 − 64)/10 = 32 − 1.8 = 30.2.

## The deviation method

Pick a convenient base near the values, add up the deviations, and divide. For 72, 78, 81, 69, 75: base 75, deviations −3, +3, +6, −6, 0 → sum 0 → average 75.

**Hint:** when the numbers are large and close together (marks, temperatures, weights), always use deviations.

## Averages of sequences

**Example.** Average of the first 50 natural numbers = (1 + 50)/2 = 25.5.

**Example.** Average of 5 consecutive odd numbers is 27; the largest? Middle is 27 → 23, 25, 27, 29, 31 → 31.

**Example.** Average of all multiples of 7 between 1 and 100 = (7 + 98)/2 = 52.5.

## Combined groups

**Example.** Class A (40 students) averages 60; class B (60 students) averages 70. Combined?
(40 × 60 + 60 × 70)/100 = (2400 + 4200)/100 = 66.

**Example.** The average weight of a group of 12 is 48 kg. Two people of 42 and 50 kg leave and one of 58 kg joins. New average?
Sum = 576 − 92 + 58 = 542; count 11 → 542/11 ≈ 49.27 kg.

## Average speed

**Example.** A car goes to a town at 60 km/h and returns at 40 km/h. Average speed for the round trip?
2 × 60 × 40/(60 + 40) = 4800/100 = 48 km/h. (Not 50: more time is spent at the slower speed.)

**Example.** A man travels 2 hours at 30 km/h and 3 hours at 40 km/h. Average speed?
Total distance 60 + 120 = 180 over 5 hours = 36 km/h.

## Traps

- "Average increases by 2 when a person joins" means the newcomer exceeds the old average by 2 × the **new** count.
- Average speed over equal distances is the harmonic mean, never the simple mean.
- Replacing a member: change in sum = (new − old member), divided by the unchanged count.
- The average of numbers from 1 to n is (n + 1)/2, not n/2.

## Practice set

1. Average of 15 numbers is 40. Adding two numbers makes the average 42. Sum of the two numbers?
2. The average of five numbers is 27. Removing one makes the average 25. The removed number?
3. Average age of a family of 6 is 22; a child is born and the average becomes 20. Age of the child?
4. Average of 20 results is 30; the first 10 average 24; the last 10?
5. A cricketer's average after 20 innings is 32. He needs what score in the 21st to make the average 35?
6. Average temperature Monday to Wednesday is 37°, Tuesday to Thursday 34°; Thursday is 30°. Monday?
7. Average of 7 consecutive even numbers is 24. The smallest?
8. A student's average in 4 subjects is 68; in the fifth he scores 83. New average?
9. The average of 9 numbers is 14; the average of the first four is 12 and of the last four is 15. The fifth number?
10. A train goes 120 km at 40 km/h and returns at 60 km/h. Average speed?
11. The average salary of 20 workers is ₹1,500 and the manager's salary raises the average by ₹100. Manager's salary?
12. The average of x, x + 2, x + 4, x + 6, x + 8 is 15. Find x.

**Answers**

1. 17 × 42 − 15 × 40 = 714 − 600 = 114.
2. 135 − 100 = 35.
3. 7 × 20 − 6 × 22 = 140 − 132 = 8.
4. (600 − 240)/10 = 36.
5. 21 × 35 − 20 × 32 = 735 − 640 = 95.
6. M + T + W = 111; T + W + Th = 102 → M − Th = 9 → M = 39°.
7. Middle (4th) is 24 → smallest = 24 − 6 = 18.
8. (272 + 83)/5 = 71.
9. 126 − 48 − 60 = 18.
10. 2 × 40 × 60/100 = 48 km/h.
11. 1500 + 100 × 21 = ₹3,600.
12. Middle term x + 4 = 15 → x = 11.
