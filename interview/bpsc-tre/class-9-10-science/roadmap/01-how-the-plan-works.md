This plan starts on **Saturday 24 October 2026** (Day 1) and ends on **Thursday 21 January 2027** (Day 90). The exam date is not announced yet; it is expected between **December 2026 and January 2027**. The plan is built so that you finish the full syllabus once by **Day 60 (22 December)** — which keeps you safe even if the exam comes early — and then use 30 days for revision and mocks.

> **In simple words:** Days 1–60: learn every chapter once, one science chapter (or half) per day, plus 1.5 hours of GS/language and 1 hour of revision. Every 7th day is a review day. Days 61–90: revise everything a second time and take two full mocks a week. If the exam is announced earlier, use Section 6 to squeeze the plan.

## 2. The daily timetable (about 6 hours)

| Slot | Time | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Science slot** | 3.5 h (e.g. 6:00–9:30 am, with a 10-minute break each hour) | The chapter of the day: read, make one-line notes, solve the chapter's *Practice with answers* |
| **GS / Language slot** | 1.5 h (e.g. 4:00–5:30 pm) | 20 min current affairs + one GS chapter, a maths/reasoning topic or a language topic (rotating; see below) |
| **Revision slot** | 1 h (e.g. 8:30–9:30 pm) | Spaced revision of earlier chapters (+1, +3, +21 days) and the error log |

If you have more time on some days (holidays), add it to the science slot — never skip the revision slot to "save time".

> **Remember:** The day's science chapter is done only when you have solved its *Practice with answers* and written every wrong answer into your error log. Reading alone is not "done".

### The GS / Language rotation (Days 1–60)

Each week has six study days. The GS slot rotates like this:

| Study day of the week | GS / Language topic |
|---|---|
| 1st | Maths (quantitative aptitude) |
| 2nd | History (ancient → national movement) |
| 3rd | Reasoning (mental ability) |
| 4th | Geography or Polity (alternate weeks) |
| 5th | Bihar special or Economy |
| 6th | Language (English and Hindi on alternate weeks) |
| 7th day | Weekly review: current affairs of the week + one 30-question language set |

Why this rotation: TRE GS has about 8 maths and 8 science questions, then history (especially the national movement and Bihar), geography, Bihar and current affairs — see [Language and General Studies](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/exam-guide/language-and-general-studies). General science is already covered by your science slot.

## 3. The science split — about 30% each

The real TRE papers split the 80 science questions almost exactly one-third each (TRE 1.0 + 2.0: Physics 54, Chemistry 55, Biology 51 — see [Syllabus Decoded and PYQ Blueprint](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/exam-guide/syllabus-decoded-and-pyq-blueprint)). So the 52 science days of Days 1–60 are shared equally:

| Subject | Science days (Days 1–60) | Chapters given two days | Chapters given one day |
|---|---|---|---|
| Physics | 17 | Heat–thermodynamics, ray optics, electricity, magnetism–EMI–AC, modern physics | Units, kinematics, work–energy, gravitation, waves, wave optics, electronics |
| Chemistry | 17 | Bonding, redox–electrochemistry–kinetics, d/f-block and coordination, organic basics, hydrocarbons and functional groups | Basic concepts, atomic structure, periodicity, thermodynamics–equilibrium, solid state, s/p-block, polymers–biomolecules |
| Biology | 18 | Cell and biomolecules, genetics, animal kingdom, both human-physiology chapters | Evolution, classification, plant kingdom, morphology, physiology, health, ecology, biotechnology |

The subjects rotate **Biology → Physics → Chemistry** every day, so no subject is untouched for more than two days. The two-day chapters are the ones with the most PYQ questions or the most content.

> **Exam angle:** The top PYQ chapters — Redox–Electrochemistry–Kinetics (12 questions in two papers), Magnetism–EMI–AC (11), Coordination, Organic functional groups, Polymers–biomolecules, Electronics and Cell–biomolecules (8 each) — all get extra time either in Phase 1–2 or in the Phase 3 second pass.

## 4. Spaced revision and the 7th-day review

The **Revision** column of every day names the exact chapters to revise, following the 1–3–7–21 rule from [Books, Notes and Revision](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/exam-guide/books-notes-and-revision):

| Label in the table | Meaning | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **+1 recall** | Yesterday's science chapter: read your notes and its *Quick revision* | 15 min |
| **+3 practice** | The chapter from three study-days ago: redo its *Practice with answers* without looking | 20 min |
| **+21 traps** | The chapter from about three weeks ago: re-read its *Traps and exceptions* and your error-log entries | 15 min |
| (rest) | Error log, units/formula pages | 10 min |

**Every 7th day (Days 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 49, 56) is a review day:** redo the practice questions of the week's six chapters, take a 40-question mixed test scored with +1/−1/3, revise the week's current affairs, do one 30-question language set, and count your error-log tags.

| Days 68–74 | Day 70 (TRE 1.0 science + language + GS), Day 74 | Day 73 |
| Days 75–81 | Day 77, Day 81 | Day 80 |
| Days 82–88 | Day 84, Day 88 (last full mock) | Day 87 |
| Days 89–90 | Light revision only | — |

Download the official TRE question papers and final answer keys from [bpsc.bihar.gov.in](https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in). For the other mocks use the app's Quiz tab where sets are available, or one good test series. **Always** score with +1 / −1/3, darken E for skips, and analyse the same day.

## 6. If the exam is announced earlier — how to compress

The moment BPSC announces dates, count the days left to the **Mains** (and separately to the **Prelims**). Then use the row that fits:

| Days left to Mains when announced | What to do |
|---|---|
| **80–90** | Follow the plan; Days 89–90 become the last-week routine. |
| **60–79** | Keep Days 1–60 as they are but combine the two halves of low-weight two-day chapters (Physics heat, ray optics; Biology physiology) into one day each. Shorten Phase 3 to the remaining days with **one** full mock every 3 days. |
| **45–59** | From today, do **two science slots a day** (2 h + 2 h) and cut GS to 1 h: one high-weight chapter and one low-weight chapter per day. Give the 7th-day review only 3 hours. Keep at least 10 days for Phase 3 with 4 full mocks. |
| **30–44** | Study only the remaining **high-weight** chapters in full (list below); for the rest read *Quick revision*, *Traps and exceptions* and *Practice with answers* only. Last 10 days: question banks + 3 full mocks. |
| **under 30** | Stop new chapters. Use the three question-bank chapters, the *Quick revision* sections and your error log; take one full mock every 3 days. |

**Never cut these (high PYQ weight):** Physics — [Magnetism, EMI and AC](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/physics/magnetism-emi-and-ac), [Electronics and Communication](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/physics/electronics-and-communication), [Modern Physics](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/physics/modern-physics-atoms-and-nuclei), [Heat, Thermodynamics and Kinetic Theory](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/physics/heat-thermodynamics-and-kinetic-theory). Chemistry — [Redox, Electrochemistry and Kinetics](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/chemistry/redox-electrochemistry-and-kinetics), [d-, f-Block and Coordination Compounds](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/chemistry/d-f-block-and-coordination-compounds), [Hydrocarbons and Functional Groups](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/chemistry/hydrocarbons-and-functional-groups), [Polymers, Biomolecules and Applied Chemistry](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/chemistry/polymers-biomolecules-and-applied-chemistry). Biology — [Cell Structure and Biomolecules](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/biology/cell-structure-and-biomolecules), [Cell Division, Genetics and Molecular Biology](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/biology/cell-division-genetics-and-molecular-biology), [Animal Kingdom](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/biology/animal-kingdom), [Plant Kingdom and Economic Botany](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/biology/plant-kingdom-and-economic-botany), [Health, Disease and Immunity](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/biology/health-disease-and-immunity).

**Cut first (fewest PYQs so far):** [Work, Energy, Power and Rotation](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/physics/work-energy-power-and-rotation) B.Sc. parts, [Thermodynamics, Equilibrium, Acids and Bases](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/chemistry/thermodynamics-equilibrium-acids-and-bases) derivations, [Evolution and Origin of Life](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/biology/evolution-and-origin-of-life) details, [Biotechnology and Applied Biology](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/biology/biotechnology-and-applied-biology) techniques. Even then, read their *Quick revision* — TRE 3.0 asked basic Class 9 questions from "light" areas.

### If the Prelims comes first with little gap

The Prelims is pure GS. If its date is announced and it is **less than 4 weeks away**:

| Period | Science slot | GS slot | Revision slot |
|---|---|---|---|
| Until 3 weeks before Prelims | Plan as usual | 1.5 h | 1 h |
| Last 3 weeks before Prelims | **2.5 h** (continue the plan at a slower pace, or revise) | **3 h**: GS question banks, Bihar, current affairs, one Prelims GS mock every 4 days | 0.5 h |
| After Prelims | Resume the plan; use the extra Phase-3 days to catch up | Back to 1.5 h | 1 h |

> **Common mistake:** Dropping science completely for the Prelims. Two weeks without science means forgetting a large part of Phase 1. Keep at least 2.5 h of science every day.

## 7. If you start late or miss days

- **Start date later than 24 Oct:** keep the order of the days; just ignore the dates. Use Section 6 to see if you need to compress.
- **Missed one day:** do the missed science chapter on the next 7th-day review day (do the review in the evening).
- **Missed several days:** do not try to "double up" for a week. Move to the compression row for the days left.
- **Fell ill:** on low-energy days do only the Revision column — it keeps memory alive.

## Quick revision

- Day 1 = Sat 24 Oct 2026; Day 60 = Tue 22 Dec 2026; Day 90 = Thu 21 Jan 2027.
- Days 1–60: every chapter once; Days 61–90: second pass + mocks.
- Daily: science 3.5 h + GS/language 1.5 h + revision 1 h.
- Science days: Physics 17, Chemistry 17, Biology 18 — about 30% each.
- Rotation Biology → Physics → Chemistry.
- Revision column: +1 recall, +3 practice, +21 traps.
- Every 7th day is a review day with a 40-Q test and a language set.
- Phase 3: 8 full Mains mocks + 4 Prelims GS mocks.
- Compress by days left: 60–79, 45–59, 30–44, under 30 — never cut the high-weight chapters.
- Prelims less than 4 weeks away → GS 3 h, science 2.5 h.
