## Chapter 1 — Cell Structure and Biomolecules

1. ⭐ Who coined the term "cell"? — **Robert Hooke (1665)** (dead cork cells).
2. Who proposed the cell theory? — **Schleiden (1838) and Schwann (1839)**; Virchow added "omnis cellula e cellula" (1855).
3. Smallest known cell? — **Mycoplasma (PPLO)**.
4. ⭐ Functional unit of protein synthesis? — **Polysome** (many ribosomes on one mRNA).
5. ⭐ Main function of ribosomes? — **Protein synthesis**.
6. Ribosome type in prokaryotes? — **70S** (50S + 30S); eukaryotes **80S**.
7. ⭐ Who proposed the fluid mosaic model? — **Singer and Nicolson (1972)**; membrane = lipids + proteins.
8. ⭐ Active transport moves substances how? — **Against the concentration gradient using ATP**.
9. "Suicidal bags" of the cell? — **Lysosomes** (discovered by de Duve).
10. ⭐ Where is DNA mainly found? — **Nucleus**.
11. Structure of cilia and flagella axoneme? — **9 + 2** microtubules (centriole 9 triplets).
12. ⭐ Basic amino acids? — **Lysine, arginine, histidine**.
13. ⭐ Most abundant protein in animals? — **Collagen**; in the biosphere — **RuBisCO**.
14. ⭐ Basic unit of nucleic acid? — **Nucleotide** (base + sugar + phosphate).
15. Purines? — **Adenine and guanine**; pyrimidines — cytosine, thymine, uracil.
16. ⭐ Element absent in carbohydrates but present in chlorophyll and RNA? — **Nitrogen**.
17. Haemoglobin is what kind of conjugated protein? — **Chromoprotein**; casein — phosphoprotein.
18. Who gave the lock-and-key model of enzyme action? — **Emil Fischer**; induced fit — Koshland.

## Chapter 2 — Cell Division, Genetics and Molecular Biology

19. ⭐ Diploid chromosome number in humans? — **46** (23 pairs).
20. In which phase does DNA replicate? — **S phase** of interphase.
21. Which stage is best for counting chromosomes? — **Metaphase**.
22. Crossing over occurs in which sub-stage? — **Pachytene** of prophase I.
23. ⭐ Mendel's laws are based on which reproduction? — **Sexual reproduction** (crosses of pea plants).
24. ⭐ Dihybrid F₂ ratio? — **9 : 3 : 3 : 1**.
25. Ratio in incomplete dominance (snapdragon) F₂? — **1 : 2 : 1** (same for genotype and phenotype).
26. ⭐ ABO blood groups show? — **Codominance and multiple alleles**.
27. Who discovered linkage in *Drosophila*? — **T.H. Morgan**.
28. ⭐ Sickle-cell anaemia mutation? — **Glutamic acid → valine at the 6th position of the β-chain**.
29. Down's syndrome is due to? — **Trisomy 21**.
30. Who proved semi-conservative replication? — **Meselson and Stahl (1958)**, using ¹⁵N.
31. How many codons code for amino acids? — **61** of 64 (3 stop codons).
32. Start codon? — **AUG** (methionine).
33. Who proposed the lac operon? — **Jacob and Monod**.
34. Who developed DNA fingerprinting? — **Alec Jeffreys**; in India — Lalji Singh (CCMB).

37. Who disproved spontaneous generation with swan-neck flasks? — **Louis Pasteur**.

39. Forelimbs of whale, bat and man are? — **Homologous organs** (divergent evolution).
40. Wings of insect and bird are? — **Analogous organs** (convergent evolution).

42. Who coined "survival of the fittest"? — **Herbert Spencer**.
43. Mutation theory was given by? — **Hugo de Vries** (*Oenothera*).

45. Hardy–Weinberg equation? — **p² + 2pq + q² = 1**.

## Attempt strategy for biology under 1/3 negative marking

TRE 4.0 Mains (as reported from the notice — verify with the official notice) has **1/3 mark negative per wrong answer** and **option (E) = "question not attempted"**, which must be darkened to skip; leaving all five circles blank also costs 1/3.

**Expected-value arithmetic** (assuming four real answer options A–D):

| Situation | Chance correct | Expected marks per guess |
|---|---|---|
| Pure guess among 4 | 1/4 | 1/4 − (3/4)(1/3) = **0** (break-even, no gain) |
| One option surely eliminated (guess among 3) | 1/3 | 1/3 − (2/3)(1/3) = **+0.11** |
| Two options eliminated (guess between 2) | 1/2 | 1/2 − (1/2)(1/3) = **+0.33** |

**Rules for biology:**
1. **Biology is the fastest-scoring part** of the science section — one-line facts with no calculation. Finish it first or second and aim for **85–90% accuracy** here.
2. **Attempt if you can eliminate at least one option.** If you cannot eliminate any, darken **(E)** and move on.
3. **Watch spellings and single words**: renin/rennin, "not", "incorrect", "majority", "mainly". Two TRE 2.0 biology answers were "None of the above" because of one word.
4. **Statement-type questions** (e.g. "(i) and (iii) only"): check the statement you are surest about first and strike out every option that disagrees.
5. **Do not change answers** without a clear reason; first instincts on recall facts are usually right.
6. **Spend no more than 30–40 seconds** on a biology recall question; mark and return later if needed.
7. **Bihar-linked biology** (kala-azar, AES, arsenic, Valmiki, Vikramshila, makhana, litchi, tasar silk) is easy marks — revise it from [Ecology](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/biology/ecology-environment-and-biodiversity) and the BPSC shelf's [Bihar natural resources](/interview/bpsc-2027/bihar-special/agriculture-minerals-and-natural-resources-of-bihar).

## Quick revision

1. Revise the **TRE 2.0 table** above until you can answer all 25 without looking.
2. Starred (⭐) questions = the core list; there are about 70 of them.
3. Most repeated biology themes in TRE: **vectors and diseases**, **hormones and their chemistry**, **larvae and parasites**, **biomolecules (protein types)**, **economic botany (fibres, oils, agar)**, **ecology (GHG, BOD, ozone)**, **biotech terms (somaclone, eugenics)**.
4. Every chapter's "Traps and exceptions" list is worth one more reading in the last three days.
5. Under negative marking, the best biology score comes from **high accuracy**, not from attempting everything.
