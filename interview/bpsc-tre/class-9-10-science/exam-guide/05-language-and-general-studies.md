Science gives you 80 of the 120 merit marks, but two other things decide whether those 80 marks ever count: the **Prelims GS screening** (you must clear it to sit the Mains) and **Part I Language** (you must score at least 30%). Part II GS then adds 40 marks to your merit. This chapter tells you exactly what to study for each, and links to the chapters on this shelf that teach it.

> **In simple words:** Language = pass it safely with only sure answers. GS = 40 merit marks + the whole Prelims; give it 1.5 hours every day. A science graduate already knows the general-science part, so spend GS time on history, geography, polity, Bihar, current affairs and quick maths.

## 1. Part I — Language (30 questions, qualifying)

### 1.1 What it is

- 30 MCQs in the Mains; **qualifying only** — you need **30%**, i.e. **9 net marks** out of 30.
- Marks are **not** added to merit.
- In TRE 2.0 (Class 9–10) the section had English questions followed by Hindi questions (roughly half and half). Check the TRE 4.0 notice for whether the Indian-language half can be Hindi, Urdu or Bangla.
- Level: **Class 6–10**. TRE 2.0 asked articles, "which is not a river", days of the week, months, household items and family words.

> **PYQ:** TRE 2.0 (2023) Q5 asked: "Today is Sunday. The day before yesterday was (A) Tuesday (B) Friday (C) Thursday". **Official answer: B.** Yesterday = Saturday, the day before = Friday.

> **PYQ:** TRE 2.0 (2023) Q7 asked: "Husband's brother is (A) cousin (B) son (C) brother-in-law". **Official answer: C.**

> **PYQ:** TRE 2.0 (2023) Q2 asked: "I have a friend in Mumbai who is ___ actress. (A) a (B) an (C) the". **Official answer: B** — *an* before a vowel **sound** ("actress" starts with the sound /æ/).

### 1.2 English topics (Class-10 grammar)

| Topic | What to know | Typical question |
|---|---|---|
| Articles | a/an by sound (an hour, a university, an MLA), *the* for specific/unique things, superlatives, rivers, oceans | "In this school, all ___ students are given free education." → *the* (TRE 2.0 Q1, official C) |
| Tenses | 12 tenses, *since/for*, present perfect vs past simple | Fill in the blank |
| Subject–verb agreement | each/every/neither + singular; "one of the + plural noun + singular verb" | Spot the error |
| Prepositions | in/on/at (time, place), *between/among*, *by/with*, fixed prepositions (*fond of, afraid of, good at*) | Fill in |
| Conjunctions | *although/but*, *unless*, *no sooner…than*, *hardly…when* | Fill in / error |
| Voice and narration | Active ↔ passive; direct ↔ indirect speech (tense backshift) | Choose the correct form |
| Vocabulary | Synonyms, antonyms, one-word substitution, idioms (common ones), spelling | Choose the meaning |
| Everyday words | Days, months, relations (maternal/paternal, in-laws), household items, odd one out | TRE 2.0 Q3–Q8 |
| Comprehension | Short passage, direct questions | Answer from the passage |

> **Memory trick:** Relations: **maternal** = mother's side, **paternal** = father's side. Husband's/wife's brother and sister's husband are all **brother-in-law**.

### 1.4 Qualifying strategy under negative marking

1. Do Part I **first or last, but never in a hurry**. It takes 10–15 minutes.
2. Mark only answers you are **sure** of. You need just 9 net. With 15 sure answers and 0 wrong you are safely through.
3. Darken **E** for every doubtful question. A wrong answer costs 1/3, and here an extra mark gives you nothing (it is qualifying).
4. Example: 14 right, 3 wrong, 13 E → 14 − 1 = **13** (43%) ✓. Example: 11 right, 9 wrong → 11 − 3 = **8** ✗ — failed by greed.

> **Common mistake:** Practising nothing for language because "it is easy". Under negative marking, two or three careless errors plus a few doubtful attempts can push you below 9. Do one 30-question language set every week (the [90-Day Plan](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/roadmap/how-the-plan-works) has a language slot).

## 2. Part II — General Studies in the Mains (40 questions)

### 2.1 What was actually asked

| Area | TRE 1.0 (GS Q1–40) | TRE 2.0 (GS Q31–70) | Examples from the papers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arithmetic / mental ability | 8 (Q1–8) | 8 (Q31–38) | Ages ratio, average income, percentages, discount, ratio of numbers (TRE 1.0); bats and balls equations, profit % (615 → 820 = 33.33%), ATM PIN counting (6 × 6 × 10 × 2 = 720), triangle area, mean shift, quadratic with roots −1 and 4 → x² − 3x − 4 = 0 (TRE 2.0) |
| General science | 8 (Q9–16) | 8 (Q39–46) | Critical mass, gases, edible part of litchi, bryophyte spore mother cell (TRE 1.0); Aditya-L1 at L1, ginger is a stem, lens in water, fog, c/137, Stefan's law (TRE 2.0) |
| Current affairs | 8 (Q17–24) | 5 (Q47–51) | NGT year, G20 events, International Booker winners (TRE 1.0); new Parliament architect (Bimal Patel), African Union president (Comoros), Human Rights Day 10 Dec (TRE 2.0) |
| Geography (India + Bihar) | 8 (Q25–32) | about 7 | Char/Kankar/Kayal/Mand match, Likhapani glacier, Magadha and Anga plains, Kosi agreement 1954, seaports order (TRE 1.0); black soil, sedimentary rocks, East–West Corridor (Silchar–Porbandar), stalactites (underground water), Survey of India (TRE 2.0) |
| History and national movement (with Bihar) | 8 (Q33–40) | about 10 | 1857 descriptions, Rajendra Prasad's portfolio, "Day of Deliverance", Bihar National College (TRE 1.0); INA founder (Mohan Singh), Tana Bhagat (tribal), Savarkar and 1857, Anandamath (Sannyasi revolt), Gaya Congress 1922 (C.R. Das), Swami Vidyanand and Bihar peasants, Hindu College 1817 (TRE 2.0) |
| Bihar culture / days / environment | spread above | about 2 | Raksha Bandhan as "Tree Safety Day" in Bihar, World Earth Day 22 April (TRE 2.0) |

> **Exam angle:** About **16 of 40** GS questions (maths + science) are already "home ground" for a science graduate. The other 24 come from history (especially the **national movement and Bihar**), Indian geography, Bihar facts and current affairs. Polity and economy were light in these two papers but are part of the Prelims syllabus.

> **PYQ:** TRE 2.0 (2023) Q32 asked: "A shopkeeper purchases an item at ₹615 and sells it at ₹820. What is his percentage profit? (A) 25% (B) 33.33% (C) 35.50%". **Official answer: B.** Profit = 205; 205 ÷ 615 = 1/3 = 33.33%.

> **PYQ:** TRE 2.0 (2023) Q33 asked: "A hacker finds that the ATM PIN of a person has first two digits ranging from 3 to 8, and the fourth digit is either 0 or 9. How many attempts must the hacker try in order to decode the PIN? (A) 48 (B) 24 (C) 720". **Official answer: C.** First digit: 6 choices (3–8), second: 6, third: 10 (any), fourth: 2 → 6 × 6 × 10 × 2 = 720.

## 3. The Prelims GS screening (150 questions)

The Prelims covers the same areas as Part II, but with 150 questions in 2 hours — so it needs more depth, especially in history, polity, geography, economy, Bihar and current affairs. Mental ability (reasoning plus arithmetic) is also included.

## 4. How much time to give

| Period | GS + Language per day | Main focus |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–60 of the plan | 1.5 h | One GS chapter or maths/reasoning topic per day + 20 min current affairs; one language session a week |
| Days 61–90 | 1.5–2 h | GS question banks, current-affairs revision, one Prelims-pattern GS mock per week |
| If the Prelims date is announced before the Mains | 3 h for the last 3 weeks before Prelims | Shift science time to GS temporarily; see [How the Plan Works](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/roadmap/how-the-plan-works) |

> **Exam angle:** Daily **20 minutes of current affairs** (one newspaper or one monthly magazine page per day) is more effective than a 10-hour crash course at the end. Write 5 lines a day in a current-affairs notebook under fixed heads: national, Bihar, international, science and tech, sports and awards, days and reports.

## Quick revision

- Language: 30 Q, qualifying, need 9 net (30%); mark only sure answers.
- TRE 2.0 language level: articles, days, months, relations, odd one out.
- English: articles, tenses, agreement, prepositions, conjunctions, voice, narration, vocabulary.
- Hindi: sandhi, samas, upsarg–pratyay, paryayvachi–vilom, shuddh–ashuddh, muhavare.
- GS Part II: 40 Q; about 8 maths + 8 science + 5–8 current affairs + rest history/geography/Bihar.
- TRE GS history leans to the national movement and Bihar.
- Prelims: 150 GS Q in 2 h; screening only but compulsory to clear.
- Maths: Class 8–10 level — ratio, percent, profit, average, speed, algebra, geometry, counting.
- 20 minutes of current affairs every day; last 12 months.
