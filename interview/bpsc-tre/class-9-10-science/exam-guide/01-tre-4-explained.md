This chapter gives you the full picture of the exam you are preparing for: the advertisement, the post, the eligibility rules, the stages and the pay. It also explains what is **new** in TRE 4.0 compared with the three earlier Teacher Recruitment Exams (TRE 1.0, 2.0 and 3.0), because the old papers are your best practice material but the old rules no longer apply.

> **In simple words:** TRE 4.0 is the Bihar government's exam to hire school teachers through BPSC. You are applying for **Secondary Teacher (Class 9–10), Science**. You must have a science degree, a B.Ed and a pass in **Bihar STET Paper-I (Science)**. There is a GS screening test first, then a main test of 150 MCQs where 80 questions are pure science. Wrong answers now cost **1/3 mark**.

> **Remember:** Everything below was collected on **23 October 2026** from the BPSC notice as reported by news and coaching websites. Some numbers differ between sources. Before you rely on any number — especially vacancies, age limits, qualifying marks and the exam date — **check the official notice on [bpsc.bihar.gov.in](https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in)**.

> **Exam angle:** About 463 seats for one subject across the whole state means you are competing with every science B.Ed + STET holder in Bihar. The difference between selection and non-selection is usually **3–6 marks** in the science part. That is why this collection focuses on accuracy and on not losing marks to negative marking.

> **Common mistake:** Reading "33,320 posts" and assuming the competition is easy. The large number is spread over Classes 1–5, 6–8, 9–10 and 11–12 and over many subjects. Only your subject's number matters.

## 2. Who can apply (Class 9–10 Science)

### 2.1 Educational qualification

| Requirement | Detail (as reported) |
|---|---|
| Degree | Graduation **or** post-graduation in science with at least **50% marks**. The subject combination must match the "Science" teacher rules in the notice (usually Physics, Chemistry and Botany/Zoology/Biology at graduation level). |
| Relaxation in degree marks | **5%** for SC/ST/EBC/BC/women/PwD (so 45%) — verify the exact categories |
| Teacher training | **B.Ed**, or a 4-year integrated **B.Sc.Ed / B.A.Ed** |
| Eligibility test | **Bihar STET Paper-I (Secondary) in Science** — passed |

> **Remember:** For the secondary level the eligibility test is **Bihar STET Paper-I**, not CTET. CTET covers only Classes 1–8. STET Paper-II is for Classes 11–12. If your STET subject is not Science, you cannot apply for the Science post even with a B.Sc.

> **In simple words:** Degree in science (50%, or 45% with relaxation) + B.Ed + STET Paper-I Science = eligible.

### 2.2 Age limit

Age is counted on **1 August 2026** (as reported).

| Category | Minimum age | Maximum age (reported) |
|---|---|---|
| Unreserved (UR) male | 21 | 37 |
| UR female | 21 | 40 |
| BC and EBC (male and female) | 21 | 40 |
| SC and ST (male and female) | 21 | 42 |
| PwD | 21 | usually an extra 10 years over the category limit — **verify** |

> **Common mistake:** Some coaching websites print limits that differ by one or two years, and some quote the age-reckoning date from TRE 3.0. Only the advertisement decides. If you are close to the upper limit, read the age clause of Advt. 15/2026 word by word.

### 2.3 Domicile and reservation

Since 2025 the Bihar government has announced a **domicile preference** for teacher recruitment (benefits linked to being a resident of Bihar who studied in Bihar). Reservation for women (35%) and other reserved categories is available only to Bihar residents. The exact rules for TRE 4.0 — who counts as a domicile, and whether non-residents can apply in the unreserved category — are written in the notice. **Read that clause yourself**; coaching sites have reported it differently.

## 3. The two stages of TRE 4.0

| | Stage 1: Preliminary (screening) | Stage 2: Mains |
|---|---|---|
| Questions | 150 MCQs | 150 MCQs |
| Marks | 150 | 150 |
| Time | 2 hours | 2 hours 30 minutes |
| Content | General Studies only: general science, current affairs, history, geography, polity, economy, mental ability, Bihar | Part I Language (30 Q, **qualifying, minimum 30%**) · Part II General Studies (40 Q) · Part III **Science (80 Q)** |
| Counted in merit? | **No** — only to shortlist for Mains | **Yes** — Part II + Part III = **120 marks** decide the merit |
| Negative marking | 1/3 mark per wrong answer | 1/3 mark per wrong answer |

The full marking rules, qualifying marks and worked score examples are in [Exam Pattern and Marking](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/exam-guide/exam-pattern-and-marking).

> **Exam angle:** Your final rank depends on 120 marks, and **80 of those are science**. Science is two-thirds of your merit. That is why three of the five books in this collection are Physics, Chemistry and Biology.

> **Remember:** The Prelims is "only screening", but if you fail it you never reach the science paper. Do not ignore GS. A daily 1.5-hour GS/language slot is built into the [90-Day Plan](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/roadmap/how-the-plan-works).

## 4. Salary and service

| Item | Reported figure |
|---|---|
| Basic pay (Secondary Teacher, Class 9–10) | **₹31,000** per month |
| Allowances | DA, HRA, medical and others as per Bihar government rules |
| Approximate in-hand at joining | **₹55,000 – ₹60,000** per month (depends on DA rate and posting place) |
| Status | Teacher appointed by the state ("Vidyalaya Adhyapak") under the Bihar school teacher rules |

> **Common mistake:** In-hand salary numbers on YouTube often mix different DA rates. Use the basic pay (₹31,000) as the fixed fact and treat the in-hand number as approximate.

## 5. What changed from TRE 1.0, 2.0 and 3.0

| Feature | TRE 1.0 (Aug 2023) | TRE 2.0 (Dec 2023) | TRE 3.0 (Jul 2024) | TRE 4.0 (2026–27, reported) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stages | One written exam | One written exam | One written exam | **Two: Prelims (GS screening) + Mains** |
| Class 9–10 paper | Language paper (qualifying, separate) + Paper "GS and Science": 120 Q, 120 marks, 2 h (GS 40 + Science 80) | One paper: 150 Q, 2 h 30 min (Language 30 + GS 40 + Science 80) | Same as TRE 2.0 | Mains same shape as TRE 2.0/3.0 (30 + 40 + 80) |
| Options | (A)(B)(C) + (D) More than one of the above + (E) None of the above | Same | Same | **Option E = "question not attempted"** (must be darkened to skip) |
| Negative marking | See the note below | None | None | **1/3 per wrong answer**; a row left fully blank also loses 1/3 |
| Merit | GS + Science | GS + Science (Language qualifying) | Same | Mains Part II + Part III (120); Prelims not counted |

> **Remember:** The TRE 1.0 Class 9–10 booklet we have (Paper-3, "General Studies and Science") printed an instruction that **one-fourth** of the marks would be deducted for a wrong answer, while most summaries describe TRE 1.0–3.0 as having **no negative marking**. TRE 2.0's booklet says your marks "depend on the number of correct responses" (no penalty). For you this history matters only in one way: old toppers could guess freely, **you cannot**. Re-learn your answering habits for 1/3 negative marking.

> **Exam angle:** In TRE 1.0–3.0, options (D) and (E) were part of the answer ("More than one of the above", "None of the above"). In TRE 4.0, E is reported to be the **skip bubble**. If the TRE 4.0 booklet still uses "More than one of the above" or "None of the above", it will now sit in option (D). Read the cover-page instructions of your booklet on the day — see [How Questions Are Asked](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/exam-guide/how-questions-are-asked).

### What did *not* change

- The **science syllabus and level**: Class 9–12 NCERT as the core plus standard B.Sc. topics, all asked as one-line MCQs. The real papers prove this — see [Syllabus Decoded and PYQ Blueprint](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/exam-guide/syllabus-decoded-and-pyq-blueprint).
- **80 science questions** in the merit paper.
- **No interview**, no pedagogy section, no descriptive writing.

## 6. Where sources conflict — your verification checklist

When the official notice and admit card come out, tick these one by one on [bpsc.bihar.gov.in](https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in):

| # | Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Exact number of Class 9–10 Science posts and category-wise split | Reported as ~463; corrigenda change subject tables |
| 2 | Upper age limits and the reckoning date (reported 1 Aug 2026) | Coaching sites differ by 1–2 years |
| 3 | Domicile rules | Decides your category and reservation |
| 4 | Prelims: how many candidates are shortlisted for Mains | Decides how safe your Prelims score must be |
| 5 | Negative marking (1/3) applies to which parts — all three Mains parts? | Language Part I is qualifying; check whether a wrong answer there is also penalised |
| 6 | Qualifying marks (reported: General 40%, BC 36.5%, EBC 34%, SC/ST/women/PwD 32%) and whether they are counted on 120 or 150 marks | Changes the minimum score you need |
| 7 | The wording of options D and E on the OMR | Decides how you skip a question |
| 8 | Exam dates for Prelims and Mains | Decides which version of the 90-day plan you follow |

> **Remember:** Write the official answers to these eight checks on the first page of your notebook. Whenever a coaching video says something different, the notebook (copied from the notice) wins.
