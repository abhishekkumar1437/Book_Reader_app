This is the last stop of the physics book: test yourself chapter by chapter, then use the revision box in the final week before the exam.
Physics is about 27 of the 80 science questions in TRE (TRE 1.0: Q41–67; TRE 2.0: Q71–97), so it is worth roughly one-third of Part III.

> **In simple words:** Cover the answer, say yours aloud, then check. Mark every miss and go back to that chapter's section. Questions with ⭐ are the themes that TRE papers have already asked or that repeat in every competitive exam — learn them first.

## 2. Question bank

### 01 Units, dimensions and errors

1. ⭐ Unit of power of a lens? — **Dioptre** (m⁻¹).
2. ⭐ Unit of inductance in terms of ohm? — **Ohm-second** (henry).
3. ⭐ Unit of magnetic dipole moment? — **A m²** (or J/T).
4. ⭐ Unit of sound level? — **Decibel**.
5. ⭐ Which is not a vector: speed, displacement, velocity? — **Speed**.
6. ⭐ Is refractive index intensive or extensive? — **Intensive**.
7. Dimensions of Planck's constant? — **[M L² T⁻¹]** (same as angular momentum).
8. 1 light year in metres? — **9.46 × 10¹⁵ m**.
9. Is electric current a vector? — **No, scalar**.
10. Number of SI base units? — **Seven**.
11. Percentage error in area if the side has 3% error? — **6%**.
12. 1 kWh = ? J — **3.6 × 10⁶ J**.

### 02 Kinematics and laws of motion

13. ⭐ Slope of a distance–time graph gives? — **Speed**.
14. ⭐ Axis for time in a distance–time graph? — **x-axis (horizontal)**.
15. ⭐ Area under a velocity–time graph gives? — **Displacement**.
16. Is uniform circular motion accelerated? — **Yes** (direction keeps changing).
17. ⭐ Rocket thrust formula? — **F = u dm/dt**.
18. ⭐ Rocket of total mass 500 kg, exhaust 5 km/s, g = 9.8: burn rate to just lift off? — **0.98 kg/s** (Mg/u).
19. Tsiolkovsky equation? — **v = u ln(m₀/m)**.
20. Measure of inertia? — **Mass**.
21. Newton's law behind rocket and gun recoil? — **Third law** (momentum conservation).
22. Order of friction values? — **Static (limiting) > kinetic > rolling**.
23. Apparent weight in a freely falling lift? — **Zero**.
24. Angle for maximum projectile range? — **45°**.
25. Banking angle formula? — **tan θ = v²/(rg)**.

### 03 Work, energy, power and rotation

26. ⭐ Work done when force ⟂ displacement? — **Zero**.
27. ⭐ Definition of 1 joule? — **Work done when 1 N moves a body 1 m along the force**.
28. KE when speed is doubled? — **4 times**.
29. 1 horsepower = ? W — **746 W**.
30. ⭐ T_L : T_C for projectile m₁ and target m₂ at rest? — **(m₁ + m₂) : m₂**.
31. Reduced mass formula? — **µ = m₁m₂/(m₁ + m₂)**.
32. Coefficient of restitution of a perfectly inelastic collision? — **0**.
33. Equal masses, head-on elastic collision, one at rest — result? — **Velocities are exchanged**.
34. ⭐ Kepler's second law is based on? — **Conservation of angular momentum**.
35. Why does a skater spin faster with arms pulled in? — **I decreases, L = Iω constant**.
36. MOI of a solid sphere about a diameter? — **(2/5)MR²**.
37. First to reach the bottom when rolling: ring, disc or solid sphere? — **Solid sphere**.
38. Class of lever: forceps? — **Third class**.

### 04 Gravitation and properties of matter

39. ⭐ Value of G? — **6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m² kg⁻²**.
40. ⭐ Escape velocity from Earth? — **11.2 km/s**.
41. Orbital velocity near Earth's surface? — **7.9 km/s**.
42. Where is g minimum on the surface? — **Equator**.
43. Height of a geostationary satellite? — **≈ 36 000 km** (35 786 km).
44. ⭐ Kepler's third law? — **T² ∝ a³**.
45. ⭐ In V = −k/rⁿ, T₂/T₁ for radii 2R and R? — **2^(n/2 + 1)**.
46. Why is motion under a central force planar? — **Angular momentum is conserved**.
47. ⭐ Curve tangent everywhere to the fluid velocity? — **Streamline**.
48. Equation of continuity? — **A₁v₁ = A₂v₂**.
49. Lift on an aeroplane wing is explained by? — **Bernoulli's theorem**.
50. Steel or rubber — which is more elastic? — **Steel**.
51. Hydraulic brakes work on? — **Pascal's law**.
52. Mercury in a glass capillary — rise or fall? — **Fall**.
53. Unit of viscosity? — **Pa s** (10 poise).

### 05 Heat, thermodynamics and kinetic theory

54. ⭐ Convection can take place in? — **Liquids and gases** (fluids), not solids or vacuum.
55. ⭐ Poorest conductor among copper, lead, mercury (TRE 2.0 official key)? — **Lead** (note: data tables give mercury lower; the key says lead).
56. ⭐ Change in internal energy in a cyclic process? — **Zero** (remains unchanged).
57. ⭐ Carburettor is used in which engine? — **Petrol engine** (diesel uses fuel injection).
58. ⭐ Wien's law — peak wavelength as T rises? — **Shifts to shorter wavelength** (λ_max T = 2.9 × 10⁻³ m K).
59. Stefan's law? — **E = σT⁴**.
60. ⭐ Mean free path formula? — **λ = 1/(√2 π d² n)**, n = N/V.
61. Same number of molecules in volumes V_A, V_B — mean free paths relate how? — **λ_A/V_A = λ_B/V_B** (λ ∝ V).
62. Carnot efficiency? — **1 − T₂/T₁** (kelvin).
63. Temperature at which °C and °F are equal? — **−40°**.
64. Heat transfer from the Sun to Earth? — **Radiation**.
65. First law of thermodynamics? — **Q = ΔU + W** (energy conservation).
66. rms speed formula? — **√(3RT/M)**.
67. Cp/Cv for a monatomic gas? — **5/3**.

### 06 Oscillations, waves and sound

68. ⭐ Sound travels fastest in? — **Solids**.
69. ⭐ Speed of sound at 35 °C if 332 m/s at 0 °C? — **≈ 353 m/s** (v ∝ √T).
70. Can sound travel in vacuum? — **No** (needs a medium).
71. ⭐ Frequency from y = 0.5 sin (2π/3.2)(64t − x)? — **20 Hz**.
72. ⭐ Relation between group and phase velocity (matter wave/waveguide)? — **v_g v_p = c²**.
73. Phase velocity if v_g = 2 × 10⁸ m/s? — **4.5 × 10⁸ m/s**.
74. Time period of a simple pendulum? — **T = 2π√(l/g)**.
75. Audible range for humans? — **20 Hz – 20 kHz**.
76. Bats and SONAR use? — **Ultrasound** (> 20 kHz).
77. Beat frequency of 256 Hz and 260 Hz? — **4 Hz**.
78. Pitch depends on? — **Frequency**; loudness on amplitude.
79. Sound waves in air are? — **Longitudinal**.
80. Effect of humidity on speed of sound? — **Increases** (moist air is less dense).

### 07 Ray optics and optical instruments

81. ⭐ The eye forms the image on? — **Retina**.
82. ⭐ Optical fibre works on? — **Total internal reflection**.
83. ⭐ Glass that cuts off UV rays? — **Crookes glass**.
84. ⭐ Diffusion (scattering) of light in the atmosphere is due to (official key)? — **Dust particles**.
85. Mirror formula? — **1/v + 1/u = 1/f**.
86. Power of a lens of focal length 50 cm? — **+2 D** (convex).
87. Sky appears blue due to? — **Scattering** (Rayleigh, ∝ 1/λ⁴).
88. Lens used to correct myopia? — **Concave**.
89. Condition for TIR? — **Denser → rarer and angle > critical angle**.
90. Critical angle for glass (n = 1.5)? — **≈ 42°** (sin C = 1/n).
91. Colour deviated most by a prism? — **Violet**.
92. Mirror used as a rear-view mirror? — **Convex**.

### 08 Wave optics

93. ⭐ Two identical slits: maximum I, one slit gives I₀ — relation? — **I = 4I₀**.
94. ⭐ Slit width if λ = 600 nm, first minimum 4 mm, D = 2 m? — **0.3 mm** (a = λD/y).
95. Fringe width in YDSE? — **β = λD/d**.
96. ⭐ Brewster's law? — **tan θ_B = n** (reflected and refracted rays at 90°).
97. Brewster angle for glass, n = 1.5? — **≈ 56.3°**.
98. ⭐ Plane-polarised light at what angle to the optic axis of a λ/4 plate gives circular light? — **45°**.
99. Malus's law? — **I = I₀ cos²θ**.
100. Which proves light is transverse? — **Polarisation**.
101. Condition for sustained interference? — **Coherent sources** (constant phase difference).
102. Colours in soap films are due to? — **Interference** (thin film).

### 09 Electrostatics and current electricity

103. ⭐ Resistance of a semiconductor on heating? — **Decreases**.
104. ⭐ Solar energy → electrical energy through? — **Photovoltaic cells**.
105. ⭐ Lightning can burn a tree because it carries tremendous? — **Electric energy** (official key).
106. ⭐ Kirchhoff's current law is based on? — **Conservation of charge**; voltage law on conservation of energy.
107. Coulomb's constant 1/4πε₀? — **9 × 10⁹ N m² C⁻²**.
108. Resistance of a metal on heating? — **Increases**.
109. Equivalent of two 4 Ω resistors in parallel? — **2 Ω**.
110. Unit of electric field? — **N/C or V/m**.
111. Capacitance of a parallel-plate capacitor? — **C = ε₀A/d**.
112. Electric field inside a charged conductor? — **Zero**.
113. Heating effect (Joule's law)? — **H = I²Rt**.
114. Fuse wire should have? — **High resistance, low melting point**.

### 10 Magnetism, EMI and AC

115. ⭐ Lenz's law is based on conservation of? — **Energy**.
116. ⭐ Domains are formed in? — **Ferromagnetic substances**.
117. ⭐ Which process does not produce new poles: cutting a magnet, solenoid current, current in a straight wire? — **Current in a straight wire** (circular field lines, no poles).
118. ⭐ Speed of EM waves in vacuum? — **3 × 10⁸ m/s**.
119. ⭐ Which is not an EM wave: γ, X-ray, β-ray? — **β-ray** (electrons).
120. ⭐ Biot–Savart: B ∝ ? — **I dl sin θ / r²** (statements (i) and (iii)).
121. ⭐ Which is not a Maxwell equation: ∇·J + ∂ρ/∂t = 0? — **That one** (it is the continuity equation).
122. EMF across a stationary conductor in a static field? — **Zero**.
123. Flux through a loop whose plane is parallel to B? — **Zero** (Φ = BA cos 90°).
124. ⭐ Q-factor of a parallel resonant circuit gives? — **Current magnification** (series gives voltage magnification).
125. Resonant frequency of LCR? — **f = 1/(2π√LC)**.
126. Transformer works on? — **Mutual induction** (AC only).

### 11 Modern physics: atoms and nuclei

127. ⭐ Stopping potential for Na (φ 2.3 eV) if W (φ 4.5 eV) gives 1.8 V with the same light? — **4.0 V**.
128. ⭐ Fine structure arises from? — **Spin–orbit coupling**.
129. ⭐ ²¹⁶₈₄X emits an α — new nucleus? — **Z = 82, A = 212**.
130. ⭐ Cosmic rays are? — **Charged particles** (mostly protons).
131. ⭐ Better nuclear fuel: Th-236, U-235, Np-239? — **U-235**.
132. Einstein's photoelectric equation? — **hν = φ + K_max**.
133. Ground-state energy of hydrogen? — **−13.6 eV**.
134. Visible series of hydrogen? — **Balmer series**.
135. Nuclear radius formula? — **R = R₀A^(1/3)** (R₀ ≈ 1.2 fm).
136. Remaining fraction after 3 half-lives? — **1/8**.
137. β⁻ decay changes Z and A how? — **Z + 1, A same**.

### 12 Electronics and communication

138. ⭐ Donor impurities belong to group? — **V (15)** — P, As, Sb.
139. ⭐ Fermi level in an n-type semiconductor lies? — **Just below the conduction band**.
140. ⭐ Maximum efficiency of a half-wave rectifier? — **40.6%** (full-wave 81.2%).
141. ⭐ Modulations used for TV broadcasting? — **AM (picture) and FM (sound)**.
142. ⭐ Reverse saturation current per 10 °C rise? — **Doubles**.
143. ⭐ Cut-off wavelength of Si (1.1 eV) and Ge (0.67 eV) photodiodes? — **1127 nm and 1851 nm** (λ = 1240/E).
144. Fermi level in an intrinsic semiconductor? — **Middle of the forbidden gap**.
145. Barrier potential of a silicon junction? — **≈ 0.7 V** (Ge ≈ 0.3 V).
146. Universal logic gates? — **NAND and NOR**.
147. Zener diode is used as? — **Voltage regulator**.

## 4. Attempt strategy under 1/3 negative marking

- Each question: +1 for correct, **−1/3 for wrong**, and **−1/3 also if all five ovals are left blank** — to skip, you must darken **(E) "question not attempted"** (per reports of the TRE 4.0 notice; verify with the official notice and the instructions on your booklet). See [Exam Pattern and Marking](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/exam-guide/exam-pattern-and-marking).
- With four real answer choices (A–D) and a pure guess: expected score = ¼ × 1 − ¾ × ⅓ = **0**. Blind guessing neither helps nor hurts on average, but increases risk.
- If you can **eliminate one option**: ⅓ × 1 − ⅔ × ⅓ = **+1/9** per question → guess. Eliminate two: ½ − ½ × ⅓ = **+1/3** → definitely answer.
- Physics questions of the "unit / fact" type (dioptre, decibel, TIR, retina) should take **under 20 seconds**; do them first.
- B.Sc. numericals (rocket, slit width, stopping potential, cut-off λ) take 40–60 s each — attempt them **only if the formula is in your head**; skip with (E) otherwise and come back.
- Option **(D) "More than one of the above"** is the trap in "which is true" questions: check every option before marking A, B or C. In TRE 1.0 Q63 (n-type Fermi level), option A was also literally true, so read such questions twice.
- Use dimensions to kill options in numericals (e.g. a speed answer must be in m/s; a phase velocity faster than c is allowed, a group velocity is not).
- Keep a target: about 20 correct out of ~27 physics questions with no more than 3–4 wrong is a strong score.

> **Remember:** The official TRE 2.0 key sometimes differs from textbook data (lead vs mercury as poorest heat conductor; dust particles for atmospheric scattering). In the exam, choose what BPSC has accepted before when the same question repeats.

## Top 15 traps to re-read the night before

1. Speed and current are **scalars**.
2. Henry = **Ω s**, not Ω/s.
3. Rocket lift-off uses **total** mass (body + fuel).
4. T_L/T_C = **(m₁ + m₂)/m₂**.
5. Kepler's 2nd law ↔ **angular momentum**.
6. Wien: hotter → peak at **shorter** wavelength.
7. ΔU = **0** in any cyclic process (W and Q need not be zero).
8. Sound is fastest in **solids**, cannot travel in **vacuum**.
9. Two coherent slits give **4I₀**, not 2I₀, at a maximum.
10. β-rays are **electrons**, not EM waves; cosmic rays are mostly **charged** particles.
11. Lenz's law ↔ **energy** conservation.
12. Domains exist only in **ferromagnetic** materials.
13. Parallel resonance → **current** magnification.
14. Semiconductor resistance **falls** on heating.
15. Donor = **group V** (n-type); acceptor = **group III** (p-type).
