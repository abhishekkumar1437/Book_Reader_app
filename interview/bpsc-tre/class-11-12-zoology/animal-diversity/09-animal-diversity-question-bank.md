A ⭐ marks a theme that has appeared in TRE papers or is repeated in almost every competitive exam. The PYQ table at the end lists every TRE 1.0 and TRE 2.0 Zoology question from this book's topics with the BPSC answer.

> **In simple words:** One line, one fact. If you get a line wrong twice, write it in your "error notebook" and read that notebook every evening in the last week.

## How to use this bank

- **Round 1** (after each chapter): only that chapter's block.
- **Round 2** (weekly): the whole bank in one sitting — about 40 minutes.
- **Round 3** (last week): only ⭐ lines + the PYQ table + your error notebook.

## Attempt strategy under 1/3 negative marking

In TRE 4.0 each question has four answer options (A–D) plus **(E) "question not attempted"**, which you must darken to skip. A wrong answer costs **1/3 mark**; leaving all five blank also costs 1/3 (verify these rules with the official notice).

<div class="formula">

**Expected marks from a guess**

- Pure guess among 4 options: (1/4 × 1) − (3/4 × 1/3) = 0.25 − 0.25 = **0** → no gain, no loss on average.
- After eliminating **one** wrong option (guess among 3): (1/3 × 1) − (2/3 × 1/3) = 0.333 − 0.222 = **+0.11** per question.
- After eliminating **two** (guess between 2): (1/2) − (1/2 × 1/3) = **+0.33** per question.

</div>

1. **Always mark something** — either an answer or **E**. Never leave all five blank (that costs 1/3 like a wrong answer).
2. **Attempt when you can cut at least one option.** In this book, elimination is often easy: "exclusively marine" → strike phyla with freshwater members; "Darwin's book" → strike titles without "Origin".
3. **"More than one of the above" (option D in earlier TREs)** — before choosing D, check each option separately. In TRE 1.0 Q72 only one phylum was fully marine, so D was wrong.
4. **"None of the above"** can be correct (TRE 1.0 Q50). If every option is a *true* part of the theory and the question asks what is *wrong*, E/"none" is the answer.
5. **Spelling and single words decide** — "Origin" vs "Evolution", "internal" vs "external" carotid, "female" vs "male" *Anopheles*. Read the last line of the question twice.
6. **First pass**: answer all sure questions from this book in about 30 seconds each; mark doubtful ones; come back only if time remains.

> **Remember:** Accuracy beats volume. Attempting 70 with 60 right gives 60 − (10 × 1/3) = **56.7**; attempting 100 with 65 right gives 65 − (35 × 1/3) = **53.3**. More attempts, fewer marks. Guess only with a reason.
