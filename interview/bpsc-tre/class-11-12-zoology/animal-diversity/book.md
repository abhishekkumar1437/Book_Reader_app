---
title: Animal Diversity and Evolution
subtitle: NCERT 11 Animal Kingdom to B.Sc. non-chordates, chordates and evolution
description: Nine chapters that take you from the basis of classification through every animal phylum, its classes and its textbook type examples (Amoeba, Plasmodium, Obelia, Fasciola, Ascaris, earthworm, cockroach, Pila, starfish, Herdmania, Amphioxus) up to fishes, amphibians, reptiles, birds and mammals. Comparative anatomy, larval forms, extra-embryonic membranes, origin of life and evolution (Darwin, de Vries, Hardy–Weinberg numericals, speciation, human evolution, geological time scale) complete the book. Real TRE 1.0 and TRE 2.0 Zoology questions are quoted with their BPSC answers, and the last chapter is a question bank.
order: 4
