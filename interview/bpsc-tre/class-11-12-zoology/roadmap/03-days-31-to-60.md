The second month carries the chapters that gave the most PYQs: Mendelian genetics (10 questions in TRE 1.0 + 2.0), endocrine (9), the reproductive system and gametogenesis (9), the menstrual cycle and pregnancy (8), reproductive health (8) and the two biotechnology chapters (7 each). It also holds most of the M.Sc.-level reproduction questions of TRE 1.0 (Bartholin glands, mesovarium, epididymal secretions, spermatogonia, cortical reaction). By **Day 60 (22 December)** the whole syllabus will have been covered once. Even if BPSC fixes the exam for early January, you will have finished everything.

> **In simple words:** Same daily pattern as Phase 1. The chapters are denser, so in every two-day chapter finish the NCERT layer on the first day and the M.Sc. layer on the second. Review days are Days 35, 42, 49 and 56.

**Watch the calendar:** the exam date may be announced during this phase. If it is, open [How the Plan Works](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/roadmap/how-the-plan-works) Section 6 the same day and pick your compression row.

**Every day:** 20 minutes of current affairs inside the GS slot.

## Checkpoint on Day 60

You are ready for Phase 3 if:

1. Weekly test average on Days 35–56 is **32/40 or more** net.
2. You can give the F2 ratio for incomplete dominance, codominance, complementary genes (9 : 7), dominant epistasis (12 : 3 : 1) and recessive epistasis (9 : 3 : 4) without looking.
3. You can solve "father affected, all daughters affected, no sons" (X-linked dominant) and "100 secondary spermatocytes → how many sperms" (200) in under 30 seconds.
4. You can draw the 28-day cycle with the FSH, LH, oestrogen and progesterone curves and mark ovulation (LH surge, about day 14).
5. You can list the enzymes and vectors of rDNA technology and the types of PCR (RT-PCR, qRT-PCR), and name who made the first recombinant insulin (Eli Lilly, 1983).
6. You can name the hormone, gland and disorder for each of: excess cortisol (Cushing's), low cortisol (Addison's), low thyroxine in children (cretinism), excess GH in adults (acromegaly).
7. Your error log has entries from every chapter, and your K tags (knowledge gaps) are falling week by week.

> **Exam angle:** If some chapters are still incomplete on Day 60, finish them in the first days of Phase 3 by replacing the second-pass day of the *same book*. Do not drop the mocks — they train the exam behaviour that negative marking demands.
