The first month builds the base. It opens with **physiology** — the block that gave 34 of 80 questions in TRE 2.0, straight from NCERT Class 11 — and runs it side by side with **cell biology** (structure, membrane, organelles, biomolecules, cell cycle: the TRE 1.0 favourites) and **ecology–biodiversity**, then begins the animal-diversity book. You also read the whole exam guide in the first six days, in the Revision slot, so that you know the rules of the game before the first weekly test.

> **In simple words:** Each day = one zoology chapter (or half of a big one) + one GS/language topic + revision of older chapters. Days 7, 14, 21 and 28 are review days with a 40-question test. By Day 30 you will have finished 19 zoology chapters and started two more.

**Before Day 1:** keep five zoology notebooks (one per book), one GS notebook and one current-affairs notebook ready (see [Books, Notes and Revision](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/exam-guide/books-notes-and-revision)). Keep NCERT Biology Class 11 and 12 on your desk — every two-day chapter starts with the NCERT layer. The online application closes on **26 October 2026** (as reported) — finish it on Day 1 or 2 if you have not already, and keep the PDF of the submitted form.

**Every day:** 20 minutes of current affairs inside the GS slot; write 5 lines in the current-affairs notebook.

## Checkpoint on Day 30

You are on track if:

1. Your four weekly tests (Days 7, 14, 21, 28) average **30/40 or more** net (with −1/3).
2. You can write, from memory, every digestive enzyme with its source, substrate and product, and every hormone of the gut (gastrin, secretin, CCK, GIP).
3. You can draw a nephron and name what happens in each part (glomerulus → ultrafiltration; PCT → most reabsorption; DCT and collecting duct → ADH and aldosterone).
4. You can draw a sarcomere and say which bands shorten in contraction (I band and H zone; A band stays the same).
5. You can name the marker enzyme of each mitochondrial membrane and the types of lysosomes.
6. You can list the stages of prophase I with what happens in each (synapsis in zygotene, crossing over in pachytene).
7. Your language sets are at **9/30 net or above** every time (target 20+).
