The last 30 days turn knowledge into marks. You will read every chapter a second time — this time only its *Quick revision*, *Traps and exceptions* and *Practice with answers* plus your own error log — and you will practise the full exam under negative marking twice a week. Mocks are on Days 63, 67, 70, 74, 77, 81, 84 and 88; Prelims-pattern GS mocks on Days 66, 73, 80 and 87, each followed by one or two zoology question banks in the afternoon.

> **In simple words:** Revise two to four chapters a day, take a full mock every three or four days, analyse every mock the same day, and keep the error log growing. No new books, no new topics.

### How to run a mock day

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| Exam shift time (check admit card; assume 10:00 am until known) | Full mock, 150 minutes, OMR sheet, E for skips, no erasing |
| After lunch (2 h) | Score with +1/−1/3 for each part; tag every wrong or guessed answer K/F/T/R/G |
| Evening (1.5 h) | Read the chapter sections behind the K and F errors; add the T errors to the *Traps* page |
