This plan starts on **Saturday 24 October 2026** (Day 1) and ends on **Thursday 21 January 2027** (Day 90). The exam date is not announced yet; it is expected between **December 2026 and January 2027**. The plan is built so that you finish the full zoology syllabus once by **Day 60 (22 December)** — which keeps you safe even if the exam comes early — and then use 30 days for revision and mocks.

> **In simple words:** Days 1–60: learn every chapter once — one zoology chapter (or half of a big one) per day, plus 1.5 hours of GS/language and 1 hour of revision. Every 7th day is a review day. Days 61–90: revise everything a second time, do the five question banks and take two full mocks a week. If the exam is announced earlier, use Section 6 to squeeze the plan.

## 2. The daily timetable (about 6 hours)

| Slot | Time | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Zoology slot** | 3.5 h (e.g. 6:00–9:30 am, with a 10-minute break each hour) | The chapter of the day: read, make one-line notes, solve the chapter's *Practice with answers* |
| **GS / Language slot** | 1.5 h (e.g. 4:00–5:30 pm) | 20 min current affairs + one GS chapter, a maths/reasoning topic, a physics/chemistry refresher or a language topic (rotating; see below) |
| **Revision slot** | 1 h (e.g. 8:30–9:30 pm) | Spaced revision of earlier chapters (+1, +3, +21 days) and the error log |

If you have more time on some days (holidays), add it to the zoology slot — never skip the revision slot to "save time".

> **Remember:** The day's chapter is done only when you have solved its *Practice with answers* and written every wrong answer into your error log. Reading alone is not "done".

### Two-day chapters: NCERT layer first, M.Sc. layer second

When a chapter gets two days, **part 1** is the NCERT layer (the first half of the sections — the Class 11–12 facts that gave about 76 of 80 questions in TRE 2.0), and **part 2** is the B.Sc./M.Sc. layer (the deeper points that filled nearly half of TRE 1.0) plus *Traps*, *Quick revision* and *Practice*. Never start part 2 before part 1 is solid.

### The GS / Language rotation (Days 1–60)

Each week has six study days. The GS slot rotates like this:

| Study day of the week | GS / Language topic |
|---|---|
| 1st | Maths (quantitative aptitude) |
| 2nd | History (ancient → national movement) |
| 3rd | Reasoning (mental ability); twice in the 60 days a physics or chemistry refresher for GS science |
| 4th | Geography or Polity (alternate weeks) |
| 5th | Bihar special or Economy |
| 6th | Language (Hindi on most weeks, English on the others) |
| 7th day | Weekly review: current affairs of the week + one 30-question language set (8 English + 22 Hindi, the TRE 2.0 split) |

Why this rotation: both Zoology booklets had 8 maths, 8 general-science, 8 current-affairs, 8 geography and 8 history questions in GS — see [Language and General Studies](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/exam-guide/language-and-general-studies). The biology part of general science is already covered by your zoology slot.

## 3. The zoology split — in proportion to the blueprint

In TRE 1.0 + 2.0 (160 zoology questions) the five books gave: cell–genetics **40**, physiology **40**, reproduction–health **36**, ecology–biotech–applied **36**, animal diversity **8** (see [Syllabus Decoded and PYQ Blueprint](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/exam-guide/syllabus-decoded-and-pyq-blueprint)). The 52 study days of Days 1–60 are shared like this:

| Book | Study days | Share of days | Share of PYQs | Chapters given two days | Chapters given one day |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cell Biology, Biochemistry and Genetics | 13 | 25% | 25% | Organelles, Mendelian genetics, molecular biology, chromosomes–linkage–sex | Cell structure, membrane, biomolecules, cell cycle, gene regulation |
| Animal Physiology | 11 | 21% | 25% | Digestion, excretion, muscles, endocrine | Respiration, blood–circulation, nervous system |
| Reproduction, Development and Human Health | 10 | 19% | 22.5% | Reproductive system–gametogenesis, cycle–fertilisation–pregnancy, reproductive health | Embryology, immunology, diseases, drugs–cancer |
| Ecology, Biotechnology and Applied Zoology | 10 | 19% | 22.5% | Environmental issues, biotech tools, biotech applications | Ecosystem, population–cycles, biodiversity–Bihar, economic zoology |
| Animal Diversity and Evolution | 8 | 15% | 5% | — | All eight chapters |

Animal diversity gets more days than its PYQ share because every chapter must be read at least once — it is the core of the M.Sc. syllabus, and a new paper-setter could ask far more than 8 questions from it. The extra time goes back to the big four books in Phase 3.

The books are interleaved in three streams — **cell–genetics**, **physiology then reproduction–health**, and **ecology–biotech with animal diversity** — so that you meet every stream at least once a week (physiology–reproduction almost every second day), and molecular biology (Days 40, 44) comes before the biotechnology chapters (Days 48–59) that depend on it.

> **Exam angle:** The top PYQ chapters — Mendelian genetics (10 questions in two papers), endocrine (9), reproductive system and gametogenesis (9), digestion (8), menstrual cycle–pregnancy (8), reproductive health (8) — all get two days in Phase 1–2 and come back in the Phase 3 second pass and question banks.

## 4. Spaced revision and the 7th-day review

The **Revision** column of every day names the exact chapters to revise, following the 1–3–7–21 rule from [Books, Notes and Revision](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/exam-guide/books-notes-and-revision):

| Label in the table | Meaning | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **+1 recall** | Yesterday's chapter: read your notes and its *Quick revision* | 15 min |
| **+3 practice** | The chapter from three study-days ago: redo its *Practice with answers* without looking | 20 min |
| **+21 traps** | The chapter from about three weeks ago: re-read its *Traps and exceptions* and your error-log entries | 15 min |
| (rest) | Error log, the "X — by — year", ratios and hormone/enzyme pages | 10 min |

**Every 7th day (Days 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 49, 56) is a review day:** redo the practice questions of the week's six study days, take a 40-question mixed test scored with +1/−1/3 (from the question banks of the books you studied that week), revise the week's current affairs, do one 30-question language set, and count your error-log tags.

|---|---|---|
| Days 61–67 | Day 63 (TRE 2.0 Zoology paper), Day 67 (TRE 3.0 Zoology paper if available) | Day 66 |
| Days 68–74 | Day 70 (TRE 1.0 Zoology + GS + a language set), Day 74 | Day 73 |
| Days 75–81 | Day 77, Day 81 | Day 80 |
| Days 82–88 | Day 84, Day 88 (last full mock) | Day 87 |
| Days 89–90 | Light revision only | — |

Download the official TRE question papers and answer keys from [bpsc.bihar.gov.in](https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in). For the other mocks use the app's Quiz tab where sets are available, or one good test series. **Always** score with +1 / −1/3, darken E for skips, and analyse the same day.

## 6. If the exam is announced earlier — how to compress

The moment BPSC announces dates, count the days left to the **Mains** (and separately to the **Prelims**). Then use the row that fits:

| Days left to Mains when announced | What to do |
|---|---|
| **80–90** | Follow the plan; Days 89–90 become the last-week routine. |
| **60–79** | Keep Days 1–60 as they are but merge the two halves of the two-day chapters you find easy (often digestion and excretion for an M.Sc. candidate) into one day each. Shorten Phase 3 to the remaining days with **one** full mock every 3 days. |
| **45–59** | From today, do **two zoology slots a day** (2 h + 2 h) and cut GS to 1 h: one high-weight chapter and one low-weight chapter per day. Give the 7th-day review only 3 hours. Keep at least 10 days for Phase 3 with 4 full mocks. |
| **30–44** | Study only the remaining **high-weight** chapters in full (list below); for the rest read *Quick revision*, *Traps and exceptions* and *Practice with answers* only. Last 10 days: question banks + 3 full mocks. |
| **under 30** | Stop new chapters. Use the five question-bank chapters, the *Quick revision* sections and your error log; take one full mock every 3 days. |

**Never cut these (high PYQ weight):** Cell–genetics — [Mendelian Genetics and Its Extensions](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/cell-genetics/mendelian-genetics-and-its-extensions), [Chromosomes, Linkage, Sex Determination and Disorders](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/cell-genetics/chromosomes-linkage-sex-determination-and-disorders), [Cell Organelles and Cytoskeleton](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/cell-genetics/cell-organelles-and-cytoskeleton), [Molecular Biology: DNA to Protein](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/cell-genetics/molecular-biology-dna-to-protein). Physiology — [Endocrine System and Hormones](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/physiology/endocrine-system-and-hormones), [Digestion and Nutrition](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/physiology/digestion-and-nutrition), [Muscles, Skeleton and Movement](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/physiology/muscles-skeleton-and-movement), [Excretion and Osmoregulation](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/physiology/excretion-and-osmoregulation). Reproduction–health — [Human Reproductive System and Gametogenesis](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/reproduction-health/human-reproductive-system-and-gametogenesis), [Menstrual Cycle, Fertilisation and Pregnancy](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/reproduction-health/menstrual-cycle-fertilisation-and-pregnancy), [Reproductive Health and Contraception](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/reproduction-health/reproductive-health-and-contraception). Ecology–applied — [Environmental Issues and Pollution](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/ecology-applied/environmental-issues-and-pollution), [Biodiversity, Conservation and Wildlife of Bihar](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/ecology-applied/biodiversity-conservation-and-wildlife-of-bihar), [Biotechnology Principles and Tools](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/ecology-applied/biotechnology-principles-and-tools), [Biotechnology Applications and Ethics](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/ecology-applied/biotechnology-applications-and-ethics).

**Cut first (fewest PYQs so far):** the B.Sc. parts of [Flatworms, Roundworms and Annelids](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/animal-diversity/flatworms-roundworms-and-annelids), [Protochordates, Fishes and Amphibians](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/animal-diversity/protochordates-fishes-and-amphibians), [Reptiles, Birds and Mammals](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/animal-diversity/reptiles-birds-and-mammals) and [Comparative Anatomy and Larval Forms](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/animal-diversity/comparative-anatomy-and-larval-forms) (zero questions in TRE 1.0 and 2.0), and the details of [Embryology and Developmental Biology](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/reproduction-health/embryology-and-developmental-biology). Even then, read their *Quick revision* — a new setter may well ask animal diversity.

### If the Prelims comes first with little gap

The Prelims is pure GS. If its date is announced and it is **less than 4 weeks away**:

| Period | Zoology slot | GS slot | Revision slot |
|---|---|---|---|
| Until 3 weeks before Prelims | Plan as usual | 1.5 h | 1 h |
| Last 3 weeks before Prelims | **2.5 h** (continue the plan at a slower pace, or revise) | **3 h**: GS question banks, Bihar, current affairs, one Prelims GS mock every 4 days | 0.5 h |
| After Prelims | Resume the plan; use the extra Phase-3 days to catch up | Back to 1.5 h | 1 h |

> **Common mistake:** Dropping zoology completely for the Prelims. Two weeks without zoology means forgetting a large part of Phase 1. Keep at least 2.5 h of zoology every day.

## 7. If you start late or miss days

- **Start date later than 24 Oct:** keep the order of the days; just ignore the dates. Use Section 6 to see if you need to compress.
- **Missed one day:** do the missed chapter on the next 7th-day review day (do the review in the evening).
- **Missed several days:** do not try to "double up" for a week. Move to the compression row for the days left.
- **Fell ill:** on low-energy days do only the Revision column — it keeps memory alive.
