## 1. Human reproductive system and gametogenesis

Chapter: [Human Reproductive System and Gametogenesis](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/reproduction-health/human-reproductive-system-and-gametogenesis)

1. Why are the testes kept in the scrotum? — To stay **2–2.5 °C below body temperature**, needed for spermatogenesis.
2. Failure of testes to descend is called? — **Cryptorchidism**.
3. How many lobules are in each testis? — About **250**.
4. ⭐ Which cells secrete testosterone? — **Leydig (interstitial) cells**.
5. ⭐ Which cells nourish developing sperms? — **Sertoli cells**.
6. Which cells secrete inhibin in males? — **Sertoli cells**.
7. Where do sperms mature and gain motility? — **Epididymis**.
8. Where are sperms stored? — **Cauda epididymis** (and vas deferens).
9. Name two epididymal secretions used as markers. — **L-carnitine and neutral α-glucosidase**.
10. ⭐ Which gland's secretion stimulates sperm activity? — **Prostate**.
11. Which gland supplies fructose? — **Seminal vesicles**.
12. Which glands secrete lubricating mucus in males? — **Bulbourethral (Cowper's) glands**.
13. Number of sperms per ejaculation (NCERT)? — **200–300 million**.
14. ⭐ Where are Bartholin's glands located? — **On either side of the vaginal opening**.
15. Male homologue of Bartholin's glands? — **Cowper's glands**.
16. ⭐ Which peritoneal fold suspends the ovary? — **Mesovarium**.
17. Which ligament joins the ovary to the uterus? — **Ovarian ligament**.
18. Mesosalpinx encloses which organ? — **Fallopian tube**.
19. ⭐ Region of fallopian tube where fertilisation occurs? — **Ampulla**.
20. Finger-like projections of the infundibulum? — **Fimbriae**.
21. Narrow part of the oviduct joining the uterus? — **Isthmus**.
22. Uterine layer that contracts during birth? — **Myometrium**.
23. ⭐ Type B spermatogonia divide by? — **Mitosis**.
24. Spermatid to sperm transformation is called? — **Spermiogenesis**.
25. Release of sperms from Sertoli cells into the lumen? — **Spermiation**.
26. ⭐ 100 secondary spermatocytes give how many sperms? — **200**.
27. ⭐ 100 secondary oocytes give how many ova? — **100**.
28. Meiotic divisions needed to make 100 zygotes? — **125** (100 for ova + 25 for sperms).
29. At what stage is the primary oocyte arrested till puberty? — **Prophase I (diplotene)**.
30. ⭐ What is released at ovulation in humans? — **Secondary oocyte (arrested at metaphase II)**.
31. When does the oocyte complete meiosis II? — **After sperm entry**.
32. Follicles left per ovary at puberty? — **60,000–80,000**.
33. Fluid-filled cavity of the tertiary follicle? — **Antrum**.
34. ⭐ Which sperm part helps penetration of the ovum? — **Acrosome** (hyaluronidase, acrosin).
35. Which sperm part gives energy for swimming? — **Middle piece (mitochondria)**.
36. Corona radiata belongs to? — **The ovum (follicle cells)**.
37. ⭐ Gonadotropins are secreted by? — **Anterior pituitary**.
38. ⭐ Spermatogenesis is controlled by? — **FSH and LH** (with testosterone).
39. ⭐ Male secondary sex characters are due to? — **Testosterone**.
40. ⭐ Oestrogen is produced mainly by? — **Ovaries** (granulosa cells of growing follicles).

## 2. Menstrual cycle, fertilisation and pregnancy

Chapter: [Menstrual Cycle, Fertilisation and Pregnancy](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/reproduction-health/menstrual-cycle-fertilisation-and-pregnancy)

41. Average length of the menstrual cycle? — **28–29 days**.
42. ⭐ Ovulation is caused by? — **LH surge** (about day 14).
43. ⭐ Menstrual cycle is controlled by? — **FSH, LH (pituitary) and oestrogen, progesterone (ovary)**.
44. Which hormone is highest in the luteal phase? — **Progesterone**.
45. ⭐ Cessation of menstrual cycles is called? — **Menopause**.
46. First menstruation is called? — **Menarche**.
47. Non-primate mammals show which cycle? — **Oestrous cycle**.
48. Degenerated corpus luteum is called? — **Corpus albicans**.
49. Removal of coating from the sperm in the female tract? — **Capacitation**.
50. ⭐ Fusion of sperm and oocyte membranes triggers? — **Cortical reaction** (blocks polyspermy).
51. Acrosome reaction occurs where? — **At the zona pellucida**, before fusion.
52. 8–16-celled embryo is called? — **Morula**.
53. Outer layer of the blastocyst? — **Trophoblast** (forms placenta).
54. Which part forms the embryo? — **Inner cell mass**.
55. When does implantation occur? — About **6–7 days after fertilisation**.
56. ⭐ Hormone detected in pregnancy tests? — **hCG**.
57. Hormones made only during pregnancy? — **hCG and hPL**.
58. Human placenta by shape? — **Discoidal** (and haemochorial, deciduate).
59. ⭐ By which time does the conceptus fill the uterine cavity (TRE key)? — **By 12 weeks**.
60. When are the heart, then limbs formed? — **End of month 1; end of month 2**.
61. When are first foetal movements felt? — **Fifth month**.
62. ⭐ Hormones of parturition (TRE key)? — **Oxytocin and relaxin**.
63. Type of feedback in parturition? — **Positive**.
64. Signal for parturition starts from? — **Fully developed foetus and placenta** (foetal ejection reflex).
65. Hormone for milk production? — **Prolactin**; for milk ejection — **oxytocin**.
66. ⭐ Antibody in colostrum? — **IgA**.

## 3. Embryology and developmental biology

Chapter: [Embryology and Developmental Biology](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/reproduction-health/embryology-and-developmental-biology)

80. Notochord develops from? — **Mesoderm**.
81. ⭐ Respiratory organ of the chick embryo? — **Allantois** (with chorion).
82. Primary organiser is the? — **Dorsal lip of blastopore** (Spemann and Mangold, 1924).

84. Hormone of frog metamorphosis? — **Thyroxine**.
85. Hydra regenerates by? — **Morphallaxis**.

## 4. Reproductive health and contraception

Chapter: [Reproductive Health and Contraception](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/reproduction-health/reproductive-health-and-contraception)

88. First country with a national family planning programme? — **India (1951)**.
89. ⭐ Emergency contraceptives work within? — **72 hours of coitus**.
90. ⭐ Oral pills mainly work by? — **Preventing ovulation**.
91. ⭐ Saheli — developed where, taken how often? — **CDRI Lucknow; once a week**.
92. Copper IUDs work by? — **Suppressing sperm motility and fertilising capacity**.
93. Non-medicated IUD? — **Lippes loop**.
94. Hormone-releasing IUDs? — **Progestasert, LNG-20**.
95. ⭐ Male contraceptives available? — **Condoms and vasectomy** (and withdrawal).
96. ⭐ Why are condoms popular? — **Effective barrier, protect against STIs, easily available**.
97. Lactational amenorrhoea is effective up to? — **6 months after delivery**.
98. Days to avoid in periodic abstinence? — **Days 10–17**.
99. ⭐ STI that does not specifically affect sex organs? — **AIDS**.
100. ⭐ First well-recorded European STD outbreak? — **Syphilis** (Naples, 1494–95).
101. STIs that are not completely curable? — **Hepatitis B, genital herpes, HIV**.
102. ⭐ Test-tube baby means? — **Ovum fertilised outside the body, embryo then placed in the uterus**.
103. ⭐ World's first test-tube baby? — **Louise Brown (25 July 1978)**.
104. India's first test-tube baby? — **Durga / Kanupriya Agarwal (Kolkata, 3 October 1978)**.
105. ZIFT transfers what, where? — **Zygote/embryo up to 8 blastomeres into the fallopian tube**.
106. GIFT transfers? — **Donor ovum (gametes) into the fallopian tube**.
107. Sperm injected directly into ovum? — **ICSI**.
108. Amniocentesis is banned for? — **Sex determination** (PCPNDT Act).

## 5. Immunology

Chapter: [Immunology](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/reproduction-health/immunology)

109. ⭐ Blood component that makes antibodies? — **Lymphocytes** (B cells → plasma cells).
110. Cells that secrete interferons? — **Virus-infected cells**.
111. Primary lymphoid organs? — **Bone marrow and thymus**.
112. ⭐ Immunoglobulin that crosses the placenta? — **IgG**.
113. Largest antibody, first in primary response? — **IgM** (pentamer).
114. Antibody of allergy? — **IgE**.
115. Antibody structure? — **H₂L₂, Y-shaped, disulphide bonds**.
116. ⭐ All immunogens are antigens, but…? — **Not all antigens are immunogens** (haptens).
117. ⭐ Term "vaccine" introduced by (official TRE key)? — **Edward Jenner** (Pasteur generalised it in his honour).
118. ⭐ AIDS is transmitted by? — **Sex, blood transfusion and from mother to child (placenta)**.
119. HIV factory in the body? — **Macrophages**.
120. Cells destroyed by HIV? — **Helper T (CD4) cells**.
121. Graft rejection is due to? — **Cell-mediated immunity (T cells)**.

## 9. Last-week revision box

<div class="formula">

**Numbers:** scrotum 2–2.5 °C cooler · 250 lobules · 200–300 million sperms · oviduct 10–12 cm · 60,000–80,000 follicles per ovary at puberty · cycle 28 days, ovulation ~day 14, menstruation 3–5 days · implantation day 6–7 · morula 8–16 cells · gestation ~9 months (280 days from LMP) · emergency pill 72 h · lactational amenorrhoea 6 months · rhythm method avoid days 10–17 · ZIFT ≤ 8 blastomeres · Hayflick ~50 divisions · CD4 below 200 = AIDS.

**Counting rule:** primary spermatocyte → 4 sperms; secondary spermatocyte → 2; primary or secondary oocyte → 1 ovum. Meioses for n zygotes = n + n/4.

**Hormones:** GnRH → FSH (Sertoli/follicle) + LH (Leydig/ovulation) · inhibin blocks FSH · hCG keeps corpus luteum · oxytocin = labour + milk ejection · prolactin = milk synthesis · relaxin = pelvic relaxation (ovary, late pregnancy).

**Reactions:** capacitation (female tract) → acrosome reaction (zona) → membrane fusion → cortical reaction (block to polyspermy) → meiosis II completed → pronuclei fuse.

**Antibodies:** G crosses placenta (most abundant) · A colostrum (dimer) · M first and largest (pentamer) · D B-cell receptor · E allergy and worms.

**Vectors:** female Anopheles — malaria · Aedes — dengue, chikungunya, Zika · female Culex — filaria, JE · sandfly — kala-azar · tsetse — sleeping sickness · rat flea — plague · housefly — mechanical (typhoid, cholera, amoebiasis) · bed bug — no proven disease.

**Names and dates:** Jenner 1796 · Pasteur rabies 1885 · Koch TB 1882 · Ross 1897 · Spemann–Mangold 1924 · AIDS 1981 (India 1986) · Louise Brown 25 Jul 1978 · Durga/Kanupriya 3 Oct 1978 · India polio-free 2014 · Bihar prohibition 2016 · Muzaffarpur AES 2019.

</div>

## 10. Attempt strategy under 1/3 negative marking

- **The rule (as reported for TRE 4.0 — verify with the notice):** +1 for correct, **−1/3 for wrong**; option **E = "question not attempted"** must be darkened to skip; leaving all five blank also costs 1/3.
- **Expected value of a guess** with four real options: a blind guess gives ¼ × 1 − ¾ × ⅓ = **0** — no gain, no loss on average. If you can **eliminate one** option for sure, a guess among three gives ⅓ − ⅔ × ⅓ = **+1/9** per question. If you can eliminate **two**, the gain is ½ − ½ × ⅓ = **+1/3**.
- So: **answer** when you know it or can cut at least one option with certainty; **mark E** when all options look equally possible.
- In this book's area most questions are **direct NCERT recall** — attempt them all after revision. The risky ones are the B.Sc.-level questions (ligaments, epididymal enzymes, virus classes), where keys can be disputed; still choose the textbook answer unless you remember the key.
- **"More than one of the above"** (if this option style continues): before choosing a single option, test every other option — if two are true, the combined option is correct.
- **Time:** spend about 30–40 seconds on recall questions here, saving time for calculation questions (genetics, ecology) elsewhere in Part III.
- After the paper, compare with the provisional key and file objections only with NCERT/standard-text proof — BPSC publishes a provisional key, invites objections and then releases the final key.
