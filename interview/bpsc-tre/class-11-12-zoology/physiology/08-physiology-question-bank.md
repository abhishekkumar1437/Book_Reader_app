This is the question bank for the Animal Physiology book. Use it in three rounds: first after finishing each chapter, then once a week, and finally in the last week before the exam. Physiology was the **largest block of TRE 2.0** — Q71–105 (35 of 80 Zoology questions) came from this book, mostly NCERT class 11 one-liners. TRE 1.0 added B.Sc.-level points (Kupffer cells, sarcoplasmic reticulum, actin filament make-up, Cushing's syndrome).

> **In simple words:** Cover the answer, say it aloud, then check. If you get a question wrong twice, go back to the chapter section. A ⭐ means the theme has already come in a real TRE paper or is a standard repeat in other state TET/TRE papers — do these first.

### Mixed drill — true or false (say why)

136. ⭐ "Bile contains digestive enzymes." — **False**; it has only bile salts and pigments.
137. "Fructose is absorbed by active transport." — **False**; facilitated diffusion (GLUT-5).
138. "Expiration during quiet breathing is passive." — **True**; elastic recoil.
139. "Most CO₂ is carried as carbamino-haemoglobin." — **False**; ~70% as bicarbonate.
140. "Foetal haemoglobin has a lower affinity for O₂ than adult Hb." — **False**; higher (left shift).
141. ⭐ "Serum contains fibrinogen." — **False**; serum = plasma minus clotting factors.
142. "Anti-Rh antibodies are naturally present in Rh⁻ people." — **False**; formed only after exposure.
143. "The SA node is in the left atrium." — **False**; right atrium.
144. "The renal portal system is present in mammals." — **False**; absent in mammals.
145. ⭐ "The ascending limb of Henle's loop is impermeable to water." — **True**.
146. "ANF increases sodium reabsorption." — **False**; it promotes Na⁺ and water loss.
147. "The A band shortens during contraction." — **False**; I band and H zone shorten.
148. "Troponin is a contractile protein." — **False**; regulatory.
149. "Electrical synapses are faster than chemical synapses." — **True**.
150. "Grey matter lies outside in the spinal cord." — **False**; inside (H-shaped).
151. "The fovea contains only rods." — **False**; only cones.
152. "Oxytocin is made by the posterior pituitary." — **False**; made in the hypothalamus, released by the posterior pituitary.
153. "Calcitonin raises blood calcium." — **False**; it lowers it (PTH raises it).
154. "Thyroid hormones act through intracellular receptors." — **True**.
155. ⭐ "Diabetes insipidus is caused by insulin deficiency." — **False**; ADH deficiency.

## 4. Attempt strategy under 1/3 negative marking

TRE 4.0 Mains (as reported; **verify with the official notice**) gives **+1 for a correct answer and −1/3 for a wrong one**, and option **(E) must be darkened to skip**; leaving all five blank is also penalised. So think of each question as a four-way choice among (A)–(D).

| Your situation | Expected marks per question | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Sure of the answer | +1 | Attempt |
| Can eliminate **two** options (50–50) | ½ × 1 − ½ × ⅓ = **+0.33** | Attempt |
| Can eliminate **one** option (1 in 3) | ⅓ × 1 − ⅔ × ⅓ = **+0.11** | Attempt (small gain) |
| Pure guess among four | ¼ × 1 − ¾ × ⅓ = **0** | Mark **(E)** — no gain, adds risk |

**Physiology-specific tips**

1. **Read the stem word by word** — "renin" vs "rennin", "green" vs "yellow" bile, "contractile" vs "regulatory", "child" vs "adult".
2. **"More than one of the above" rule** — pick (D) only if each option alone is a standard NCERT fact (as in TRE 2.0 Q98 and Q109). If one option is the main textbook answer and the others are only partly true, BPSC has chosen the main one.
3. **Numbers questions** (lung volumes, cardiac output, GFR) are quick marks — do them first in your Zoology block.
4. **Disorder pairs** (hypo/hyper, child/adult) — write the pair in the margin before choosing.
5. Physiology one-liners should take **20–30 seconds** each; bank the saved time for genetics and ecology calculations.

- **Official keys sometimes prefer one answer over a defensible "(D)"** — learn the key's choice for repeated questions.
