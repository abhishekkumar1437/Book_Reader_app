This chapter gives you the full picture of the exam you are preparing for: the advertisement, the post, the eligibility rules, the stages and the pay. It also explains what is **new** in TRE 4.0 compared with the earlier Teacher Recruitment Exams (TRE 1.0, 2.0 and 3.0). The old zoology papers are your best practice material, but the old rules no longer apply.

> **In simple words:** TRE 4.0 is the Bihar government's exam to hire school teachers through BPSC (Bihar Public Service Commission). You are applying for **Senior Secondary Teacher (Class 11–12), Zoology** — the teacher who teaches the animal half of class 11–12 Biology. You need a **post-graduate degree (M.Sc.) in Zoology**, a **B.Ed** and a pass in **Bihar STET Paper-II in Zoology**. There is a GS screening test first, then a main test of 150 MCQs in which 80 questions are pure zoology. Wrong answers now cost **1/3 mark**.

> **Remember:** Everything below was collected on **23 October 2026** from the BPSC notice as reported by news and coaching websites. Some numbers differ between sources. Before you rely on any number — especially vacancies, age limits, qualifying marks and the exam date — **check the official notice on [bpsc.bihar.gov.in](https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in)**.

> **Exam angle:** Class 11–12 posts are fewer than the lower-class posts, and they are divided subject by subject. Your competition is every M.Sc. Zoology + B.Ed + STET-II holder in Bihar. In such a field the gap between selection and non-selection is usually a handful of marks in the subject part. That is why this collection is built around accuracy and around not losing marks to negative marking.

> **Common mistake:** Reading "33,320 posts" and assuming the competition is easy. That number is spread over Classes 1–5, 6–8, 9–10 and 11–12 and over dozens of subjects. Only the **Zoology (11–12)** line of the vacancy table matters to you. Write it down from the notice.

## 2. Who can apply (Class 11–12 Zoology)

### 2.1 Educational qualification

These are the rules that applied for Class 11–12 in the earlier TREs. They are expected to continue; **verify each line in Advt. 15/2026**.

| Requirement | Detail (as in earlier TREs — verify) |
|---|---|
| Degree | **Post-graduation (M.A./M.Sc.) in the subject — Zoology** — with at least **50% marks** |
| Relaxation in PG marks | **5%** for SC/ST/EBC/BC/women/PwD (so 45%) — verify the exact categories |
| Teacher training | **B.Ed**, or the integrated routes the notice allows (for example 4-year B.Sc.B.Ed, or 3-year integrated B.Ed–M.Ed) |
| Eligibility test | **Bihar STET Paper-II (Senior Secondary) in Zoology** — passed |

> **Remember:** For Class 11–12 the eligibility test is **Bihar STET Paper-II**, in your subject. STET Paper-I is for Class 9–10; CTET covers only Classes 1–8 and does not make you eligible for Class 11–12. If your STET Paper-II subject is not Zoology, you cannot apply for the Zoology post even with an M.Sc. in Zoology.

> **In simple words:** M.Sc. Zoology (50%, or 45% with relaxation) + B.Ed + STET Paper-II Zoology = eligible.

> **Exam angle:** If you also hold B.Sc. with Zoology and **STET Paper-I Science**, you may be eligible for the Class 9–10 Science post too. Its sister collection is [Class 9–10 Science](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-9-10-science/exam-guide/tre-4-explained). Read the notice to see whether one candidate may apply for more than one post, and whether the exams clash.

### 2.2 Age limit

Age is counted on **1 August 2026** (as reported).

| Category | Minimum age | Maximum age (reported) |
|---|---|---|
| Unreserved (UR) male | 21 | 37 |
| UR female | 21 | 40 |
| BC and EBC (male and female) | 21 | 40 |
| SC and ST (male and female) | 21 | 42 |
| PwD | 21 | usually extra years over the category limit — **verify** |

> **Common mistake:** Some coaching websites print limits that differ by one or two years, and some quote the age-reckoning date from an older TRE. Only the advertisement decides. If you are close to the upper limit, read the age clause of Advt. 15/2026 word by word.

### 2.3 Domicile and reservation

The Bihar government has announced a **domicile preference** for teacher recruitment (benefits linked to being a resident of Bihar). Reservation for women and other reserved categories is available only to Bihar residents. The exact TRE 4.0 rules — who counts as a domicile, and whether non-residents can apply in the unreserved category — are written in the notice. **Read that clause yourself**; coaching sites have reported it differently.

| | Stage 1: Preliminary (screening) | Stage 2: Mains |
|---|---|---|
| Questions | 150 MCQs | 150 MCQs |
| Marks | 150 | 150 |
| Time | 2 hours | 2 hours 30 minutes |
| Content | General Studies only: general science, current affairs, history, geography, polity, economy, mental ability, Bihar | Part I Language (30 Q, **qualifying, minimum 30%**) · Part II General Studies (40 Q) · Part III **Zoology (80 Q)** |
| Counted in merit? | **No** — only to shortlist for Mains | **Yes** — Part II + Part III = **120 marks** decide the merit |
| Negative marking | 1/3 mark per wrong answer | 1/3 mark per wrong answer |

The full marking rules, qualifying marks and worked score examples are in [Exam Pattern and Marking](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/exam-guide/exam-pattern-and-marking).

> **Exam angle:** Your rank depends on 120 marks, and **80 of them are zoology**. Zoology is two-thirds of your merit. That is why five of the seven books in this collection are zoology books.

> **Remember:** The Prelims is "only screening", but if you fail it you never reach the zoology paper. Many M.Sc. candidates are weak in GS (history, polity, Bihar, maths). A daily 1.5-hour GS/language slot is built into the [90-Day Plan](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/roadmap/how-the-plan-works).

| Item | Reported figure |

| Allowances | DA, HRA, medical and others as per Bihar government rules |
| In-hand at joining | Higher than the basic because of DA and HRA; depends on the DA rate and posting place — treat any figure you see online as approximate |

## 5. What changed from TRE 1.0, 2.0 and 3.0

We have the actual Class 11–12 Zoology booklets of TRE 1.0 and TRE 2.0. Their cover pages tell us exactly how the old exam worked.

| Feature | TRE 1.0 (26 Aug 2023, Advt. 26/2023) | TRE 2.0 (15 Dec 2023, Advt. 27/2023) | TRE 4.0 (2026–27, reported) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stages | One written exam | One written exam | **Two: Prelims (GS screening) + Mains** |
| Class 11–12 Zoology paper | Separate qualifying language paper + **"Paper-4: General Studies and Zoology"**: 120 Q, 120 marks, 2 h (GS Q1–40, Zoology Q41–120) | **One booklet "Language, General Studies and Zoology"**: 150 Q, 150 marks, 2 h 30 min (Language Q1–30, GS Q31–70, Zoology Q71–150) | Mains of the TRE 2.0 shape (30 + 40 + 80) |
| Options | (A)(B)(C) + (D) More than one of the above + (E) None of the above | Same | **Option E = "question not attempted"** (must be darkened to skip) |
| Negative marking | The booklet printed: "**one-fourth** of the marks assigned to that question will be deducted as penalty" | Booklet: "Your total marks will depend on the number of correct responses" — **no penalty** | **1/3 per wrong answer**; a row left fully blank also loses 1/3 |
| Answer key | Provisional and **2nd provisional** keys published | Provisional, then **final** key published | Expect the same process |
| Merit | GS + Zoology | GS + Zoology (Language qualifying) | Mains Part II + Part III (120); Prelims not counted |

> **Remember:** We have not seen the TRE 3.0 (July 2024) Class 11–12 Zoology booklet. Most summaries describe TRE 3.0 as one paper of the TRE 2.0 shape without negative marking. For you this history matters in one way: earlier candidates could guess freely; **you cannot**. Re-learn your answering habits for 1/3 negative marking.

> **Exam angle:** In the old papers, options (D) "More than one of the above" and (E) "None of the above" were **real answers** — and in zoology they were correct far more often than in the science papers: 16 times in 160 zoology questions (Section 1 of [How Questions Are Asked](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/exam-guide/how-questions-are-asked)). In TRE 4.0, E is reported to be the **skip bubble**. If the TRE 4.0 booklet still uses "More than one of the above", it will now sit in option (D). Read the cover page of your booklet on the day.

### What did *not* change

- The **zoology level**: NCERT Class 11–12 Biology (the animal half) as the core, plus standard B.Sc./M.Sc. zoology topics, all asked as one-line MCQs. The real papers prove this — see [Syllabus Decoded and PYQ Blueprint](/interview/bpsc-tre/class-11-12-zoology/exam-guide/syllabus-decoded-and-pyq-blueprint).
- **80 subject questions** in the merit paper.
- **No interview**, no pedagogy section, no descriptive writing.

## 6. Where sources conflict — your verification checklist

When the official notice and admit card come out, tick these one by one on [bpsc.bihar.gov.in](https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in):

| # | Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Exact number of Class 11–12 **Zoology** posts and the category-wise split | Not known to us; corrigenda often change subject tables |
| 2 | Eligibility wording: PG in Zoology with 50%; accepted B.Ed / integrated routes; **STET Paper-II Zoology** | Decides whether you can apply at all |
| 3 | Upper age limits and the reckoning date (reported 1 Aug 2026) | Coaching sites differ by 1–2 years |
| 4 | Domicile rules | Decides your category and reservation |
| 5 | Prelims: how many candidates per post are called to the Mains | Decides how safe your Prelims score must be |
| 6 | Negative marking (1/3): does it apply to all three Mains parts, including Language? | Part I is qualifying; a wrong answer there may still be penalised |
| 7 | Qualifying marks (reported: General 40%, BC 36.5%, EBC 34%, SC/ST/women/PwD 32%) and whether they are counted on 120 or 150 | Changes the minimum you need |
| 8 | Wording of options D and E on the OMR | Decides how you skip a question |
| 9 | Exam dates for Prelims and Mains | Decides which version of the 90-day plan you follow |
| 10 | Basic pay for Class 11–12 (reported ₹32,000) | Only for your information |

> **Remember:** Write the official answers to these checks on the first page of your notebook. Whenever a coaching video says something different, the notebook (copied from the notice) wins.
