## 1. Ecosystem structure and energy flow

1. ⭐ Who coined the term "ecosystem"? — **A.G. Tansley (1935)**.
2. Who coined "ecology"? — **Ernst Haeckel (1866)**.
3. ⭐ Who gave the 10% law of energy transfer? — **Raymond Lindeman (1942)**.
4. Write the relation between GPP and NPP. — **NPP = GPP − R** (respiration).
5. Annual NPP of the whole biosphere? — **About 170 billion tonnes** (oceans ~55 billion).
6. Percentage of PAR captured by plants? — **2–10%**.
7. ⭐ Which pyramid is always upright? — **Pyramid of energy**.
8. ⭐ Pyramid of biomass is inverted in? — **Sea/aquatic ecosystems**.
9. Pyramid of number is inverted in? — **A tree ecosystem and parasitic food chains**.
10. In terrestrial ecosystems, which food chain carries more energy? — **Detritus food chain**.
11. Step of decomposition that forms humus? — **Humification**.
12. Decomposition is slow when detritus is rich in? — **Lignin and chitin**.
13. Energy flow in an ecosystem is? — **Unidirectional**.
14. Producers fix 40,000 J; energy at tertiary consumers? — **40 J**.
15. ⭐ Desert ecosystem is characterised by? — **Extreme temperatures, low rainfall (< 25 cm), scarce vegetation** (TRE 1.0 Q93).
16. Pioneer species on bare rock? — **Lichens**.
17. Pioneer species in a pond (hydrarch)? — **Phytoplankton**.
18. Climax community is reached in hydrarch and xerarch at which moisture condition? — **Mesic (medium)**.
19. Secondary succession is faster than primary because? — **Soil (and seeds) already exist**.
20. Who valued global ecosystem services at about US $33 trillion a year? — **Robert Costanza (1997)**.

## 2. Population, community and biogeochemical cycles

21. ⭐ Method to estimate the density of mobile animals? — **Capture–recapture** (TRE 1.0 Q92).
22. Formula of the Lincoln index? — **N = (M × C) ÷ R**.
23. 50 birds marked; later 60 caught with 15 marked. Population? — **200**.
24. Method for plants and sessile animals? — **Quadrat method**.
25. Exponential growth equation? — **dN/dt = rN** (J-shaped curve).
26. ⭐ Logistic growth equation? — **dN/dt = rN(K − N)/K** (S-shaped curve).
27. What is K? — **Carrying capacity**.
28. At what population size is logistic growth fastest? — **K/2**.
29. Urn-shaped age pyramid means? — **Declining population**.
30. Who proposed r and K selection? — **MacArthur and Wilson (1967)**.
31. ⭐ Competitive exclusion principle was given by? — **G.F. Gause (1934)**.
32. Orchid on a mango branch shows? — **Commensalism**.
33. Koel laying eggs in a crow's nest is? — **Brood parasitism**.
34. *Penicillium* inhibiting bacteria shows? — **Amensalism**.
35. Fig and fig-wasp relationship? — **Mutualism**.
36. ⭐ Lightning converts atmospheric N₂ into? — **Oxides of nitrogen** (TRE 2.0 Q137).
37. Bacterium converting ammonia to nitrite? — ***Nitrosomonas***.
38. Bacterium converting nitrite to nitrate? — ***Nitrobacter***.
39. Denitrifying bacterium? — ***Pseudomonas*** (also *Thiobacillus*).
40. Pigment protecting nitrogenase from oxygen? — **Leghaemoglobin**.
41. Which cycle has no significant gaseous phase? — **Phosphorus cycle**.
42. Percentage of global carbon dissolved in oceans? — **About 71%**.

## 9. Mixed numericals (two-minute drill)

1. GPP 18,000 kcal m⁻² yr⁻¹, respiration 7,000. NPP? — **11,000**.
2. Producers 1,00,000 J. Energy at the 4th trophic level? — **100 J** (three transfers).
3. A carnivore at the 3rd level gets 60 kcal. Producer energy? — **6,000 kcal**.
4. 40 tagged, 75 recaptured, 10 tagged among them. N? — **300**.
5. 120 marked, 100 caught later, 30 marked. N? — **400**.
6. Population 2,000; births 300, deaths 100, immigrants 50, emigrants 50. Next year? — **2,200**.
7. Birth rate when 30 new individuals join a population of 150 in a year? — **0.2 per individual per year**.
8. r = 0.1/yr. Doubling time? — **About 6.9 years** (0.693/0.1).
9. Logistic: r = 0.5, K = 800, N = 200. dN/dt? — **75** (0.5 × 200 × 600/800).
10. Quadrats: 25 laid; species in 10, with 40 plants. Density, frequency, abundance? — **1.6; 40%; 4**.
11. PCR: 3 starting molecules, 10 cycles? — **3,072** (3 × 1,024).
12. Expected frequency of a 6-bp restriction site? — **Once per 4,096 bp**.
13. Species–area: Z = 0.2, area cut to 1/32. Fraction of species kept? — **(1/32)^0.2 = 1/2 → 50%** kept.

## 11. Attempt strategy under one-third negative marking

- In TRE 4.0 (as reported), each wrong answer costs **1/3 mark**, and **option E ("not attempted") must be darkened** to skip; leaving all five bubbles blank also costs 1/3. Verify with the official notice.
- If the real answer choices are A–D (E being "not attempted"), a **pure guess** among four has expected score 1/4 × 1 − 3/4 × 1/3 = **0** — no gain, no loss on average. If you can **eliminate one** wrong option, a guess among three gives 1/3 − 2/3 × 1/3 = **+1/9** per question; eliminating **two** gives 1/2 − 1/2 × 1/3 = **+1/3**. So: **attempt whenever you can confidently rule out at least one option; mark E when you cannot.**
- Ecology and biotech questions are short recall items — answer them in **20–30 seconds** each and save time for genetics numericals elsewhere in the paper.
- Watch the "**More than one of the above**" option (used in TRE 1.0 and 2.0). Choose it only when two options are **clearly** true by NCERT wording (TRE 1.0 Q63, Q111). When the key's single answer follows NCERT wording exactly (TRE 2.0 Q142 "repetitive DNA", Q145 "amplification"), prefer that single option.
- For Bihar questions (sanctuary–district pairs), the location answer is usually the NCERT/state government fact: Vikramshila → **Bhagalpur**, Valmiki → **West Champaran**, Kanwar → **Begusarai**, Bhimbandh → **Munger**, Nagi–Nakti → **Jamui**.
- Numbers change (tiger count, Ramsar sites, protected-area counts). If an option set includes old and new numbers, choose the figure from the **latest official release** and check the news in the final month.
