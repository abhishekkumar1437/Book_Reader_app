# CLAIM_SYSTEM.md — दावा (Canonical)

The only magic. Power = self-definition. Cost = freedom.

## Rules (hard canon)
1. **Only your own रिक्त.** You can claim only what was never written about *you*. Never about others, never about the world.
2. **Form.** Spoken aloud, first person, present tense, with whole intent. "मैं जानता हूँ…" / "मेरा…" / "मैं हूँ…". Half-hearted → nothing happens. Lying to yourself → nothing happens.
3. **Instant, permanent.** True the moment it's said. Cannot be undone, except by **फाड़ना** (see below).
4. **रेखा.** A thin ink-line appears on the inner left wrist per claim. Visible to all. The half-made read wrists the way soldiers read medals.
5. **No विरोध.** A claim cannot contradict your लिखित. Attempting one fails with a whip-crack of pain and a smell of ink — and संशोधक feel it from far away.
6. **Claimed skill = lived skill.** Claim "मैं तलवार जानता हूँ" and your hands know it. The memory of learning arrives too — but it feels like *a book you read*, not a life you lived. ("किताब की तरह याद। ज़िंदगी की तरह नहीं।")
7. **Gaps are finite.** Nobody knows their count. Fewer gaps = more **भार** (weight/authority): the Blank slows near you, you can't be shoved, your word holds in your own draft, Proofreaders' strike-through takes longer. More gaps = lighter, weaker, freer: you can still become anything.
8. **पूर्ण = zero gaps.** Cannot claim. Cannot change. Cannot be erased or eaten. Cannot stay: the page turns and the finished one is pulled out of the Drift. Nobody knows where. (Answer, Arc 6: real.)
9. **भार in a foreign draft.** Written natives assert their लिखित inside their own draft ("मैं हेमगढ़ की राजकुमारी हूँ" → gates open). Outside, only partially.

## Advanced / forbidden
- **फाड़ना (tearing).** Un-writing a written fact about yourself. Agony. Creates a new gap but the torn place bleeds white; the Blank is drawn to the one who tore. Purna's technique. Each tear cracks something in her home draft.
- **नाम देना (naming).** Giving a name to the nameless is NOT a claim; it is a gift. If accepted aloud, it holds. Loophole used for Katha (Arc 3) and others.
- **सम्मति-दावा (consent-claim).** A claim that touches another person is possible only if the other consents aloud in the same breath — and both pay a gap. Discovered Arc 5. "मैं उससे प्रेम करता हूँ।" / "मैं यह स्वीकार करती हूँ।"
- **रिक्त-भक्षण (gap-eating).** अंत's monstrosity. A finished being devours another's gaps, gaining temporary openness; the victim loses future and goes brittle, then crumbles like dry paper.
