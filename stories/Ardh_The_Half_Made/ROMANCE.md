# ROMANCE.md (Canonical — the spine)

## Principles
1. **One endgame: पूर्णा.** Every other love is real, costs something, and is honored — but the story is not coy about where it lands by Arc 5.
2. **Each heroine's love answers the central question differently.**
   - लाली: "stay unfinished, come back unfinished" (freedom as home).
   - ज़ोया: "take what's in front of you now" (the present over the ending).
   - अवनि: "duty before desire" (meaning over want).
   - मिष्टी: "one real feeling is worth a world" (feeling over rule).
   - कथा: "love as *reading* someone without correcting them" (acceptance).
   - तारा: "love that gives away" (maker's love).
   - पूर्णा: "love nobody wrote" (the original act).
3. **Ardh is not oblivious.** He notices, he's awkward, he's honest, and he hurts people by choosing. The story shows the hurt and never makes a heroine small to make the choice easy.
4. **Rivalry with dignity.** Zoya vs Purna sparks (Arc 2) are allowed: sharp, funny, adult. No slapstick. By Arc 5 they are allies who love the same boy and say so.
5. **Cadence.** A ♥ beat at least every 3 chapters. Alternate: tenderness / tension / humor / ache. Romantic chapter-end hooks allowed.
6. **Physicality.** Kisses are earned and few. First kiss of the saga: Zoya (ch ~55, she takes it). Purna: ch ~98 (after the Proofreader strike, on the ridge, in the rain that isn't hers). Keep intimacy PG-13: implied, never explicit.

## Heroine arcs in one line each
- **लाली**: friend → wants more → cannot → stays and holds → written whole.
- **ज़ोया**: suspect → ally → first kiss → lets go → returns → makes the betrayal hers → finished.
- **अवनि**: commander → protector → unspoken → spoken → chooses soldiers → lives.
- **मिष्टी**: smile → wants one feeling → gets it → loses her world → remembered by everyone who left.
- **कथा**: eraser → named → defects → learns feelings → learns the Reader → the first reader.
- **तारा**: maker → refuser → reader → writer → giver → begins again.
- **पूर्णा**: waiting-girl → tearer → traveler → rival → lover → the unwritten love → open forever.

## Things that must never happen
- Ardh does not kiss two heroines in the same arc.
- No heroine is punished by the narrative for loving him.
- Nobody "wins" by being the last one standing. Purna is chosen while Zoya is alive (ch 160–171), not by default after ch 166.
- Tara and Ardh: never romantic in the adult sense.
