# CHARACTERS.md (Canonical)

## अर्ध (Ardh) — नायक
- Looks ~18. Face: unwritten — blurred in water/mirrors, but people *see* him fine face-to-face (they fill it in; everyone sees a slightly different boy; this is noticed in Arc 2). Grey wool scarf with one crooked yellow star — never removes it.
- लिखित: brave; scarf; lives in सुनहरी; "will come to save me"; friend of लाली and मोती. That's all.
- Personality: stubborn, warm, straight-talking, bad at lying, laughs easily, angry at unfairness. Not clever by nature — becomes clever by listening. Honest in love even when it costs.
- Core want: to be finished (to be real, to deserve the promise). Core fear: that he is nobody's, that the voice stopped because he wasn't worth finishing.
- Arc: wants to be finished → learns what finishing costs → chooses to stay open and let the *story* be finished instead.
- Signature habits: touches the star on the scarf when deciding; counts the lines on his wrist at night; says "ठीक है" before doing something stupid and brave.
- Claim ledger lives in CONTINUITY_LOG.md.

## तारा (Tara) — the maker
- At 12: parents fighting; summers at नानी's village; sat in the mustard field and imagined "a boy who'll come to save me". Named him "अर—" and never finished the name (her नानी called her for dinner; she never came back to the page; नाना died that winter; she stopped visiting).
- At 34: senior editor at a publishing house in a metro city. Finishes other people's books all day. Unmarried, tired, sharp, kind under the fatigue. Keeps the old notebook in a shoebox. Hasn't imagined anything of her own in 20 years.
- Her love for Ardh is a maker's love and a 12-year-old's first love wearing a 34-year-old's face. Not sexual; the story treats it as the deepest love and the one that must *give him away*.
- Arc 6: refusal → reading → the नाना reveal → writing his face → writing "और वे कभी पूरे नहीं हुए।" → writing again.

## HEROINES (each has her own goal; see ROMANCE.md)

### लाली (Laali) — सुनहरी
- Written by Tara in one line: "उसका दोस्त लाली है।" Red ribbon in her hair (Tara's own). Face: half-sharp (Tara drew her once). ~16.
- Pinned: "वह गाँव में रहती है।" Cannot leave.
- Goal: to be more than "friend" — a word she can't claim without विरोध. Then: to hold the village together when he leaves (ties her ribbon to the last mustard tree; the Blank slows there).
- Love: first tenderness. Honest, unjealous, devastating. "अधूरा ही लौट आना। पूरे लोग लौटते नहीं।"
- Ending: Tara writes her whole (ch 207): a life, a face, a morning. She gets to leave the village.

### पूर्णा (Purna) — हेमगढ़ (ENDGAME)
- Written by Tara's नाना with love and pages of detail: princess of हेमगढ़, waits at the tower window for the hero, kind, beautiful (dark eyes, a small scar on the chin from a childhood fall he wrote in), sings badly, loves maps. ~19.
- Written: "वह नायक से विवाह करेगी।" — she TORE that line (फाड़ना). Her tower cracked. She fled her draft and has traveled the Drift for years (Drift-years), gathering rules. She has torn two more pins since. Almost no gaps left; the torn places bleed white — the Blank follows her.
- Goal: to never be anyone's ending. Then: to save her kingdom from the long winter without going back to the window. Then: to love him *without* it being written.
- Personality: dry wit, maps and plans, hides the waiting-girl inside, hates being rescued, can't stop herself from rescuing. Sharp as glass. Sings off-key when she thinks nobody's listening.
- Her power: huge भार (pages of लिखित) — inside हेमगढ़ her word moves armies; outside, she's still heavier than anyone.
- Love: the one thing neither author wrote. Slow, fighting, inevitable. First kiss ch ~98. Consent-claim ch ~171. Endgame.

### ज़ोया (Zoya) — अधजला शहर
- Written by a 23-year-old aspiring writer for three chapters: courier/fixer, motorbike, jaw like a dare, a burn scar on the left forearm (described), smokes, swears, loyal to a bar called चौथा गियर. ~22.
- The author's margin note floats in alleys: "ज़ोया बाद में धोखा देगी।" Everyone in the city can read it. She lives with a betrayal she never committed; nobody trusts her; she trusts nobody.
- Goal: to prove the margin wrong. Then (Arc 5): to make the margin *true on her terms*.
- Personality: fierce, physical, funny, honest to the point of injury. Loves fast and says so.
- Love: the first kiss of the story is hers (ch ~55) — she takes it; he's wrecked. She loses him to the road and knows it. Returns in Arc 5 and claims her last gap: "मैंने धोखा दिया। उसे नहीं — *उन्हें*।" (betrays the Proofreaders, holding the margin open). Finished. The page turns on her. One of the two great receipts.

### अवनि (Avani) — अनंत रण
- Written by a retired soldier (60s, died mid-chapter): the finest commander on the ridge, 26, scar through the left eyebrow, drinks tea black, reads her soldiers' unsent letters so someone has. Forty thousand under her. The enemy was never written; her army faces the Blank every dawn.
- Goal: a battle that means something. Then: to bring her people through.
- Personality: stern, elder-sister, dry, protective to the bone. Says "बैठो" like an order and means "मैं यहाँ हूँ".
- Love: slow-burn, never spoken until dawn of ch ~102. She lets him go. Arc 5: her army marches out of its draft (he gives them their enemy: the Blank at हेमगढ़'s walls). She lives. She chooses her soldiers. "मैं तुम्हें चुनती, अगर मैं कोई और होती।"

### मिष्टी (Mishti) — मुस्कान नगर
- Drawn by a studio for a kids'-show pitch: the human co-host beside the mascot, pigtails, polka dots, "always happy". ~17 drawn-age.
- The rule rotted: her smile is fixed. She has never cried. She wants to, once.
- Goal: one real feeling. Then: to get the children of the town (drawn, nameless) out before the correction.
- Personality: sweet, brave in a way that looks like silliness, more observant than anyone credits.
- Love: innocent, loud, uncomplicated. She is the one who says "मुझे तुम पसंद हो" in front of everyone. Tragic: her first real tear comes when he leaves (ch ~138), which breaks her rule, which brings बंटी. Her draft closes. She is the arc's wound.

### कथा (Katha) — संशोधक सत्रह (17)
- A Proofreader: grey coat, numbered, a thin red line across the eyes, no name. Observes Ardh from ch 9. Erases contradictions.
- Her one gap: the Reader never wrote *why* she serves. Ardh names her कथा (ch ~86); she accepts; the name holds (नाम देना loophole). She defects ch ~90.
- Goal: to understand what she is. Then: to read instead of erase.
- Personality: precise, literal, learns feelings like a foreign language and reports them ("मेरे पेट में कुछ है। यह डर है?"). Dry accidental humor. Fierce once she chooses.
- Love: forbidden, quiet, never fully returned; she knows and stays anyway. She is the one who knows what the Reader is. Ending: the first reader; stays in the Drift; narrates the final chapter.

## ANTAGONISTS

### अंत (Ant) — true name कैवल्य
- The villain of a *published, finished* novel ("कैवल्य", a famous dark epic by a living novelist, **प्रभाकर सेन**, 71). Finished → real → but a finished villain just sits in the book, dying the same death on page 611 forever.
- He clawed back into the Drift through his book's deleted scenes. A finished being among the half-made. Cannot claim (no gaps). So he eats gaps (रिक्त-भक्षण): victims go brittle and crumble like dry paper; he gains temporary openness and can change for a while.
- Goal: reach the real world, kill/un-write his author so the book is unfinished and he becomes free.
- Ardh's mirror: finished and desperate to be open vs open and desperate to be finished. He *likes* Ardh. He offers him the trade more than once.
- Look: tall, beautiful, perfectly described — too sharp to look at for long; eyes that have read their own ending. Speaks gently. Carries page 611 folded in his coat.
- Fate: hears Prabhakar say "मैंने तुझमें सबसे ज़्यादा ख़ुद को रखा था" and chooses his written death (ch ~203).

### बंटी-मास्कॉट — मुस्कान नगर
- Round, soft, fur the color of a bruise, a stitched smile. Corrects anyone who stops smiling. Not evil: a rule with a body. Unkillable in his draft; survived only by leaving.

### संशोधक (Proofreaders)
- Numbered. Grey. Red line across the eyes. Feel every विरोध. Strike-through = erasure. Cannot touch written text. Cannot enter the real world. Chief voice: **संशोधक एक (One)** — speaks only in corrections.

## SUPPORTING

- **दादा** — सुनहरी. Tara's नाना as she drew him at 12 (she loved him: he has the most detail in the village — white moustache, a cough, cardamom in his pocket). Knows the rules because he's nearly finished. Dies to the Blank ch 24; his last line is a rule.
- **मोती** — the dog. Written COMPLETELY by a 12-year-old ("मोती हर जगह अर के साथ जाता है, काला है, एक कान सफ़ेद है, सबको चाटता है, कभी नहीं मरता"). The most finished being in the village — and "कभी नहीं मरता" means he cannot die. Can go anywhere. Comic relief and the saga's quiet miracle. Survives everything. Ends with Tara, in the real world (ch 209).
- **कुम्हार** — village potter with no hands; first to be eaten by the Blank (ch 4).
- **रिंकू** — अधजला, 14, drawn as "the kid at the bar who knows everything". Zoya's shadow. Ardh's information. Survives; sent to हेमगढ़ with Purna's people in Arc 5.
- **हरिया** — अनंत रण, Avani's deputy, 40s, tea and jokes, carries letters.
- **सूरज** — अनंत रण, a boy-soldier (16) written to die in the first charge that never came. Ardh's friend. Dies in Arc 5's march — the first charge finally happens.
- **बूढ़ा चित्रकार** — मुस्कान नगर, the one drawn background artist who stopped smiling by hiding his face behind his easel for years. Teaches Ardh how the draft's rule works.
- **प्रभाकर सेन** — real world, 71, celebrated novelist, author of "कैवल्य". Proud, lonely, loved his villain most.
- **नाना** (Tara's grandfather) — appears only in first-page glimpses and the notebook's back pages. Wrote हेमगढ़. Died the winter Tara was 12.
