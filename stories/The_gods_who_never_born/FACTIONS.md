# FACTIONS.md (Canonical)

## सूर्यवज्र वाहिनी (disbanded)
- Shaurya's legendary legion; dissolved overnight 20 years ago, records burned.
- Surviving members scattered under false names. Some still loyal to "वज्रकेतु". Some hunt him.
- Secret: they witnessed an erasure-event. Most have forgotten WHAT they guarded. It itches.

## सोमवंश (erased)
- Devyani's bloodline of चंद्रशिला. Not destroyed — ERASED. History has no gap where they were; that's the horror.
- Only Devyani remembers. Her proof: स्मृतिसूत्र hair-pin.

## नाग राजवंश / फणरक्षक
- Isha's people. Keepers of the record: "जब शून्यहृदय जागेगा, नागवंश फिर लहूलुहान होगा।"
- Internal split (later): those who obey the record vs. those who ask who WROTE it.

## कालवक्त्र का पंथ — "मौनव्रती"
- Silent order serving कालवक्त्र across centuries. They do not recruit; they *inherit* members from... somewhere.
- Mission (as they believe it): prevent the birth of the God. They think they are saving the world. Maybe they are.

## सप्त सिंहासन (the seven courts)
- Politics layer: सूर्यवर्त vs वज्रकूट rivalry; चंद्रशिला neutrality-as-weapon; निशाकर information-brokers; तमोवर्त officially nonexistent, actually everywhere.

## गुप्त सूत्र (unknown sender)
