# MONSTERS.md (Canonical)

Hierarchy: ग्राम्य (village-level) → वन्य (wild) → अभिशप्त (cursed) → प्राचीन (ancient) → युगांत (world-boss).

## अभिशप्त — story-active early
1. **स्मृति-भक्षक (शिशु रूप)** — lesser spawn of the स्मृति-राक्षस line. Appears in रक्तवन (ch 3–4).
   - Form: too-tall, backwards-jointed, skin like wet ash; NO face — its face is a smooth surface where forgotten faces surface and sink.
   - It speaks in STOLEN VOICES (voices of its victims' loved ones).
   - Feeding: eats memories first, flesh after. Around it, victims forget faces of those they love.
   - Weakness: a memory held with absolute intensity burns its "skin". Killed by Shaurya in one strike (ch 4).
   - Dying whisper (ch 4): "वज्रकेतु… इस बार भी उसी की रक्षा…?" — plant, do not explain.
2. **रक्तवन के वृक्ष-मुख** — trees with sleeping faces grown into bark; they murmur names of the erased at dusk. Not hostile. Worse: they *remember*.

## प्राचीन (mid-game)
3. **नरक-वृक्ष** — grows from dead souls; its fruit are last words.
4. **काल-सर्प** — moves through time; scar today, bite tomorrow.
5. **आकाश-भक्षी** — eats stars; night grows darker each century (nobody has noticed. Swarika has.)

## युगांत (world bosses, every 100–150 ch)
6. **स्मृति-राक्षस (पूर्ण रूप)** — the parent of #1. Arc 14 (महाविस्मृति) boss.
7. **शून्य देव** — god with no identity. It and Atharva's शून्यहृदय resonate. (Late.)
8. **अंधकार समुद्र** — living ocean beneath Aryavarta; the drowned civilizations are its dreams.

## Rules
- Monsters are horror-first, stat-second. Every ancient monster is a clue about previous cycles.
- No monster exists "for XP". Each appearance changes someone or reveals something.
