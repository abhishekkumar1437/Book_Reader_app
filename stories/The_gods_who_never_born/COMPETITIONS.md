# COMPETITIONS.md (Canonical)

Rule: NOT all martial tournaments. Psychologically dangerous, philosophy-flavored per Samrajya. Atharva must NOT win them all: sometimes lose, sometimes win by intellect, sometimes the prize creates a new problem.

1. **सूर्य महारण** (सूर्यवर्त) — 1000-warrior pure-combat tournament. Arc 6.
2. **नवफण परीक्षा** (नागलोक) — survive seven days while hunted. Arc 8.
3. **स्वप्नयज्ञ** (चंद्रशिला) — fight without your physical body, in dreams. Arc 9.
4. **स्मृति परीक्षा** (चंद्रशिला) — forget something precious to advance. (The cruelest; ties to M7.)
5. **वज्र रण** (वज्रकूट) — your weapon judges whether you're worthy. Arc 12.
6. **अग्निकुंड परीक्षा** (अग्निरथ) — forge your own weapon inside a living furnace. Arc 10.
7. **नील निमज्जन** (सागरमंडल) — dive the drowned ruins; horror arc. Arc 11.
8. **मौन परीक्षा** (निशाकर) — political game of secrets; assassination-survival variant.
9. **रात्रि खेल** (तमोवर्त) — murderer's game; everyone has a secret identity. Arc 13.
10. **स्वर-परीक्षा** (स्वरवन) — compete through music/dream-forms.
11. **अंतिम प्रतियोगिता — God's Game** — rules untold; the winner writes the rules. Endgame.

Prize acquisition modes to rotate: win / earn / discover / steal / bargain / defeat-owner / trial / sacrifice / outsmart.
