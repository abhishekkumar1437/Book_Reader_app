# ARTIFACTS.md (Canonical)

RULES: every legendary artifact has history, previous owners, a CONDITION, often a personality, hidden abilities, cost/curse, and a tie to ancient events. Artifacts are choices, not power-ups. Not all are combat items. (Target: 30+; grows as the story does.)

## Tier-0 (myth)
1. **कालचक्र** — the ultimate artifact. Real purpose unknown. The circular symbol on the black stone under the shop matches it. ("पुनः।")
2. **अमृतकुंभ** — can restore life... another person must die. (Endgame tragedy device.)

## Tier-1 (legendary, condition-bound)
3. **कालाग्नि** (sword) — cuts anything; REFUSES to cut anyone the wielder truly loves. (Late: the blade once refused to cut ईशा — proof of love before he knows it himself.)
4. **मृतदर्पण** (mirror) — shows the truth; each gaze makes one more person forget the gazer.
5. **नागमणि** — grants immense power; slowly siphons the user's memories into the Naga ancestral pool.
6. **अमर दीप** (lamp) — resurrects one person; another must die. Sister-object of अमृतकुंभ; one of them is a fake. Which?
7. **वज्रकेतु** (sword) — Shaurya's sealed blade under the shop; nobody can lift it. It is *waiting* — not for strength, for a specific memory.
8. **सूर्यास्त्र** — solar astra of सूर्यवर्त's royal line.
9. **कालदीप** — lamp tied to the flow of time; its flame burns backward.
10. **शून्यमुद्रा** — seal that suppresses spiritual laws; one of the few things that can calm शून्यहृदय overload.

## Tier-2 (major, story-active early/mid)
11. **स्मृतिसूत्र** — thread that ties one memory so it can never be erased. Single use. (Devyani owns one. She has never used it. Why not?)
12. **रक्तवन-शूल** — thorn-dagger from the forest; wounds it makes are forgotten by the victim's body (they don't heal — they're *forgotten*).
13. **मौनघंटा** — bell whose silence, not sound, spreads; erases sound-based mantras.
14. **छायादीप** — lamp that casts shadows of things not present.
15. **प्राणकोष** — stores another's donated prana; a cruel gift for a शून्य boy... or a lifeline.
16–30. To be introduced arc-by-arc (forging arc, drowned-city arc, Naga arc, dream arc, तमोवर्त arc). Update THIS file each time.
