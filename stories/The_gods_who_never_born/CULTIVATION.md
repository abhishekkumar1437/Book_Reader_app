# CULTIVATION.md — साधना प्रणाली (Canonical)

Original system built on Indian concepts (प्राण, नाड़ी, चक्र, तत्त्व, आत्मा, ऋषि-परंपरा). NOT a Chinese-style qi ladder.

## THE NINE REALMS (नौ अवस्थाएँ)

| # | Realm | Essence | Typical age | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | **प्राणजागरण** | prana awakens; faint glow in palms | 8–12 | none |
| II | **नाड़ीशोधन** | channels open/purify | 12–16 | burst nadis (crippling) |
| III | **चक्रदीप्ति** | inner centers ignite | 15–20 | one chakra can "eclipse" the others (obsession/madness) |
| IV | **तत्त्वसिद्धि** | master ONE fundamental tattva | 18–30 | tattva rejection = body breakdown |
| V | **आत्मदीप** | the soul becomes a lamp; senses souls/lies | 25–40 | soul-flame can attract स्मृति-राक्षस |
| VI | **ऋषि अवस्था** | perceive spiritual laws | 35+ | "law-deafness" — most plateau here forever |
| VII | **महर्षि अवस्था** | bend natural laws locally | rare | each bending costs lifespan |
| VIII | **देव अवस्था** | transcend ordinary mortality (NOT immortal) | legendary | the महाविस्मृति begins noticing you |
| IX | **परमदेव** | true divine existence | mythical | unknown. No confirmed case. (Or 107 failed cases…) |

Breakthrough requires: realm-specific trial + insight + often a cost. Famous cultivators per realm listed as introduced (update here).

## लक्ष्य-नियम (power balance)
- Realm ≠ victory. Strategy, preparation, environment, artifacts, mantras, teamwork, psychology, sacrifice can defeat higher realms.
- Cultivation does NOT extend lifespan except slightly at V+ (years, not centuries).

## अथर्व की विशेष स्थिति — शून्यहृदय
- Core reads शून्य: no prana, silent nadis, dormant chakras.
- Truth: his heart doesn't hold energy — it touches **नियम (laws)** directly.
- Cost: forcing it consumes LIFESPAN. No refunds.
- Hidden 2nd track — **स्मृति-साधना (Memory Cultivation)**: memory → identity → existence.
  - Normal path strengthens body → energy → soul.
  - Atharva's path strengthens memory → identity → existence.
  - Being strongly remembered = harder to erase. This is why he gathers true friends across the Samrajyas.
  - Discovered gradually from Arc 9 (स्वप्नयज्ञ). DO NOT name it earlier.

## विस्मृति-नियम
The more power a being holds, the more reality "notices" and begins erasing them (face → name → deeds → bonds → existence). Realm VIII+ beings are actively hunted by the Forgetting. This is why the world has no living gods. (Full mechanics in MYSTERIES.md M1.)
