# MANTRAS.md (Canonical)

Categories: आक्रमण, रक्षा, चिकित्सा, माया, बंधन, आवाहन, गोपन, स्मृति, आत्मा, काल, मृत्यु, सृष्टि.
Rule: strongest mantras demand conditions + costs (lifespan, memories, silence-vows, love). Target 30+; grows arc-by-arc.

## स्मृति-वर्ग (Memory) — this story's signature school
1. **स्मृतिपाठ** — read memories inside objects (Devyani's specialty). Cost: reader loses a small memory of her own each deep reading.
2. **स्मृतिबंध** — bind a memory so it cannot be erased. Higher forms need blood of one who remembers you truly.
3. **विस्मरण** — FORBIDDEN. Erase a memory from the world. Users unknowingly feed महाविस्मृति.
4. **स्मृति-आवाहन** — call the last memory of the dead. (The floating inscriptions imitate this... or are they this?)

## आक्रमण
5. **सूर्यवज्र** — legion-mantra of Shaurya's old vahini; a spear of dawn-light. Cost: user cannot lie for a day after casting (light rejects falsehood).
6. **अग्निशर** — basic fire-arrow; village-guard level.

## रक्षा / बंधन / गोपन
7. **कवचमंत्र** — standard prana-armor.
8. **नागपाश** — serpent-binding; Naga royalty only.
9. **छायागोपन** — hide within shadows. In Atharva's case it behaves... strangely. (Do not explain.)

## माया / स्वप्न
10. **स्वप्नद्वार** — enter shared dreams (चंद्रशिला). Cost: the dreamer may be followed back.
11. **मायाजाल** — layered illusion.

## आत्मा / काल / मृत्यु / सृष्टि (forbidden ladder — introduce mid/late)
12. **आत्मदीपन** — burn soul for power. Cost: years of lifespan.
13. **कालस्तंभ** — freeze one breath of time. Cost: one month of life per second.
14. **मृत्युनाम** — speak a true-name to wound existence itself. Cost/condition sealed (late-game).
15. **सृष्टिबीज** — creation-seed; myth; tied to आदिदेव.
