## CORE PREMISE

A boy named **अथर्व** is born with zero spiritual power in a forgotten town.
His parents run a tiny weapon shop — but were once legendary warriors.
His shadow sometimes moves on its own.
Reality is slowly erasing powerful people from existence (महाविस्मृति).
Atharva is the **108th cycle** — the 108th version of a being reality keeps recreating every 1,000 years to become God. The previous 107 all failed and left one message:

> "हम देव बनने में असफल रहे। इस बार देव मत बनना।"
> "क्यों?"
> "क्योंकि इस बार… भगवान बनने के बाद तुम्हें हमें मारना पड़ेगा।"

**DO NOT reveal the cycle truth before ~Arc 15 (chapter ~280+).**
For the first 200–300 chapters the reader must believe this is a traditional cultivation adventure. It is actually a mystery disguised as a cultivation story.

## THE FIVE PILLARS (what makes this story unique)

1. **महाविस्मृति (The Great Forgetting)** — reality erases the too-powerful: face → name → deeds → relationships → existence. Atharva alone resists and remembers the erased.
2. **स्मृति-साधना (Memory Cultivation)** — memories ARE spiritual power. Being remembered anchors you to reality. Atharva builds friendships across the seven Samrajyas because *he needs people who remember him*.
3. **The 100-Year Clock** — cultivation does NOT grant immortality. Atharva's lifespan is ~100 years; forbidden techniques and the शून्यहृदय consume it. Track it every arc. Never casually refund lifespan.
4. **The Shadow** — moves independently, warns, fights, eventually speaks. Identity hidden for hundreds of chapters.
5. **Contradictory Histories** — every Samrajya remembers a different First God (आदिदेव)... because each remembers a *different previous Atharva*.

## HERO'S CONDITION: शून्यहृदय (The Empty Heart)

- Atharva's spiritual core reads as शून्य (zero). It is not empty — it interacts with LAWS themselves.
- Cost: every time it is forced past its limit, it consumes lifespan (months → years → decades).
- The stronger he becomes, the faster he approaches death. TIME IS HIS GREATEST ENEMY.

## The "notification" mechanic (NOT a system)

Occasionally ancient Sanskrit-like inscriptions appear in the air, visible only to Atharva:

```
[स्मृति प्राप्त]
मृत व्यक्ति की अंतिम स्मृति प्राप्त हुई।
```

TWIST (hidden till late): these are not a system. **Someone is sending them.** Eventually:
```
[संदेश प्राप्त]
"भागो। मैं तुम्हें खोज चुका हूँ।"
```

## ROMANCE RULE (dangerous love)

Whenever Atharva and Isha fall deeper in love, one shared memory of each other disappears.
- She forgets their first meeting.
- He forgets their first kiss.
- She forgets WHY she loves him — but still feels it when she sees him.
Isha may have loved a previous-cycle Atharva. She doesn't remember. Yet.

Not "can he become the strongest" — but:
**"अगर देव बनने का अर्थ है वह सब खोना जो उसे मनुष्य बनाता है — तो क्या उसे देव बनना चाहिए?"**
Free will over destiny. Memory over oblivion. Love over eternity. Humanity over absolute power.
