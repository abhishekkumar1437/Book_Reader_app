# CHARACTERS.md (Canonical)

## अथर्व (Atharva) — नायक
- Age at start: 6. Natural lifespan: 100 (see CONTINUITY_LOG for live count).
- Spiritual power: शून्य → later revealed as शून्यहृदय (interacts with laws; consumes lifespan when forced).
- Goal ladder: माता-पिता की रक्षा → साथियों की रक्षा → अपनी पहचान → भूला हुआ अतीत → महाविनाश रोकना → देव बनने या न बनने का निर्णय।
- Fear: बेकार/शून्य होना; बाद में — भुला दिया जाना।
- Personality: जिद्दी, जिज्ञासु, भावुक, रक्षक-प्रवृत्ति, अपमान से जलता है पर टूटता नहीं। "अभी कमजोर हूँ। हमेशा नहीं।"
- Secret (unknown to him): he is the 108th cycle; his shadow is not a shadow; the erased remember him.
- Arc: मासूम बच्चा → योद्धा → नेता → संदेह → त्याग → बुद्धत्व-जैसा निर्णय।

## शौर्यवर्धन — पिता ("शौर्य काका")
- Public: मृदुलपुर का लोहार/शस्त्र-व्यापारी।
- Real: सूर्यवज्र वाहिनी का पूर्व सेनापति; युद्ध-उपाधि **वज्रकेतु**; अपने युग के सबसे घातक योद्धाओं में एक।
- Weapon: a sealed sword beneath the shop nobody can lift (his old blade, वज्रकेतु).
- Why he vanished (SECRET, reveal in Arc 4+): a mission involving the previous "forgetting wave"; he saw something erased that he alone half-remembers — because Devyani anchored his memory.
- Strength: one-strike lethality, battlefield mind. Weakness: guilt; he believes his past will one day come for his son.
- Rule as teacher: "शक्ति से पहले नींव। नींव से पहले इच्छा।"

## देवयानी — माता
- Public: गृहिणी, दुकान में हाथ बँटाती है।
- Real: प्राचीन सोमवंश की अंतिम उत्तराधिकारी; स्मृति-मंत्र की असाधारण साधिका — वस्तुओं में बसी स्मृतियाँ पढ़ती है।
- She knows MORE about Atharva than his father does (she has seen "कुछ जाग रहा है" inside his emptiness). She hides it even from Shaurya.
- Weakness: her smriti-mantra slowly costs her own memories (long-term tragedy thread).

## ईश्वरी नागेश्वरी ("ईशा") — नायिका (enters wider-world arcs)
- Naga royal bloodline; elite warrior; proud, precise, lethal.
- Her clan's record: "जब शून्यहृदय जागेगा, नागवंश फिर लहूलुहान होगा।" She is ordered to kill Atharva.
- Own goals: restore Naga honor; find who erased her elder sister from history (she doesn't know it was महाविस्मृति).
- Arc: शत्रु → प्रतिद्वंद्वी → विवश सहयोगी → विश्वास → मित्रता → प्रेम — then the memory-price begins.
- NEVER a damsel. She wins fights Atharva can't.

## Final arc (ch 481–495) — closures ═ SAGA COMPLETE ═
- **अथर्व (DEATH, ch490)** — died ~75, ON the threshold of the open door, both worlds around him, mid-welcome: "आ गए? अच्छा हुआ।" One hand in ईशा's, one taken by देवयानी's. The सौ-बरस clock never needed its full wind — "इसे कहते हैं ठीक समय।" Last own-hand words (ch470) stand on the गवाही-शाला door; last title: देहरी वाला.
- **the reader → पहला (NAMED, ch489)** — by अथर्व's vaulted letter: first of all readers, eldest brother of the अनगिनत; his line is in the last page's signature; his vow: first thali to every new reader. Entered the main ledger-lines at last.
- **the आने वाले (REVEALED, ch485)** — the story's own readers/hearers of every future age, gathered beyond the turning line since ch422; they know the story and are new to being inside it; their count permanently unrecorded (मुंशी's count stopped itself at 108, ch491 — M9 kept forever)।
- **the stone page (WRITTEN, ch492)** — the actual last page in both scripts: आदिदेव was a seat, not a person; 108 attempts at a god each became a house; "वह कभी एक नहीं था" extended to god, hero, and reader alike. Signatures: "घर।" / "पहला — और उसके बाद के सब।"
- **ईशा (DEATH, ch493)** — years later, on the rooftop's bright spot; her धीमी डाक: one line for every guest, "और लेना। शर्म घर में नहीं चलती।"
- **मुंशी (DEATH, ch493)** — mid-nap in his post office; last letter addressed to all who ever waited for one: "आ लही है। थत्त। डाकिये झूठ नहीं बोलते।"
- **बाँचू → सीढ़ी (RENAMED, ch494)** — stairs completed (2652); the deepest erased climbed out ON him; second name from the last climber: सीढ़ी — "सज़ा पूरी।" New unending ledger: "उतरने वालों का लेखा" ("ब्याज अब मीठा है")। दो-बार-पुकारो keeps the 118th देहरी at the top.
- **दस्तक (SUCCESSION, ch493)** — holds the wooden first-cycle थाली ("अदले हाथ तक"); the thread the saga leaves deliberately open.
- **परछाईं (AT CLOSE)** — her line = the two worlds' children's playground; "पहले एक के साथ चलती थी। अब सबके।"
