# WORLD.md — आर्यावर्त (Canonical)

A fully fictional ancient-Indian-inspired world. NOT historical India.

## Structure
- Mortal world: **आर्यावर्त** — seven great human Samrajyas (see SAMRAJYAS.md). (Note: ocean empire renamed वरुणमंडल → **सागरमंडल** by author; सागरमंडल is canonical.)
- Beyond human society:
  - **नागलोक** — Naga civilization (underground river-kingdoms; memory-jewel culture)
  - **स्वरवन** — Gandharva realm (music = mantra)
  - **यक्षवन** — ancient forest civilization (hoarders of forgotten things)
  - **रक्तभूमि** — Rakshasa clan territories
  - Dead-civilization ruins everywhere — layers of previous cycles (readers must not know this yet).

## मृदुलपुर (starting village)
- Small town north of सूर्यवर्त, between hills and साल forests.
- East gate: अग्निधार आयुधशाला (the family shop).
- Old temple with **108 statues** (at dusk, sometimes 109 — the 109th has no face... or Atharva's face; nobody else ever notices. Clue C3).
- North boundary: **रक्तवन** — forbidden forest. Inside it, memories thin: people briefly forget faces of loved ones. Locals just call it "बुरी हवा"।

## The shop's secret
Beneath अग्निधार आयुधशाला: a sealed chamber —
ancient weapons, broken astras, bloodline records, maps, forbidden manuscripts,
a sealed black stone bearing a circular symbol (कालचक्र?) that glowed once with one word: **"पुनः।"**,
and a sword nobody can lift. Atharva does not know it exists.

## Supernatural laws of the world
1. **साधना** raises power but NOT lifespan (mostly). Death is real for everyone.
2. **महाविस्मृति** — the world can forget you (see MYSTERIES.md M1).
3. **स्मृति = शक्ति** — memories carry spiritual weight; strong remembrance anchors existence.
4. Astras and mantras have costs and conditions; the strongest demand lifespan, memories, or love.
5. Every ~1,000 years: a "युग-संधि" — histories blur, records contradict, civilizations misremember. (Cycle boundary — hidden truth.)

## World bosses (deploy every 100–150 chapters)
- आकाश-भक्षी — creature that eats stars
- स्मृति-राक्षस — eats memories (a LESSER spawn appears in ch 3–4)
- काल-सर्प — moves through time
- नरक-वृक्ष — grows from dead souls
- शून्य देव — god with no identity
- अंधकार समुद्र — living ocean beneath Aryavarta
